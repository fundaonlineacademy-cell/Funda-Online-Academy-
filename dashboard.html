📜 Policies, Payment Rules & Declaration

Please read this information carefully before enrolling in a course.

These policies explain payment rules, student responsibilities, assessment requirements, conduct requirements and protection of student information.

💳 Payment Rules

  • Courses below R2,000 must be paid in full.
  • Courses above R2,000 require at least half of the course fee upfront.
  • The remaining balance must be paid according to the agreed payment arrangement before completion/certification.
  • Where applicable, students may request a payment plan.

📚 Student Rules & Responsibilities

  • Students must remain committed to completing their chosen course.
  • Students must treat Funda Online Academy staff and other students with respect.
  • Assessments must be completed through the approved academy learning system.
  • Students must provide accurate information.
  • Students must not share their account password.
  • Students must not attempt to access another student's account or information.

🔐 Privacy & Protection of Information

Funda Online Academy will take reasonable steps to protect student information and use it for legitimate academy purposes such as administration, enrolment, communication, assessments, records and certification.

📝 Assessments & Academic Integrity

  • Students must complete their own assessments.
  • Students must not submit work completed by another person as their own.
  • Results may be withheld while an assessment concern is being reviewed.
