📜 Policies & Terms

Please read the following information carefully.

💳 Payment Policy

  • Courses costing R2,000 or less require full payment.
  • Courses costing more than R2,000 require at least 50% payment upfront.
  • The remaining balance may be paid according to an agreed payment plan.
  • Students are responsible for following their agreed payment arrangement.
  • Payment proof must be submitted through the official student platform where requested.

🎓 Student Responsibilities

  • Students must be committed to completing their course.
  • Students must treat academy staff and other students with respect.
  • Students must follow Funda Online Academy rules and instructions.
  • Students must keep their login information secure.
  • Students must provide accurate information during registration and enrolment.

📚 Course Rules

  • Course changes are not permitted after a course has started.
  • Students should carefully choose their course before completing enrolment.
  • Learning materials provided to students are for their personal educational use.
  • Students must not copy, sell or redistribute academy learning materials without permission.

📝 Assessment Rules

  • Assessments must be submitted by the required deadlines.
  • Assessments must be the student's own work.
  • Assessments may not be submitted through WhatsApp.
  • Students must use the official assessment submission system provided by the academy.
  • Academic dishonesty or fraudulent submissions may result in disciplinary action.

💰 Refunds

  • No refunds are provided for students who voluntarily drop out after starting a course, subject to any applicable legal requirements.
  • Students should contact the academy as soon as possible if they experience circumstances affecting their studies or payments.

🏆 Certificates

  • Certificates are issued after the applicable course requirements have been successfully completed.
  • Students must provide accurate personal information for their academic and certificate records.
  • Any outstanding requirements or applicable fees may need to be resolved before certificate release.

🔐 Privacy & Personal Information

  • Funda Online Academy takes reasonable steps to protect student personal information.
  • Personal information is used for legitimate registration, administration, communication, learning and certificate purposes.
  • Students should keep their account credentials confidential.
  • Students should notify the academy if their personal information changes.

💻 Platform Rules

  • Students must use the academy platform responsibly.
  • Students must not attempt to access another student's account or information.
  • Fraudulent activity, abuse of the platform or serious misconduct may result in account suspension or termination.
