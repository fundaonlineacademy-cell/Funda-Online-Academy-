Student Orientation

Know your dashboard. Use every benefit with confidence.

This orientation explains where to begin, what each area is for and how the Student Portal supports you from application approval through learning, assessment, completion and career preparation.

✓ Learn in one place ✓ Track your progress ✓ Receive official updates ✓ Get the right support

Start Here

Your first five steps

Follow this simple order whenever you return to the Academy. It will help you find the correct information quickly and continue learning without confusion.

1

Check My Dashboard

Confirm your learner details, current status and recent activity. Read the Student House Rules and permanent Academy Identity information.

2

Open My Courses

Your submitted course remains visible while it is reviewed. When access is approved, use Continue Learning to enter the course.

3

Learn in sequence

Work through the required lessons and modules in order. Use Study Materials for course-specific documents and the Digital Library for additional learning resources.

4

Complete and track

Complete every required assessment honestly and carefully. Use My Progress, My Results and My Certificates to follow your academic journey.

5

Ask for the right help

Read FAQs first, use Academic Support for learning queries and log a Support System ticket—with a screenshot when the issue is visible on your screen.

Your Benefits

What your Student Portal gives you

The portal brings your academic journey, official Academy information and support services together under your secure student account.

1
A secure learner recordYour identity, enrolments and learning activity remain connected to your own account.
2
Clear status visibilitySee whether an application is under review, requires attention or has approved learning access.
3
Direct course accessReturn to approved courses and continue from your learning area without searching through the public catalogue.
4
Progress and achievement recordsFollow course progress, published results and certificate eligibility from the appropriate tabs.
5
Official Academy informationReceive notices, important dates and service guidance through the Academy's authorised channels.
6
Academic, career and technical supportReach the correct support area and use career-readiness services when you become eligible.

Important Habits

Use the portal responsibly

  • Keep your profile and contact information accurate.
  • Never share your password or allow another person to use your student account.
  • Check Communication and My Calendar regularly for official updates and dates.
  • Complete lessons and modules in the required order.
  • Submit your own work and follow all assessment instructions.
  • When requesting help, include your Learner ID, course, module and lesson details.
  • Attach a clear screenshot to a support ticket when the problem can be seen on screen.

Navigation Guide

Know what every dashboard area is for

Each tab has one purpose. Use this guide to choose the right destination instead of searching through unrelated sections.

MAIN

  • My DashboardYour welcome, learner status, account activity, House Rules and permanent Academy Identity.
  • OrientationReturn to this guide whenever you need help understanding the portal.
  • My CoursesView submitted and approved courses and enter active learning.
  • My ProgressMonitor recorded learning progress across approved courses.
  • AssessmentsOpen the assessments made available for your enrolled course.

LEARNING

  • Study MaterialsCourse-specific textbooks, addenda and documents provided for your enrolled course.
  • Digital LibraryA separate secure resource centre for additional Academy learning materials.
  • My ResultsView assessment results after they have been recorded or published.
  • My CertificatesAccess eligible certificates after the required completion conditions are met.
  • My CalendarCheck Academy dates, consultations, deadlines and reminders.

CAREER & WORKPLACE

  • Career & Workplace SupportAccess career preparation, employment-readiness guidance and Academy-managed opportunity services available to eligible learners.

COMMUNICATION & SUPPORT

  • CommunicationRead official Academy messages, announcements and notices.
  • FAQsFind clear answers to common student questions.
  • Academic SupportIdentify the correct department and information required for a learning-related query.
  • Support SystemSubmit and track a formal technical or academic support ticket, including a screenshot where relevant.

ACCOUNT

  • My ProfileReview and maintain your personal student information.
  • Security / PasswordProtect access to your account and manage your password securely.

A Message from the Chief Executive Officer

Let purpose, discipline and integrity define your journey.

Dear Student,

Your decision to pursue education is a declaration that your present circumstances will not define the limits of your future. At Funda Online Academy, we recognise that lasting progress is built through discipline, courage and consistent effort.

Approach every lesson with seriousness, every assessment with integrity and every challenge with determination. Knowledge becomes valuable when it is understood, practised and applied. Do not measure your journey by how quickly others advance; measure it by the competence, confidence and character you develop through your own work.

There may be demanding moments, but difficulty is not a signal to withdraw. It is an invitation to grow. Remain committed, ask for support when it is needed and take full responsibility for the opportunity before you.

You are not here merely to complete a course. You are here to expand your possibilities, strengthen your professional character and prepare yourself to contribute meaningfully wherever opportunity finds you. The future you want is being shaped by the standards you uphold and the work you are willing to do today.

Move forward with purpose. Funda Online Academy is honoured to walk this journey with you.

— Chief Executive OfficerFunda Online Academy
