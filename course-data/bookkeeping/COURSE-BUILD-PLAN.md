# Professional Certificate in Bookkeeping — Authoritative Rebuild Plan

## Build approach
The repository becomes the approved source before production reconciliation. The existing live course already has 10 modules with 8 lesson records per module, so the rebuild will preserve that 10 × 8 structure while replacing short legacy content with full professional lessons and secure module assessments.

Because the live course currently has an approved enrolment and existing lesson progress, production deployment must preserve learner progress. The preferred reconciliation method is to update existing lesson records in place by module number and lesson number rather than deleting and recreating lesson IDs.

## Lesson quality standard
Each lesson should include, as appropriate:
- estimated study time
- learning outcomes
- why the lesson matters
- key terms
- structured teaching content
- South African workplace examples
- worked calculations where relevant
- practical activity
- knowledge check with feedback
- key takeaways
- visual learning guidance

Narrow lessons may be approximately 500–700 words. Deeper accounting, reconciliation, VAT, payroll or reporting lessons may be 700–1,000+ words where the topic requires it. Content must not be padded simply to meet a word count.

## Assessment standard
Each of the 10 modules will contain:

### Formative bank
- 32 MCQs
- exactly 4 traceable questions per lesson
- 15 delivered per attempt
- 70% pass
- maximum 3 attempts

### Summative bank
- 52 MCQs covering all 8 lessons
- 25 delivered per attempt
- 70% pass
- maximum 3 attempts

Answer positions must be deliberately balanced:
- formative: A/B/C/D = 8 each
- summative: A/B/C/D = 13 each

Questions must have one clearly best answer, plausible distractors, traceable lesson coverage and no predictable answer-key pattern.

## Accuracy and South African scope
- Use rand for money examples.
- Keep bookkeeping mechanics technically correct and internally consistent.
- VAT, PAYE, UIF, SARS filing and tax-compliance content must be verified against current authoritative South African sources before final approval where exact rates, thresholds, dates or statutory requirements are stated.
- Legal/tax modules teach operational awareness and recordkeeping responsibilities, not personalised tax or legal advice.
- Do not invent universal retention periods where requirements depend on record type or law.

## Production reconciliation
The live course currently has 10 modules × 8 lessons and learner progress exists. Production deployment must therefore:
1. preserve existing course and module IDs;
2. preserve lesson IDs by updating matching module_number + lesson_number records in place;
3. keep the existing enrolment and lesson-progress relationship intact;
4. archive the superseded legacy final assessment;
5. add 20 secure module assessments;
6. load 840 protected assessment-bank questions;
7. use the existing generic secure formative/summative assessment runtime;
8. perform a post-deployment learner-progress and assessment-integrity audit before completion.
