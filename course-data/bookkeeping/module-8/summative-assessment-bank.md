# Module 8 Summative Assessment Bank
## VAT, Tax Records & SARS Awareness

**Bank size:** 52 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 25 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Coverage:** All eight Module 8 lessons.

1. What is the standard South African VAT rate used in this course's current 2026 source check?  
A. 15%  
B. 14%  
C. 16%  
D. 18%  
**Answer: A**

2. What is output tax?  
A. Tax paid on payroll  
B. VAT charged on taxable supplies made by a vendor  
C. Provisional tax  
D. VAT on every bank withdrawal  
**Answer: B**

3. What is input tax?  
A. VAT that is always refundable  
B. Income tax on purchases  
C. VAT incurred that may qualify for deduction under applicable rules  
D. VAT charged to customers  
**Answer: C**

4. Which statement about zero-rated and exempt supplies is correct?  
A. Both always use 15%  
B. Both are income-tax concepts only  
C. They are interchangeable  
D. They are different VAT treatments  
**Answer: D**

5. From 1 April 2026, current SARS guidance gives the general compulsory VAT-registration threshold as taxable supplies exceeding:  
A. R2.3 million per annum  
B. R1 million per annum  
C. R120,000 per annum  
D. R5 million per annum  
**Answer: A**

6. From 1 April 2026, the current general voluntary-registration threshold highlighted by SARS is:  
A. R50,000  
B. More than R120,000 under the applicable rules  
C. R1 million  
D. R2.3 million minimum  
**Answer: B**

7. Why should VAT-registration status not be assessed from profit alone?
