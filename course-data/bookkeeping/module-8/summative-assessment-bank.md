# Module 8 Summative Assessment Bank
## VAT, Tax Records & SARS Awareness

**Bank size:** 52 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 25 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Coverage:** All eight Module 8 lessons.

1. A vendor makes a standard-rated supply in October 2026. Which VAT rate should be used before considering any special treatment?  
A. 18%  
B. 15%  
C. 14%  
D. 16%  
**Answer: B**

2. A VAT vendor charges VAT on taxable sales. What is that VAT called?  
A. VAT charged on taxable supplies made by a vendor  
B. Provisional tax  
C. Tax paid on payroll  
D. VAT on every bank withdrawal  
**Answer: A**

3. What is input tax?  
A. Income tax on purchases  
B. VAT that is always refundable  
C. VAT incurred that may qualify for deduction under applicable rules  
D. VAT charged to customers  
**Answer: C**

4. How should zero-rated and exempt supplies be understood for VAT classification?  
A. They are interchangeable  
B. Both are income-tax concepts only  
C. They are different VAT treatments  
D. Both always use 15%  
**Answer: C**

5. From 1 April 2026, current SARS guidance gives the general compulsory VAT-registration threshold as taxable supplies exceeding:

B. R120,000 per annum  
C. R1 million per annum  
D. R2.3 million per annum

A. More than R120,000 under the applicable rules
