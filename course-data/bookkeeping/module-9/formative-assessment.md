# Module 9 Formative Assessment Bank
## Payroll Administration & Payroll Records

**Bank size:** 32 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 15 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Prerequisite:** Complete all eight Module 9 lessons.

## Lesson 1 — Payroll Information and Employee Master Data
1. What is an employee master record?  
A. Core approved employee information used in payroll processing  
B. A bank reconciliation  
C. Supplier account  
D. VAT return  
**Answer: A**

2. Why should salary rates be changed only from authorised evidence?  
A. Pay rates never change  
B. Pay changes affect employee cash and payroll reporting  
C. Only banks set salaries  
D. It reduces VAT  
**Answer: B**

3. Which payroll field is highly sensitive?  
A. Office colour  
B. Meeting room  
C. Employee bank details  
D. Supplier invoice number  
**Answer: C**

4. What should opening payroll data reconcile to when migrating systems?  
A. Customer statements  
B. Bank only  
C. Asset register  
D. Approved prior payroll and year-to-date records  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 2 — Gross Pay, Ordinary Hours, Overtime and Allowances
5. What is gross pay?  
A. Total earnings before deductions  
B. Net salary after deductions  
C. Employer tax only  
D. Bank balance  
**Answer: A**

6. What should support hourly payroll?  
A. Customer invoice  
B. Approved hours/timesheet and pay rate  
C. Supplier statement  
D. VAT201  
**Answer: B**

7. Who should determine the overtime rate payroll uses?  
A. The employee alone  
B. The bookkeeper's guess  
C. Approved employment/payroll rules and authorised HR process  
D. The bank  
**Answer: C**

8. Why should unusual gross-pay movements be reviewed?  
A. To reduce all wages  
B. Because payroll never changes  
C. To calculate VAT  
D. They may reveal duplicate, missing or unsupported inputs  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 3 — Deductions, Net Pay and Payroll Control
9. What is net pay?  
A. Amount payable after applicable employee deductions  
B. Gross earnings before deductions  
C. Employer SDL only  
D. Total payroll expense  
**Answer: A**

10. Is PAYE a flat percentage for every employee?  
A. Yes  
B. No; use current SARS deduction rules/tables and payroll configuration  
C. Yes at 15%  
D. Yes at 1%  
**Answer: B**

11. What is a payroll control account?  
A. Employee bank account  
B. Customer debtor account  
C. Ledger account holding payroll liabilities until settlement  
D. Sales account  
**Answer: C**

12. What should happen with a negative net-pay result?  
A. Pay it as calculated  
B. Delete the employee  
C. Increase salary automatically  
D. Investigate deductions and inputs before payment  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 4 — PAYE, UIF and Statutory-deduction Awareness
13. Current ordinary UIF employee contribution used in the course source check is:  
A. 1% subject to applicable rules/ceiling  
B. 15%  
C. 2% employee only  
D. 5%  
**Answer: A**

14. Current ordinary UIF employer contribution used in the course source check is:  
A. 0%  
B. 1% subject to applicable rules/ceiling  
C. 15%  
D. 2% plus employee  
**Answer: B**

15. What current monthly UIF remuneration ceiling is listed in the SARS employer guide used for this course?  
A. R12,478  
B. R14,872  
C. R17,712  
D. R21,250  
**Answer: C**

16. What is EMP201?  
A. VAT return  
B. Employee payslip  
C. Customer statement  
D. Monthly Employer Declaration for applicable PAYE, SDL, UIF and related amounts  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 5 — Payroll Source Documents and Payslip Records
17. Which is a payroll source document?  
A. Approved timesheet  
B. Supplier tax invoice  
C. Customer remittance  
D. Bank reconciliation only  
**Answer: A**

18. What is a payroll register?  
A. Visitor list  
B. Report detailing payroll calculations and totals  
C. Supplier age analysis  
D. VAT schedule  
**Answer: B**

19. Does a payslip alone prove every payroll input was authorised?  
A. Yes  
B. Only for overtime  
C. No; trace to approved source records  
D. Only if signed  
**Answer: C**

20. What should happen to a late unsupported payroll input?  
A. Estimate it  
B. Process silently  
C. Ask employee to calculate it  
D. Follow the approved exception/correction process  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 6 — Payroll Reconciliation and Payment Controls
21. What should the net-pay payment schedule agree with?  
A. Approved payroll register net-pay total  
B. Gross-pay total  
C. VAT control  
D. Supplier payments  
**Answer: A**

22. Why compare active employee count to payroll?  
A. To increase headcount  
B. To detect missing, duplicate or terminated employees  
C. To calculate SDL only  
D. To set tax rates  
**Answer: B**

23. What is segregation of duties in payroll?  
A. One person controls everything  
B. Payroll is never reviewed  
C. Preparation, approval and payment release are separated where practical  
D. Employees approve themselves  
**Answer: C**

24. If a payment file differs from the approved payroll register, what should happen?  
A. Release it anyway  
B. Alter the register  
C. Post difference to expense  
D. Investigate and correct before release  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 7 — Payroll Confidentiality, Corrections and Audit Trails
25. What does need-to-know access mean?  
A. Only legitimate authorised roles receive payroll information they require  
B. Every manager sees all salaries  
C. Payroll reports are public  
D. Payslips can be shared freely  
**Answer: A**

26. What should happen if a payroll error is found before finalisation?  
A. Ignore it  
B. Correct the authorised source/input and rerun review  
C. Wait until year end  
D. Change the payslip manually  
**Answer: B**

27. What can a prior-period correction affect?  
A. Only employee name  
B. Office budget only  
C. PAYE, UIF, declarations, certificates and accounting records  
D. Customer balances  
**Answer: C**

28. What should happen after accidental payroll-data disclosure?  
A. Hide it  
B. Delete all payroll  
C. Contact everyone publicly  
D. Report promptly through the authorised privacy/security process  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 8 — Payroll-cycle Practical
29. What should happen before payroll calculation starts?  
A. Verify master data and approved period inputs  
B. Submit EMP201 first  
C. Pay employees first  
D. Delete prior payroll  
**Answer: A**

30. Why are fictional PAYE values in the practical not live tax-table examples?  
A. PAYE never changes  
B. Live PAYE depends on current SARS rules and employee/payroll circumstances  
C. PAYE equals UIF  
D. Payroll systems do not calculate PAYE  
**Answer: B**

31. What should statutory totals reconcile to?  
A. Customer balances  
B. Asset register  
C. Payroll register, ledger controls and declaration schedules  
D. Sales journal  
**Answer: C**

32. What makes a payroll cycle controlled?  
A. Calculation only  
B. Payslips only  
C. Bank payment only  
D. Accuracy, authority, reconciliation, confidentiality, payment control and audit trail  
**Answer: D**
