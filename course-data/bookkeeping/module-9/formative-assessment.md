# Module 9 Formative Assessment Bank
## Payroll Administration & Payroll Records

**Bank size:** 32 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 15 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Prerequisite:** Complete all eight Module 9 lessons.

## Lesson 1 — Payroll Information and Employee Master Data
1. What is an employee master record?  
A. Supplier account  
B. Core approved employee information used in payroll processing  
C. A bank reconciliation  
D. VAT return  
**Answer: B**

2. Why should salary rates be changed only from authorised evidence?  
A. Pay changes affect employee cash and payroll reporting  
B. Only banks set salaries  
C. It reduces VAT  
D. Pay rates never change  
**Answer: A**

3. Which payroll field is highly sensitive?  
A. Employee bank details  
B. Office colour  
C. Meeting room  
D. Supplier invoice number  
**Answer: A**

4. What should opening payroll data reconcile to when migrating systems?  
A. Asset register  
B. Customer statements  
C. Approved prior payroll and year-to-date records  
D. Bank only  
**Answer: C**

## Lesson 2 — Gross Pay, Ordinary Hours, Overtime and Allowances
5. What is gross pay?  
A. Total earnings before deductions  
B. Employer tax only  
C. Bank balance  
D. Net salary after deductions  
**Answer: A**

6. What should support hourly payroll?  
A. Customer invoice  
B. VAT201  
C. Supplier statement  
D. Approved hours/timesheet and pay rate  
**Answer: D**

7. Who should determine the overtime rate payroll uses?  
A. The employee alone  
B. Approved employment/payroll rules and authorised HR process  
C. The bookkeeper's guess  
D. The bank  
**Answer: B**

8. Why should unusual gross-pay movements be reviewed?  
A. To calculate VAT  
B. They may reveal duplicate, missing or unsupported inputs  
C. To reduce all wages  
D. Because payroll never changes  
**Answer: B**

## Lesson 3 — Deductions, Net Pay and Payroll Control
9. What is net pay?  
A. Amount payable after applicable employee deductions  
B. Gross earnings before deductions  
C. Total payroll expense  
D. Employer SDL only  
**Answer: A**

10. Is PAYE a flat percentage for every employee?  
A. Yes at 1%  
B. Yes at 15%  
C. Yes  
D. No; use current SARS deduction rules/tables and payroll configuration  
**Answer: D**

11. What is a payroll control account?  
A. Customer debtor account  
B. Employee bank account  
C. Sales account  
D. Ledger account holding payroll liabilities until settlement  
**Answer: D**

12. What should happen with a negative net-pay result?  
A. Increase salary automatically  
B. Investigate deductions and inputs before payment  
C. Pay it as calculated  
D. Delete the employee  
**Answer: B**

## Lesson 4 — PAYE, UIF and Statutory-deduction Awareness
13. Current ordinary UIF employee contribution used in the course source check is:  
A. 2% employee only  
B. 15%  
C. 1% subject to applicable rules/ceiling  
D. 5%

14. Current ordinary UIF employer contribution used in the course source check is:  
A. 15%  
B. 2% plus employee  
C. 1% subject to applicable rules/ceiling  
D. 0%  
**Answer: C**

15. What current monthly UIF remuneration ceiling is listed in the SARS employer guide used for this course?  
A. R14,872  
B. R17,712  
C. R21,250  
D. R12,478  
**Answer: B**

16. What is EMP201?  
A. VAT return  
B. Employee payslip  
C. Customer statement  
D. Monthly Employer Declaration for applicable PAYE, SDL, UIF and related amounts  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 5 — Payroll Source Documents and Payslip Records
17. Which is a payroll source document?  
A. Bank reconciliation only  
B. Approved timesheet  
C. Customer remittance  
D. Supplier tax invoice  
**Answer: B**

18. What is a payroll register?  
A. Visitor list  
B. VAT schedule  
C. Supplier age analysis  
D. Report detailing payroll calculations and totals  
**Answer: D**

19. Does a payslip alone prove every payroll input was authorised?  
A. Yes  
B. Only for overtime  
C. Only if signed  
D. No; trace to approved source records  
**Answer: D**

20. What should happen to a late unsupported payroll input?  
A. Estimate it  
B. Process silently  
C. Follow the approved exception/correction process  
D. Ask employee to calculate it  
**Answer: C**

## Lesson 6 — Payroll Reconciliation and Payment Controls
21. What should the net-pay payment schedule agree with?  
A. Approved payroll register net-pay total  
B. Supplier payments  
C. Gross-pay total  
D. VAT control  
**Answer: A**

22. Why compare active employee count to payroll?  
A. To increase headcount  
B. To set tax rates  
C. To detect missing, duplicate or terminated employees  
D. To calculate SDL only  
**Answer: C**

23. What is segregation of duties in payroll?  
A. Preparation, approval and payment release are separated where practical  
B. Employees approve themselves  
C. Payroll is never reviewed  
D. One person controls everything  
**Answer: A**

24. If a payment file differs from the approved payroll register, what should happen?  
A. Investigate and correct before release  
B. Release it anyway  
C. Post difference to expense  
D. Alter the register  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 7 — Payroll Confidentiality, Corrections and Audit Trails
25. A payroll administrator is asked to send the full salary file to a manager who only approves one department. What does need-to-know access require?  
A. Publish the payroll report to the wider management group  
B. Use a personal email account so the file is easier to open  
C. Give only the authorised information required for that manager's legitimate task  
D. Send every employee's salary because the requester is a manager  
**Answer: C**

26. What should happen if a payroll error is found before finalisation?  
A. Correct the authorised source/input and rerun review  
B. Change the payslip manually  
C. Wait until year end  
D. Ignore it  
**Answer: A**

27. What can a prior-period correction affect?  
A. Customer balances  
B. Only employee name  
C. Office budget only  
D. PAYE, UIF, declarations, certificates and accounting records  
**Answer: D**

28. What should happen after accidental payroll-data disclosure?  
A. Contact everyone publicly  
B. Delete all payroll  
C. Report promptly through the authorised privacy/security process  
D. Hide it  
**Answer: C**

## Lesson 8 — Payroll-cycle Practical
29. What should happen before payroll calculation starts?  
A. Delete prior payroll  
B. Submit EMP201 first  
C. Pay employees first  
D. Verify master data and approved period inputs  
**Answer: D**

30. Why are fictional PAYE values in the practical not live tax-table examples?  
A. PAYE equals UIF  
B. Live PAYE depends on current SARS rules and employee/payroll circumstances  
C. PAYE never changes  
D. Payroll systems do not calculate PAYE  
**Answer: B**

31. What should statutory totals reconcile to?  
A. Customer balances  
B. Payroll register, ledger controls and declaration schedules  
C. Asset register  
D. Sales journal  
**Answer: B**

32. What makes a payroll cycle controlled?  
A. Payslips only  
B. Bank payment only  
C. Accuracy, authority, reconciliation, confidentiality, payment control and audit trail  
D. Calculation only  
**Answer: C**
