# Module 6 Formative Assessment Bank
## Adjustments & Basic Financial Reporting

**Bank size:** 32 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 15 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Prerequisite:** Complete all eight Module 6 lessons.

## Lesson 1 — Why Period-end Adjustments Are Needed
1. Why are period-end adjustments prepared?  
A. To align records with the correct reporting period and values  
B. To force profit to a target  
C. To replace source documents  
D. To avoid reconciliations  
**Answer: A**

2. What is an adjusted trial balance?  
A. A bank statement  
B. Trial balance after approved adjustments  
C. A supplier schedule  
D. A payroll register  
**Answer: B**

3. Which is a common period-end adjustment?  
A. Customer greeting  
B. Bank password change  
C. Accrual  
D. Filing label  
**Answer: C**

4. Why should adjustment journals have evidence?  
A. Journals cannot be approved  
B. Evidence makes them shorter  
C. Adjustments do not affect reports  
D. They can materially change profit, assets and liabilities  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 2 — Accruals and Prepayments
5. What is an accrual?  
A. Expense incurred but not yet recorded/paid  
B. Cash received in advance  
C. Asset purchase  
D. Customer credit note  
**Answer: A**

6. What is a prepayment?  
A. Unpaid supplier invoice  
B. Payment made for a future-period benefit  
C. Bank error  
D. Bad debt  
**Answer: B**

7. R12,000 annual insurance is paid on 1 January. At 31 March, what portion remains prepaid if spread evenly?  
A. R3,000  
B. R6,000  
C. R9,000  
D. R12,000  
**Answer: C**

8. Why should accrual estimates have a support basis?  
A. To increase expenses  
B. To avoid approval  
C. To make them exact  
D. To ensure they are reasonable rather than invented  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 3 — Depreciation and Asset-record Basics
9. What does depreciation do?  
A. Allocates depreciable asset amount over useful life  
B. Creates a new cash payment every period  
C. Increases asset cost  
D. Records customer debt  
**Answer: A**

10. What is accumulated depreciation?  
A. Purchase price  
B. Total depreciation recorded to date  
C. Residual value  
D. Cash paid for repairs  
**Answer: B**

11. Equipment costs R60,000, no residual value, 5-year straight-line life. Annual depreciation?  
A. R5,000  
B. R10,000  
C. R12,000  
D. R15,000  
**Answer: C**

12. What should determine useful life and capitalisation policy?  
A. Bookkeeper preference  
B. Invoice colour  
C. Asset location only  
D. Approved accounting policy and authorised judgement  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 4 — Bad Debts, Allowances and Other Routine Adjustments
13. Is every overdue balance a bad debt?  
A. No; investigate and reconcile first  
B. Yes  
C. Yes after 30 days  
D. Yes if customer is inactive  
**Answer: A**

14. What is a specific bad-debt write-off?  
A. Customer payment  
B. Authorised removal of a specific irrecoverable receivable  
C. Supplier credit  
D. Prepayment  
**Answer: B**

15. What is an allowance for doubtful debts?  
A. A bank loan  
B. A cash discount  
C. Estimate reducing receivables for potential collection losses under policy  
D. Supplier balance  
**Answer: C**

16. Why should write-offs require authority?  
A. Debtors do not affect reports  
B. Every old balance is correct  
C. Customers approve write-offs  
D. They affect assets and profit and involve recoverability judgement  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 5 — Adjusted Trial Balance and Closing Checks
17. What should happen after adjustment journals are posted?  
A. Extract and review a fresh trial balance  
B. Delete journals  
C. Stop reconciling  
D. Reopen all prior periods  
**Answer: A**

18. Which account should normally reconcile to customer subsidiary balances?  
A. Bank Charges  
B. Debtors Control  
C. Depreciation Expense  
D. Capital  
**Answer: B**

19. Why should suspense balances be investigated?  
A. Suspense is permanent equity  
B. They are always profit  
C. They represent unresolved items that may distort reports  
D. They automatically clear  
**Answer: C**

20. Does a balanced adjusted trial balance prove every balance is correct?  
A. Yes  
B. Yes if software produced it  
C. Yes after month end  
D. No; classification, omission and valuation errors can remain  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 6 — Income Statement / Statement of Profit or Loss Basics
21. How is gross profit calculated in a trading business?  
A. Sales - Cost of Sales  
B. Assets - Liabilities  
C. Bank - Creditors  
D. Debtors - Sales  
**Answer: A**

22. Which is an operating expense?  
A. Owner capital  
B. Rent  
C. Debtors  
D. Loan principal  
**Answer: B**

23. Why can profit rise while cash falls?  
A. Profit equals cash  
B. Cash never follows sales  
C. Credit sales and payment timing can differ from profit recognition  
D. Depreciation increases bank  
**Answer: C**

24. What effect can an omitted expense accrual have?  
A. Profit understated  
B. Bank overstated automatically  
C. Debtors understated  
D. Expenses understated and profit overstated  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 7 — Balance Sheet / Statement of Financial Position Basics
25. State the accounting equation.  
A. Assets = Liabilities + Equity  
B. Sales = Expenses + Bank  
C. Debtors = Creditors + Cash  
D. Profit = Assets - Sales  
**Answer: A**

26. Equipment cost R100,000 and accumulated depreciation R30,000. Basic carrying amount?  
A. R130,000  
B. R70,000  
C. R30,000  
D. R100,000  
**Answer: B**

27. Which is a liability?  
A. Debtors  
B. Equipment  
C. Creditors  
D. Prepayment  
**Answer: C**

28. Why are missing accruals a balance-sheet concern?  
A. They increase assets  
B. They reduce Sales  
C. They affect only bank  
D. Liabilities may be understated  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 8 — Reading Basic Reports and Explaining Results
29. What is a variance?  
A. Difference between two amounts, periods or expectations  
B. A fixed asset  
C. A customer invoice  
D. A payment approval  
**Answer: A**

30. What can rising Debtors with rising Sales indicate?  
A. All customers paid faster  
B. More sales may be tied up in unpaid receivables  
C. Creditors decreased automatically  
D. Profit must be zero  
**Answer: B**

31. What is basic working capital?  
A. Total sales  
B. Bank only  
C. Current assets less current liabilities  
D. Equity plus profit  
**Answer: C**

32. What should a concise financial explanation include?  
A. Unverified guesses  
B. Only final totals  
C. Every ledger entry  
D. Verified movement, cause, exceptions and follow-up  
**Answer: D**
