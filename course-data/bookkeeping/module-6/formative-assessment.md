# Module 6 Formative Assessment Bank
## Adjustments & Basic Financial Reporting

**Bank size:** 32 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 15 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Prerequisite:** Complete all eight Module 6 lessons.

## Lesson 1 — Why Period-end Adjustments Are Needed
1. Why are period-end adjustments prepared?  
A. To replace source documents  
B. To align records with the correct reporting period and values  
C. To avoid reconciliations  
D. To force profit to a target  
**Answer: B**

2. What is an adjusted trial balance?  
A. A supplier schedule  
B. A payroll register  
C. A bank statement  
D. Trial balance after approved adjustments  
**Answer: D**

3. A June expense is invoiced only in July. Which period-end adjustment may be required for June?  
A. Accrual  
B. Customer greeting  
C. Filing label  
D. Bank password change  
**Answer: A**

4. Why should adjustment journals have evidence?  
A. Adjustments do not affect reports  
B. Evidence makes them shorter  
C. Journals cannot be approved  
D. They can materially change profit, assets and liabilities  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 2 — Accruals and Prepayments
5. What is an accrual?  
A. Asset purchase  
B. Customer credit note  
C. Cash received in advance  
D. Expense incurred but not yet recorded/paid  
**Answer: D**

6. What is a prepayment?  
A. Bad debt  
B. Unpaid supplier invoice  
C. Payment made for a future-period benefit  
D. Bank error  
**Answer: C**

7. R12,000 annual insurance is paid on 1 January. At 31 March, what portion remains prepaid if spread evenly?  
A. R3,000  
B. R6,000  
C. R9,000  
D. R12,000  
**Answer: C**

8. Why should accrual estimates have a support basis?  
A. To ensure they are reasonable rather than invented  
B. To avoid approval  
C. To make them exact  
D. To increase expenses  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 3 — Depreciation and Asset-record Basics
9. What does depreciation do?  
A. Records customer debt  
B. Allocates depreciable asset amount over useful life  
C. Increases asset cost  
D. Creates a new cash payment every period  
**Answer: B**

10. What is accumulated depreciation?  
A. Total depreciation recorded to date  
B. Cash paid for repairs  
C. Residual value  
D. Purchase price  
**Answer: A**

11. Equipment costs R60,000, no residual value, 5-year straight-line life. Annual depreciation?  
A. R10,000  
B. R15,000  
C. R12,000  
D. R5,000  
**Answer: C**

12. What should determine useful life and capitalisation policy?  
A. Bookkeeper preference  
B. Approved accounting policy and authorised judgement  
C. Asset location only  
D. Invoice colour  
**Answer: B**

## Lesson 4 — Bad Debts, Allowances and Other Routine Adjustments
13. Is every overdue balance a bad debt?  
A. Yes  
B. Yes if customer is inactive  
C. No; investigate and reconcile first  
D. Yes after 30 days  
**Answer: C**

14. What is a specific bad-debt write-off?  
A. Authorised removal of a specific irrecoverable receivable  
B. Customer payment  
C. Prepayment  
D. Supplier credit  
**Answer: A**

15. What is an allowance for doubtful debts?  
A. Supplier balance  
B. A cash discount  
C. Estimate reducing receivables for potential collection losses under policy  
D. A bank loan  
**Answer: C**

16. Why should write-offs require authority?  
A. Customers approve write-offs  
B. They affect assets and profit and involve recoverability judgement  
C. Every old balance is correct  
D. Debtors do not affect reports  
**Answer: B**

## Lesson 5 — Adjusted Trial Balance and Closing Checks
17. What should happen after adjustment journals are posted?  
A. Stop reconciling  
B. Reopen all prior periods  
C. Extract and review a fresh trial balance  
D. Delete journals  
**Answer: C**

18. Which account should normally reconcile to customer subsidiary balances?  
A. Debtors Control  
B. Depreciation Expense  
C. Bank Charges  
D. Capital  
**Answer: A**

19. Why should suspense balances be investigated?  
A. They are always profit  
B. They represent unresolved items that may distort reports  
C. Suspense is permanent equity  
D. They automatically clear  
**Answer: B**

20. Does a balanced adjusted trial balance prove every balance is correct?  
A. Yes if software produced it  
B. No; classification, omission and valuation errors can remain  
C. Yes  
D. Yes after month end  
**Answer: B**

## Lesson 6 — Income Statement / Statement of Profit or Loss Basics
21. How is gross profit calculated in a trading business?  
A. Bank - Creditors  
B. Debtors - Sales  
C. Sales - Cost of Sales  
D. Assets - Liabilities  
**Answer: C**

22. Which is an operating expense?  
A. Loan principal  
B. Debtors  
C. Owner capital  
D. Rent  
**Answer: D**

23. Why can profit rise while cash falls?  
A. Credit sales and payment timing can differ from profit recognition  
B. Depreciation increases bank  
C. Cash never follows sales  
D. Profit equals cash  
**Answer: A**

24. What effect can an omitted expense accrual have?  
A. Debtors understated  
B. Expenses understated and profit overstated  
C. Profit understated  
D. Bank overstated automatically  
**Answer: B**

## Lesson 7 — Balance Sheet / Statement of Financial Position Basics
25. State the accounting equation.  
A. Sales = Expenses + Bank  
B. Profit = Assets - Sales  
C. Debtors = Creditors + Cash  
D. Assets = Liabilities + Equity  
**Answer: D**

26. Equipment cost R100,000 and accumulated depreciation R30,000. Basic carrying amount?  
A. R70,000  
B. R130,000  
C. R100,000  
D. R30,000  
**Answer: A**

27. Which is a liability?  
A. Creditors  
B. Debtors  
C. Equipment  
D. Prepayment  
**Answer: A**

28. Why are missing accruals a balance-sheet concern?  
A. They affect only bank  
B. They reduce Sales  
C. They increase assets  
D. Liabilities may be understated  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 8 — Reading Basic Reports and Explaining Results
29. What is a variance?  
A. A customer invoice  
B. Difference between two amounts, periods or expectations  
C. A payment approval  
D. A fixed asset  
**Answer: B**

30. What can rising Debtors with rising Sales indicate?  
A. Creditors decreased automatically  
B. All customers paid faster  
C. Profit must be zero  
D. More sales may be tied up in unpaid receivables  
**Answer: D**

31. Which measure equals current assets minus current liabilities?  
A. Total sales  
B. Bank only  
C. Equity plus profit  
D. Current assets less current liabilities  
**Answer: D**

32. What should a concise financial explanation include?  
A. Every ledger entry  
B. Only final totals  
C. Verified movement, cause, exceptions and follow-up  
D. Unverified guesses  
**Answer: C**
