# Module 1 Summative Assessment Bank
## Bookkeeping Foundations

**Bank size:** 52 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 25 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Coverage:** All eight Module 1 lessons.

1. Which statement BEST describes the value of bookkeeping to management?  
A. It provides controlled financial records for decisions, reporting and follow-up  
B. It guarantees a business will make a profit  
C. It replaces management judgement  
D. It deals only with annual tax returns  
**Answer: A**

2. Which task is MOST typical of routine bookkeeping?  
A. Giving a legal opinion on a contract  
B. Recording transactions and reconciling related financial records  
C. Approving every capital investment  
D. Setting national tax policy  
**Answer: B**

3. A supplier invoice is captured twice. What is a likely consequence?  
A. Customer income is overstated  
B. Bank automatically increases  
C. The supplier liability or expense may be overstated and duplicate payment risk increases  
D. Owners' capital automatically decreases twice  
**Answer: C**

4. Why should a bookkeeper maintain source evidence?  
A. It makes reports longer  
B. It replaces double-entry  
C. It removes approval requirements  
D. It supports traceability, verification and the audit trail  
**Answer: D**

5. What should a bookkeeper do when a tax treatment is outside their competence?  
A. Preserve the evidence and refer it to the authorised specialist  
B. Guess using the previous transaction  
C. Ignore the transaction  
D. Ask the customer to decide  
**Answer: A**

6. Why is a current debtors list useful?  
A. It shows only cash expenses  
B. It helps identify amounts still owed by customers  
C. It replaces the bank statement  
D. It shows supplier balances only  
**Answer: B**

7. Which statement about financial confidentiality is correct?  
A. All internal staff may see all records  
B. Confidentiality applies only to payroll  
C. Access should be limited to legitimate authorised work needs  
D. Bank details may be sent through any convenient account  
**Answer: C**

8. What is the BEST description of an audit trail?  
A. A final profit figure  
B. A spreadsheet password  
C. A customer list  
D. Traceable evidence of transactions, changes, approvals and records  
**Answer: D**

9. Which equation must remain balanced?  
A. Assets = Liabilities + Owners' Equity  
B. Income = Cash + Expenses  
C. Equity = Expenses + Liabilities  
D. Bank = Sales + Debtors  
**Answer: A**

10. Which account type normally increases with a debit?  
A. Income  
B. Asset  
C. Liability  
D. Owners' equity  
**Answer: B**

11. Which account type normally increases with a credit?  
A. Expense  
B. Asset  
C. Income  
D. Drawings  
**Answer: C**

12. A business buys office equipment for R10,000 cash. What is the core double-entry effect?  
A. Bank increases and income increases  
B. Expense increases and creditors increase  
C. Equipment decreases and bank increases  
D. Equipment increases and bank decreases  
**Answer: D**

13. A business purchases goods for R6,000 on credit. Which basic effect is correct?  
A. An asset/purchase-related account increases and creditors increase  
B. Bank increases and income increases  
C. Creditors decrease and bank decreases  
D. Drawings increase and bank decreases  
**Answer: A**

14. Why is receiving payment from a debtor not new sales income?  
A. Debtors are liabilities  
B. The sale was recognised earlier; the receipt settles the amount owed  
C. Bank receipts are never recorded  
D. Customers cannot pay by EFT  
**Answer: B**

15. What does double-entry mean?  
A. Every transaction is entered twice in the same account  
B. Two bookkeepers must capture every transaction  
C. Every transaction has equal debit and credit effects  
D. Only cash transactions use two accounts  
**Answer: C**

16. A bank loan is received. Which statement is correct?  
A. It is automatically sales income  
B. It is automatically owner capital  
C. It should never be recorded until repayment  
D. Bank increases and a loan liability increases  
**Answer: D**

17. What are drawings in a sole proprietorship?  
A. Personal value withdrawn by the owner from the business  
B. Wages paid to all employees  
C. Supplier discounts  
D. Customer refunds  
**Answer: A**

18. Why should a shareholder transfer into a company not automatically be recorded as sales?  
A. Shareholders cannot transfer money  
B. The amount may represent capital, a loan or another transaction requiring clarification  
C. All company receipts are liabilities  
D. Sales can only be cash  
**Answer: B**

19. Which transaction is clearly a business expense if properly supported?  
A. Owner's private grocery purchase  
B. Director's unexplained personal transfer  
C. Stationery purchased for office operations  
D. Owner's capital contribution  
**Answer: C**

20. Why should a company keep its transactions distinct from directors' private spending?  
A. Directors may never receive money from a company  
B. Companies cannot reimburse expenses  
C. Only banks require separation  
D. The company is a separate entity and transactions need correct business classification  
**Answer: D**

21. What should support an employee expense reimbursement?  
A. Business purpose, source evidence and required approval  
B. Employee seniority only  
C. A verbal statement only  
D. A personal bank statement with unrelated items  
**Answer: A**

22. When should an owner-related transaction be classified as “needs clarification”?  
A. Whenever the amount is under R100  
B. When the economic nature and authorised treatment are not clear from the evidence  
C. Whenever the owner signs it  
D. Never  
**Answer: B**

23. Which stage comes first in the bookkeeping cycle?  
A. Trial balance  
B. Financial statements  
C. A transaction or financial event occurs  
D. Closing entries  
**Answer: C**

24. Why should source documents be checked before capture?  
A. Software cannot save them  
B. All invoices are automatically wrong  
C. It replaces reconciliation  
D. The bookkeeper needs reliable details and evidence for the entry  
**Answer: D**

25. What is the purpose of transaction analysis?  
A. Identify the accounts and accounting effects before or during capture  
B. Decide whether a customer is profitable  
C. Replace supporting documents  
D. Remove approval controls  
**Answer: A**

26. What does a ledger show?  
A. Only source-document images  
B. Movements and balances accumulated by account  
C. Only the annual tax amount  
D. Password activity only  
**Answer: B**

27. Which is an example of a reconciliation?  
A. Comparing two unrelated budgets  
B. Renaming a ledger account  
C. Comparing the cashbook with the bank statement and investigating differences  
D. Printing an invoice  
**Answer: C**

28. A trial balance balances. What can still be wrong?  
A. Nothing  
B. Only the date format  
C. The bank cannot reconcile  
D. A transaction may have been posted to the wrong account with equal debit and credit values  
**Answer: D**

29. Which is a common period-end adjustment?  
A. Accrual  
B. Customer greeting  
C. Supplier onboarding only  
D. Password reset  
**Answer: A**

30. Why should closing balances connect to the next period's opening balances?  
A. To remove source documents  
B. To maintain continuity between accounting periods  
C. To create new income  
D. To prevent reconciliations  
**Answer: B**

31. What does integrity require from a bookkeeper?  
A. Agreeing with every manager request  
B. Never making mistakes  
C. Keeping records honest and refusing deliberate manipulation  
D. Working without review  
**Answer: C**

32. A material error is discovered after capture. What is the WORST response?  
A. Confirm the correct information  
B. Use the approved correction process  
C. Preserve evidence of the correction  
D. Delete or alter evidence so nobody sees the original error  
**Answer: D**

33. Which situation creates a conflict-of-interest concern?  
A. A bookkeeper controls payment processing for a supplier owned by their close relative without required disclosure  
B. A bookkeeper reconciles the bank account  
C. A customer pays by EFT  
D. A supplier provides a statement  
**Answer: A**

34. Why should urgent financial requests still follow required controls?  
A. Urgent requests are always fraudulent  
B. Urgency does not remove approval, verification or security risks  
C. Controls apply only at month-end  
D. Managers may not request urgent work  
**Answer: B**

35. Which is a fraud warning sign rather than proof?  
A. A correctly approved routine payment  
B. A reconciled bank account  
C. Repeated payments just below an approval threshold  
D. A properly filed invoice  
**Answer: C**

36. What should a bookkeeper do with a suspicious transaction?  
A. Publicly accuse the employee involved  
B. Delete it immediately  
C. Ignore it unless cash is missing  
D. Preserve evidence and follow authorised verification/escalation procedures  
**Answer: D**

37. What does the accrual concept recognise?  
A. Income and expenses may belong to a period different from the cash movement  
B. Only cash transactions are valid  
C. Every invoice is paid immediately  
D. Accruals apply only to banks  
**Answer: A**

38. Why should similar transactions be classified consistently?  
A. It guarantees profit  
B. It makes reports more comparable and useful  
C. It removes the need for judgement  
D. It prevents all accounting changes  
**Answer: B**

39. Which statement BEST describes materiality?  
A. Every R1 error is always material  
B. Management may choose any threshold without policy  
C. Significance depends on the nature, size and context of the information  
D. Materiality means only physical assets  
**Answer: C**

40. What does prudence NOT mean?  
A. Being careful with uncertain estimates  
B. Avoiding unsupported income  
C. Applying accounting policies carefully  
D. Deliberately understating profit to be “safe”  
**Answer: D**

41. Which statement about accounting frameworks is correct?  
A. The applicable framework depends on the entity's reporting requirements and adopted policies  
B. Every entity uses exactly the same framework  
C. A bookkeeper may choose any framework personally  
D. Reporting frameworks replace source documents  
**Answer: A**

42. Why might annual insurance paid in advance require a prepayment adjustment?  
A. Because insurance is never an expense  
B. Part of the payment may relate to future reporting periods  
C. The bank payment must be reversed completely  
D. Prepayments are liabilities  
**Answer: B**

43. What is the chart of accounts?  
A. A list of customers only  
B. A supplier-payment schedule  
C. The organised list of ledger accounts used to classify transactions  
D. A bank reconciliation  
**Answer: C**

44. What is a danger of an overused “Miscellaneous Expense” account?  
A. It automatically makes the trial balance fail  
B. It prevents invoices from being saved  
C. It creates a loan balance  
D. It can hide inconsistent or poor transaction classification  
**Answer: D**

45. Which account is normally a liability?  
A. Loan Payable  
B. Bank  
C. Sales Income  
D. Rent Expense  
**Answer: A**

46. Why should individual customer balances not normally require separate general-ledger income accounts?  
A. Customers should not have records  
B. Customer detail belongs in the customer/subledger while the general ledger uses appropriate control and income accounts  
C. Income accounts cannot have customers  
D. Customer balances are always liabilities  
**Answer: B**

47. What should happen before deleting or merging an existing ledger account?  
A. Nothing if the balance is zero today  
B. Ask any user to approve it  
C. Consider historical use, mappings, reports and the authorised account-change process  
D. Rename all accounts first  
**Answer: C**

48. What is a preventive internal control?  
A. Reviewing an error after year-end  
B. Investigating a completed duplicate payment  
C. Correcting an entry  
D. Requiring approval before a payment is released  
**Answer: D**

49. What is a detective internal control?  
A. Bank reconciliation  
B. Password policy before access  
C. Payment approval before release  
D. Supplier verification before a change  
**Answer: A**

50. Why is segregation of duties useful?  
A. It removes accountability  
B. It reduces the ability of one person to initiate, approve, complete and conceal a high-risk transaction  
C. It means every task needs four employees  
D. It prevents electronic bookkeeping  
**Answer: B**

51. A small business has too few staff for full segregation. What is a reasonable response?  
A. Remove all controls  
B. Give one person every password  
C. Add compensating independent reviews by an owner, manager or appropriate external party  
D. Stop reconciling accounts  
**Answer: C**

52. Which principle BEST summarises strong bookkeeping foundations?  
A. Record quickly even when evidence is unclear  
B. Focus only on whether debits equal credits  
C. Let seniority override control procedures  
D. Record the economic reality accurately, preserve evidence, apply double-entry consistently and use controls to protect the process  
**Answer: D**
