# Module 1 Summative Assessment Bank
## Bookkeeping Foundations

**Bank size:** 52 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 25 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Coverage:** All eight Module 1 lessons.

1. Which statement BEST describes the value of bookkeeping to management?  
A. It deals only with annual tax returns  
B. It provides controlled financial records for decisions, reporting and follow-up  
C. It replaces management judgement  
D. It guarantees a business will make a profit  
**Answer: B**

2. Which task is MOST typical of routine bookkeeping?  
A. Recording transactions and reconciling related financial records  
B. Setting national tax policy  
C. Giving a legal opinion on a contract  
D. Approving every capital investment  
**Answer: A**

3. A supplier invoice is captured twice. What is a likely consequence?  
A. Owners' capital automatically decreases twice  
B. Bank automatically increases  
C. Customer income is overstated  
D. The supplier liability or expense may be overstated and duplicate payment risk increases  
**Answer: D**

4. Why should a bookkeeper maintain source evidence?  
A. It makes reports longer  
B. It replaces double-entry  
C. It supports traceability, verification and the audit trail  
D. It removes approval requirements  
**Answer: C**

5. What should a bookkeeper do when a tax treatment is outside their competence?  
A. Preserve the evidence and refer it to the authorised specialist  
B. Ignore the transaction  
C. Guess using the previous transaction  
D. Ask the customer to decide  
**Answer: A**

6. Why is a current debtors list useful?  
A. It shows only cash expenses  
B. It replaces the bank statement  
C. It shows supplier balances only  
D. It helps identify amounts still owed by customers  
**Answer: D**

7. Which statement about financial confidentiality is correct?  
A. Bank details may be sent through any convenient account  
B. Access should be limited to legitimate authorised work needs  
C. Confidentiality applies only to payroll  
D. All internal staff may see all records  
**Answer: B**

8. What is the BEST description of an audit trail?  
A. A customer list  
B. A final profit figure  
C. Traceable evidence of transactions, changes, approvals and records  
D. A spreadsheet password  
**Answer: C**

9. Which equation must remain balanced?  
A. Equity = Expenses + Liabilities  
B. Assets = Liabilities + Owners' Equity  
C. Bank = Sales + Debtors  
D. Income = Cash + Expenses  
**Answer: B**

10. Which account type normally increases with a debit?  
A. Owners' equity  
B. Income  
C. Liability  
D. Asset  
**Answer: D**

11. Which account type normally increases with a credit?  
A. Income  
B. Asset  
C. Drawings  
D. Expense  
**Answer: A**

12. A business buys office equipment for R10,000 cash. What is the core double-entry effect?  
A. Equipment increases and bank decreases  
B. Bank increases and income increases  
C. Expense increases and creditors increase  
D. Equipment decreases and bank increases  
**Answer: A**

13. A business purchases goods for R6,000 on credit. Which basic effect is correct?  
A. Creditors decrease and bank decreases  
B. Bank increases and income increases  
C. An asset/purchase-related account increases and creditors increase  
D. Drawings increase and bank decreases  
**Answer: C**

14. Why is receiving payment from a debtor not new sales income?  
A. Bank receipts are never recorded  
B. The sale was recognised earlier; the receipt settles the amount owed  
C. Customers cannot pay by EFT  
D. Debtors are liabilities  
**Answer: B**

15. What does double-entry mean?  
A. Every transaction is entered twice in the same account  
B. Only cash transactions use two accounts  
C. Every transaction has equal debit and credit effects  
D. Two bookkeepers must capture every transaction  
**Answer: C**

16. A bank loan is received. Which statement is correct?  
A. It is automatically owner capital  
B. Bank increases and a loan liability increases  
C. It is automatically sales income  
D. It should never be recorded until repayment  
**Answer: B**

17. What are drawings in a sole proprietorship?  
A. Customer refunds  
B. Supplier discounts  
C. Personal value withdrawn by the owner from the business  
D. Wages paid to all employees  
**Answer: C**

18. Why should a shareholder transfer into a company not automatically be recorded as sales?  
A. All company receipts are liabilities  
B. The amount may represent capital, a loan or another transaction requiring clarification  
C. Shareholders cannot transfer money  
D. Sales can only be cash  
**Answer: B**

19. Which transaction is clearly a business expense if properly supported?  
A. Owner's capital contribution  
B. Stationery purchased for office operations  
C. Owner's private grocery purchase  
D. Director's unexplained personal transfer  
**Answer: B**

20. Why should a company keep its transactions distinct from directors' private spending?  
A. Only banks require separation  
B. Directors may never receive money from a company  
C. The company is a separate entity and transactions need correct business classification  
D. Companies cannot reimburse expenses  
**Answer: C**

21. What should support an employee expense reimbursement?  
A. Business purpose, source evidence and required approval  
B. Employee seniority only  
C. A personal bank statement with unrelated items  
D. A verbal statement only  
**Answer: A**

22. When should an owner-related transaction be classified as “needs clarification”?  
A. When the economic nature and authorised treatment are not clear from the evidence  
B. Never  
C. Whenever the owner signs it  
D. Whenever the amount is under R100  
**Answer: A**

23. Which stage comes first in the bookkeeping cycle?  
A. Financial statements  
B. A transaction or financial event occurs  
C. Trial balance  
D. Closing entries  
**Answer: B**

24. Why should source documents be checked before capture?  
A. Software cannot save them  
B. All invoices are automatically wrong  
C. It replaces reconciliation  
D. The bookkeeper needs reliable details and evidence for the entry  
**Answer: D**

25. What is the purpose of transaction analysis?  
A. Decide whether a customer is profitable  
B. Identify the accounts and accounting effects before or during capture  
C. Remove approval controls  
D. Replace supporting documents  
**Answer: B**

26. What does a ledger show?  
A. Only source-document images  
B. Movements and balances accumulated by account  
C. Only the annual tax amount  
D. Password activity only  
**Answer: B**

27. Which is an example of a reconciliation?  
A. Comparing the cashbook with the bank statement and investigating differences  
B. Printing an invoice  
C. Comparing two unrelated budgets  
D. Renaming a ledger account  
**Answer: A**

28. A trial balance balances. What can still be wrong?  
A. Nothing  
B. Only the date format  
C. The bank cannot reconcile  
D. A transaction may have been posted to the wrong account with equal debit and credit values  
**Answer: D**

29. Which is a common period-end adjustment?  
A. Accrual  
B. Supplier onboarding only  
C. Password reset  
D. Customer greeting  
**Answer: A**

30. Why should closing balances connect to the next period's opening balances?  
A. To prevent reconciliations  
B. To create new income  
C. To remove source documents  
D. To maintain continuity between accounting periods  
**Answer: D**

31. What does integrity require from a bookkeeper?  
A. Agreeing with every manager request  
B. Never making mistakes  
C. Keeping records honest and refusing deliberate manipulation  
D. Working without review  
**Answer: C**

32. A material error is discovered after capture. What is the WORST response?  
A. Preserve evidence of the correction  
B. Confirm the correct information  
C. Delete or alter evidence so nobody sees the original error  
D. Use the approved correction process  
**Answer: C**

33. Which situation creates a conflict-of-interest concern?  
A. A bookkeeper controls payment processing for a supplier owned by their close relative without required disclosure  
B. A bookkeeper reconciles the bank account  
C. A supplier provides a statement  
D. A customer pays by EFT  
**Answer: A**

34. Why should urgent financial requests still follow required controls?  
A. Managers may not request urgent work  
B. Urgent requests are always fraudulent  
C. Urgency does not remove approval, verification or security risks  
D. Controls apply only at month-end  
**Answer: C**

35. Which is a fraud warning sign rather than proof?  
A. A reconciled bank account  
B. A correctly approved routine payment  
C. Repeated payments just below an approval threshold  
D. A properly filed invoice  
**Answer: C**

36. What should a bookkeeper do with a suspicious transaction?  
A. Preserve evidence and follow authorised verification/escalation procedures  
B. Delete it immediately  
C. Publicly accuse the employee involved  
D. Ignore it unless cash is missing  
**Answer: A**

37. What does the accrual concept recognise?  
A. Only cash transactions are valid  
B. Every invoice is paid immediately  
C. Accruals apply only to banks  
D. Income and expenses may belong to a period different from the cash movement  
**Answer: D**

38. Why should similar transactions be classified consistently?  
A. It prevents all accounting changes  
B. It guarantees profit  
C. It makes reports more comparable and useful  
D. It removes the need for judgement  
**Answer: C**

39. Which statement BEST describes materiality?  
A. Every R1 error is always material  
B. Materiality means only physical assets  
C. Management may choose any threshold without policy  
D. Significance depends on the nature, size and context of the information  
**Answer: D**

40. What does prudence NOT mean?  
A. Deliberately understating profit to be “safe”  
B. Being careful with uncertain estimates  
C. Avoiding unsupported income  
D. Applying accounting policies carefully  
**Answer: A**

41. Which statement about accounting frameworks is correct?  
A. A bookkeeper may choose any framework personally  
B. Reporting frameworks replace source documents  
C. Every entity uses exactly the same framework  
D. The applicable framework depends on the entity's reporting requirements and adopted policies  
**Answer: D**

42. Why might annual insurance paid in advance require a prepayment adjustment?  
A. The bank payment must be reversed completely  
B. Prepayments are liabilities  
C. Part of the payment may relate to future reporting periods  
D. Because insurance is never an expense  
**Answer: C**

43. A bookkeeper needs a consistent ledger code for every transaction category. Which structure provides that organised account list?  
A. A bank reconciliation  
B. The organised list of ledger accounts used to classify transactions  
C. A supplier-payment schedule  
D. A list of customers only  
**Answer: B**

44. What is a danger of an overused “Miscellaneous Expense” account?  
A. It can hide inconsistent or poor transaction classification  
B. It creates a loan balance  
C. It automatically makes the trial balance fail  
D. It prevents invoices from being saved  
**Answer: A**

45. Which account is normally a liability?  
A. Rent Expense  
B. Sales Income  
C. Bank  
D. Loan Payable  
**Answer: D**

46. Why should individual customer balances not normally require separate general-ledger income accounts?  
A. Customer detail belongs in the customer/subledger while the general ledger uses appropriate control and income accounts  
B. Income accounts cannot have customers  
C. Customers should not have records  
D. Customer balances are always liabilities  
**Answer: A**

47. What should happen before deleting or merging an existing ledger account?  
A. Ask any user to approve it  
B. Consider historical use, mappings, reports and the authorised account-change process  
C. Rename all accounts first  
D. Nothing if the balance is zero today  
**Answer: B**

48. What is a preventive internal control?  
A. Reviewing an error after year-end  
B. Investigating a completed duplicate payment  
C. Requiring approval before a payment is released  
D. Correcting an entry  
**Answer: C**

49. What is a detective internal control?  
A. Payment approval before release  
B. Password policy before access  
C. Supplier verification before a change  
D. Bank reconciliation  
**Answer: D**

50. Why is segregation of duties useful?  
A. It prevents electronic bookkeeping  
B. It reduces the ability of one person to initiate, approve, complete and conceal a high-risk transaction  
C. It removes accountability  
D. It means every task needs four employees  
**Answer: B**

51. A small business has too few staff for full segregation. What is a reasonable response?  
A. Give one person every password  
B. Stop reconciling accounts  
C. Remove all controls  
D. Add compensating independent reviews by an owner, manager or appropriate external party  
**Answer: D**

52. Which principle BEST summarises strong bookkeeping foundations?  
A. Record quickly even when evidence is unclear  
B. Focus only on whether debits equal credits  
C. Let seniority override control procedures  
D. Record the economic reality accurately, preserve evidence, apply double-entry consistently and use controls to protect the process  
**Answer: D**
