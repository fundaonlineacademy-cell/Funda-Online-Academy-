# Module 7 Summative Assessment Bank
## Computerised Bookkeeping & QuickBooks Fundamentals

**Bank size:** 52 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 25 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Coverage:** All eight Module 7 lessons.

1. What is the relationship between manual and computerised bookkeeping?  
A. Software automates the same underlying accounting principles  
B. Software removes double entry  
C. Manual books never use ledgers  
D. Software does not need source documents  
**Answer: A**

2. What is master data?  
A. Printed financial statements  
B. Core records/settings such as accounts, customers and suppliers  
C. Bank reconciliation only  
D. Closing journals only  
**Answer: B**

3. Why can poor software setup create widespread errors?  
A. Software changes tax law  
B. Reports stop running  
C. Wrong mappings/settings can be applied repeatedly  
D. Users cannot edit data  
**Answer: C**

4. What is an audit trail?  
A. Customer ageing  
B. Supplier statement  
C. Bank deposit list  
D. Traceable history of transactions and changes  
**Answer: D**

5. Why is manual bookkeeping knowledge still useful in software?  
A. It helps diagnose which underlying accounts and processes are wrong  
B. Software never posts automatically  
C. Computerised books have no reports  
D. It replaces the software  
**Answer: A**

6. What is a chart of accounts?  
A. Customer contact list  
B. Structured list of ledger accounts  
C. Bank-feed rule list  
D. Payroll schedule  
**Answer: B**

7. Why does an account type matter?  
A. It sets the username  
B. It changes screen colour  
C. It affects accounting logic and report presentation  
D. It controls printer settings  
**Answer: C**

8. A loan receipt is set up as Sales Income. What is the likely problem?  
A. Bank is understated  
B. Debtors increases  
C. Creditors disappears  
D. Income/profit may be overstated while liability is understated  
**Answer: D**

9. Where should migration opening balances come from?  
A. Reconciled approved closing records from the prior system  
B. Estimates  
C. Bank balance only  
D. Customer statements only  
**Answer: A**

10. Why should duplicate chart-of-accounts entries be avoided?  
A. They stop all transactions  
B. They split similar activity and weaken consistency  
C. They increase cash  
D. They automatically create tax  
**Answer: B**

11. What should happen before live deployment of a company file?  
A. Delete sample transactions without review  
B. Add every possible account  
C. Test workflows and review resulting reports  
D. Disable user access controls  
**Answer: C**

12. What is a master record?  
A. Bank statement  
B. Trial-balance line  
C. Journal narration  
D. Core recurring customer/supplier information used by the system  
**Answer: D**

13. Why search before creating a supplier?  
A. To prevent duplicate master records  
B. To avoid invoices  
C. To reduce bank balance  
D. To change Sales  
**Answer: A**

14. What should opening customer balances reconcile to?  
A. Creditors Control  
B. Debtors Control and approved customer schedule  
C. Asset register  
D. Bank only  
**Answer: B**

15. Why is invoice-level opening detail useful?  
A. It controls user passwords  
B. It changes account type  
C. Future receipts may need allocation against specific open invoices  
D. It replaces customer masters  
**Answer: C**

16. What should govern changes to sensitive supplier master information?  
A. Any user's judgement  
B. Customer request  
C. Bank feed  
D. Approved verification and change-control process  
**Answer: D**

17. Why should customer credit terms be controlled?  
A. They affect collection expectations and credit exposure  
B. They only affect invoice colour  
C. They change the bank password  
D. They are never used in reports  
**Answer: A**

18. Which software workflow is normally best for a credit customer sale?  
A. Supplier bill  
B. Customer sales invoice  
C. Bank reconciliation  
D. Fixed-asset disposal  
**Answer: B**

19. Which workflow is normally best for a supplier invoice on credit?  
A. Customer receipt  
B. Direct sales receipt  
C. Supplier bill / accounts-payable workflow  
D. Bank transfer only  
**Answer: C**

20. Why should payment of an existing supplier bill not create another expense?  
A. Expenses are never paid  
B. Bank does not change  
C. Supplier bills do not affect Creditors  
D. The bill already recorded the expense/asset and liability  
**Answer: D**

21. Which fields should be checked before saving a transaction?  
A. Party, date, reference, account, amount and applicable tax treatment  
B. Only description  
C. Only amount  
D. Only customer name  
**Answer: A**

22. What should happen when software warns of a duplicate supplier invoice number?  
A. Change the number to bypass the warning  
B. Investigate whether it is already recorded  
C. Pay both  
D. Delete the supplier  
**Answer: B**

23. Why use a credit-note workflow rather than deleting a posted customer invoice?  
A. It increases Sales  
B. It changes Bank  
C. It provides a traceable reduction linked to the original transaction  
D. It eliminates customer history  
**Answer: C**

24. What is a bank feed?  
A. Customer invoice list  
B. Manual journal  
C. Fixed-asset register  
D. Electronic import of bank transactions  
**Answer: D**

25. What does Match do in a bank feed?  
A. Link an imported line to an existing accounting transaction  
B. Create a new duplicate transaction  
C. Reopen the prior period  
D. Delete the statement item  
**Answer: A**

26. When should an imported bank item be Added rather than Matched?  
A. Whenever a transaction already exists  
B. When it represents a genuinely unrecorded valid transaction  
C. For all supplier payments  
D. For every customer receipt  
**Answer: B**

27. Why should bank-feed automation rules be reviewed?  
A. Rules are never used  
B. They only affect screen layout  
C. An outdated rule can repeatedly misclassify transactions  
D. They remove the bank statement  
**Answer: C**

28. How should an unidentified deposit in the feed be handled?  
A. Add to Sales  
B. Add to Capital  
C. Allocate to any customer  
D. Investigate before classification  
**Answer: D**

29. What can cause a software reconciliation opening balance to change unexpectedly?  
A. A previously reconciled transaction was later altered  
B. Customer name changed  
C. New report exported  
D. Invoice printed  
**Answer: A**

30. Why should a reconciliation difference not be forced with a random adjustment?  
A. Reconciliations never reach zero  
B. It can conceal the actual error or omission  
C. Adjustments always increase tax  
D. Bank statements cannot contain differences  
**Answer: B**

31. What is a reversal?  
A. Report export  
B. Master-data update  
C. Opposite accounting entry cancelling a prior posting  
D. Customer statement  
**Answer: C**

32. When may directly editing a posted transaction be inappropriate?  
A. Before a draft is saved  
B. Before source evidence exists  
C. When no impact exists  
D. When it was approved, reconciled, externally issued or belongs to a closed period  
**Answer: D**

33. What is the purpose of audit history?  
A. Show user actions and transaction changes  
B. Calculate gross profit  
C. Replace source documents  
D. Set payment terms  
**Answer: A**

34. Why should user permissions follow job responsibilities?  
A. To allow everyone full access  
B. To limit inappropriate changes and sensitive functions  
C. To prevent all accounting work  
D. To make reports longer  
**Answer: B**

35. Why are period locks useful?  
A. They increase Sales  
B. They hide transactions  
C. They restrict changes to closed reporting periods  
D. They delete reconciliations  
**Answer: C**

36. What should correction documentation contain?  
A. Only final balance  
B. No source evidence  
C. Only user name  
D. Error, support, correction, approval and references  
**Answer: D**

37. Which is a common accounting-system report?  
A. Trial Balance  
B. Social-media report  
C. Building-access list  
D. Telephone directory  
**Answer: A**

38. Why should report date parameters be checked?  
A. Dates only affect filenames  
B. Different dates can produce different balances and activity  
C. Reports always show all years  
D. Dates never affect ageing  
**Answer: B**

39. Why should report filters be reviewed?  
A. Filters cannot change results  
B. Reports ignore filters  
C. Old filters may unintentionally exclude information  
D. Filters are only visual  
**Answer: C**

40. What is a static report export?  
A. Live system report  
B. Bank feed  
C. Audit log  
D. Snapshot file that does not update with later system changes  
**Answer: D**

41. When is PDF often useful?  
A. When a controlled fixed-reference report is needed  
B. When users need to edit formulas  
C. When opening balances are created  
D. When bank feeds are matched  
**Answer: A**

42. Why should exported spreadsheets be controlled?  
A. They cannot contain data  
B. They can be edited outside the accounting system  
C. They never include formulas  
D. They automatically update the ledger  
**Answer: B**

43. What should a debtor age-analysis report agree with?  
A. Creditors Control  
B. Bank statement  
C. Relevant Debtors Control and customer schedules  
D. Fixed assets  
**Answer: C**

44. Why should financial report recipients be checked?  
A. Reports are always public  
B. Every employee needs them  
C. PDF is automatically confidential  
D. Reports may contain sensitive business or personal information  
**Answer: D**

45. In an end-to-end practice, what should happen first?  
A. Confirm setup, opening balances and masters  
B. Reconcile before transactions  
C. Export final reports  
D. Close the period  
**Answer: A**

46. A supplier payment already exists and appears in the bank feed. What should happen?  
A. Add a new expense  
B. Match it to the existing payment  
C. Delete the supplier bill  
D. Post another creditor payment  
**Answer: B**

47. A supplier invoice was posted to Equipment instead of Cleaning Materials. What is the core error?  
A. Supplier identity  
B. Bank matching  
C. Account classification  
D. Invoice existence  
**Answer: C**

48. Why should correcting that supplier invoice not create a second creditor liability?  
A. Supplier bills cannot be corrected  
B. The amount is too small  
C. Creditors are optional  
D. The original bill already created the liability; only classification is wrong  
**Answer: D**

49. Which reconciliations belong in an end-to-end workflow?  
A. Customer, supplier/control-account and bank reconciliations  
B. Only bank  
C. Only customers  
D. Only suppliers  
**Answer: A**

50. Which report helps review user transaction changes?  
A. Income Statement only  
B. Audit/history report  
C. Customer statement only  
D. Asset register only  
**Answer: B**

51. What should a final evidence pack demonstrate?  
A. Only software screenshots  
B. Only printed reports  
C. Traceable source, capture, correction, reconciliation and reporting controls

**Answer: C**

52. Which principle BEST summarises Module 7?  
A. Software removes the need for bookkeeping knowledge  
B. Bank feeds should always create new transactions  
C. Reports do not require reconciliation  
D. Computerised bookkeeping is reliable when accounting logic, setup, access, capture, correction, reconciliation and reporting controls work together
