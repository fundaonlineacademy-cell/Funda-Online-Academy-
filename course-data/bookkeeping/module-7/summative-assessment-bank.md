# Module 7 Summative Assessment Bank
## Computerised Bookkeeping & QuickBooks Fundamentals

**Bank size:** 52 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 25 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Coverage:** All eight Module 7 lessons.

1. What is the relationship between manual and computerised bookkeeping?  
A. Manual books never use ledgers  
B. Software automates the same underlying accounting principles  
C. Software does not need source documents  
D. Software removes double entry  
**Answer: B**

2. Customers, suppliers and ledger accounts are examples of which core system records or settings?  
A. Printed financial statements  
B. Bank reconciliation only  
C. Closing journals only  
D. Core records/settings such as accounts, customers and suppliers  
**Answer: D**

3. Why can poor software setup create widespread errors?  
A. Users cannot edit data  
B. Software changes tax law  
C. Wrong mappings/settings can be applied repeatedly  
D. Reports stop running  
**Answer: C**

4. Which record history lets a reviewer trace transactions and later changes?  
A. Bank deposit list  
B. Traceable history of transactions and changes  
C. Customer ageing  
D. Supplier statement  
**Answer: B**

5. Why is manual bookkeeping knowledge still useful in software?  
A. It helps diagnose which underlying accounts and processes are wrong  
B. It replaces the software  
C. Computerised books have no reports  
D. Software never posts automatically  
**Answer: A**

6. What is a chart of accounts?  
A. Structured list of ledger accounts  
B. Customer contact list  
C. Payroll schedule  
D. Bank-feed rule list  
**Answer: A**

7. Office equipment is mistakenly created as an expense account in the accounting system. What is the main reporting risk?  
A. The screen colour will differ from other accounts  
B. The printer settings will be reset when reports are generated  
C. The user name will change when the account is used  
D. Assets, expenses and profit may be misstated because the account behaves and reports incorrectly  
**Answer: D**

8. A loan receipt is set up as Sales Income. What is the likely problem?  
A. Debtors increases  
B. Bank is understated  
C. Income/profit may be overstated while liability is understated  
D. Creditors disappears  
**Answer: C**

9. Where should migration opening balances come from?  
A. Reconciled approved closing records from the prior system  
B. Customer statements only  
C. Bank balance only  
D. Estimates  
**Answer: A**

10. The chart of accounts contains two delivery-expense accounts used by different staff. What is the main risk?  
A. The bank balance will increase when either account is used  
B. All transactions will stop posting to the ledger  
C. Similar costs will be split, weakening consistency and report analysis  
D. The system will create an additional tax liability automatically  
**Answer: C**

11. What should happen before live deployment of a company file?  
A. Delete sample transactions without review  
B. Disable user access controls  
C. Test workflows and review resulting reports  
D. Add every possible account  
**Answer: C**

12. What is a master record?  
A. Journal narration  
B. Bank statement  
C. Trial-balance line  
D. Core recurring customer/supplier information used by the system  
**Answer: D**

13. Why should a user search for “Mthembu Trading” and likely spelling variants before creating a new supplier record?  
A. To prevent duplicate supplier masters, split histories and duplicate-payment risk  
B. To avoid receiving invoices from the supplier  
C. To reduce the bank balance before the first purchase  
D. To change the Sales account used for customer invoices  
**Answer: A**

14. Before live processing, the total opening customer schedule should agree with which balance?  
A. Bank only  
B. Asset register  
C. Creditors Control  
D. Debtors Control and approved customer schedule  
**Answer: D**

15. Why is invoice-level opening detail useful?  
A. It controls user passwords  
B. It changes account type  
C. It replaces customer masters  
D. Future receipts may need allocation against specific open invoices  
**Answer: D**

16. What should govern changes to sensitive supplier master information?  
A. Any user's judgement  
B. Bank feed  
C. Approved verification and change-control process  
D. Customer request  
**Answer: C**

17. Why should customer credit terms be controlled?  
A. They change the bank password  
B. They only affect invoice colour  
C. They affect collection expectations and credit exposure  
D. They are never used in reports  
**Answer: C**

18. Which software workflow is normally best for a credit customer sale?  
A. Supplier bill  
B. Bank reconciliation  
C. Fixed-asset disposal

**Answer: D**

19. Which workflow is normally best for a supplier invoice on credit?  
A. Supplier bill / accounts-payable workflow  
B. Direct sales receipt  
C. Customer receipt  
D. Bank transfer only  
**Answer: A**

20. A supplier bill was recorded earlier as an expense and creditor. When its payment is imported, what should normally happen?  
A. Match or allocate the payment to settle the existing creditor balance  
B. Leave the bank line unprocessed because the bill exists  
C. Delete the original bill before recording the payment

21. Which fields should be checked before saving a transaction?  
A. Only customer name  
B. Party, date, reference, account, amount and applicable tax treatment

22. What should happen when software warns of a duplicate supplier invoice number?

23. Why use a credit-note workflow rather than deleting a posted customer invoice?  
A. It eliminates customer history  
B. It changes Bank

24. What is a bank feed?  
A. Fixed-asset register  
B. Customer invoice list  
C. Manual journal  
D. Electronic import of bank transactions  
**Answer: D**

25. A R2,400 supplier payment already exists in the ledger and the same payment appears in the bank feed. What does matching achieve?

B. It links the bank-feed line to the existing payment without creating a duplicate  
C. It reopens the prior accounting period automatically

26. When should an imported bank item be Added rather than Matched?
