# Module 7 Formative Assessment Bank
## Computerised Bookkeeping & QuickBooks Fundamentals

**Bank size:** 32 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 15 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Prerequisite:** Complete all eight Module 7 lessons.

## Lesson 1 — Moving from Manual to Computerised Bookkeeping
1. Does accounting software replace double-entry principles?  
A. No; the same accounting logic operates underneath  
B. Yes  
C. Only for Sales  
D. Only for Bank  
**Answer: A**

2. What is master data?  
A. Printed reports  
B. Core records/settings such as customers, suppliers and accounts  
C. Bank reconciliation only  
D. Trial-balance total  
**Answer: B**

3. What is an audit trail?  
A. A customer statement  
B. Supplier ageing  
C. Traceable history of transactions and changes  
D. A tax rate  
**Answer: C**

4. Why can automation increase error impact?  
A. Software never repeats rules  
B. Reports cannot be changed  
C. Bank feeds are always correct  
D. A bad setup or rule can be applied repeatedly  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 2 — Setting Up a Company File and Chart of Accounts
5. What should opening balances come from?  
A. Approved reconciled source records  
B. Estimates typed quickly  
C. Bank balance only  
D. Customer memory  
**Answer: A**

6. Why should duplicate accounts be avoided?  
A. They reduce VAT  
B. They split similar activity and weaken reporting consistency  
C. They prevent invoicing  
D. They close periods  
**Answer: B**

7. Why does account type matter?  
A. It changes invoice colour  
B. It affects user names  
C. It affects transaction logic and report presentation  
D. It only affects printing  
**Answer: C**

8. What should happen before live use?  
A. Delete opening balances  
B. Add every possible account  
C. Skip reporting  
D. Test sample transactions and review resulting reports  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 3 — Customers, Suppliers and Opening Information
9. Why search before creating a new supplier?  
A. To reduce duplicate master records  
B. To avoid supplier invoices  
C. To create more accounts  
D. To change opening balances  
**Answer: A**

10. What should opening customer balances reconcile to?  
A. Creditors Control  
B. Debtors Control and approved customer schedule  
C. Fixed-assets register  
D. Sales only  
**Answer: B**

11. Why may invoice-level opening detail matter?  
A. It changes bank balance  
B. It sets user permissions  
C. Later receipts may need allocation to specific invoices  
D. It prevents reports  
**Answer: C**

12. What should control changes to supplier master information?  
A. Customer preference  
B. Bank feed  
C. Any user  
D. Approved verification and change procedures  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 4 — Capturing Sales, Purchases and Expenses
13. A credit customer sale should normally use:  
A. the customer invoice workflow  
B. supplier-payment workflow  
C. bank-reconciliation screen  
D. fixed-asset register only  
**Answer: A**

14. Why should payment of an existing supplier bill not create the expense again?  
A. Expenses cannot be paid  
B. The bill already recorded the expense/asset and payable  
C. Supplier payments increase Sales  
D. Bank never changes  
**Answer: B**

15. What should happen on a duplicate invoice warning?  
A. Change the invoice number  
B. Override immediately  
C. Investigate before processing  
D. Pay it twice  
**Answer: C**

16. Why use credit-note workflows?  
A. To hide original invoices  
B. To increase balances  
C. To avoid audit trails  
D. To reduce balances through a traceable linked correction  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 5 — Banking, Bank Feeds and Reconciliation Workflows
17. What does Match mean in a bank feed?  
A. Link imported bank item to an existing accounting transaction  
B. Create a duplicate transaction  
C. Delete the bank entry  
D. Reopen the period  
**Answer: A**

18. What does Add/Categorise usually mean?  
A. Link to existing entry only  
B. Create a new accounting transaction from an imported item where appropriate  
C. Delete every unmatched item  
D. Reconcile automatically  
**Answer: B**

19. Why should bank rules be reviewed?  
A. Rules cannot change transactions  
B. They always expire  
C. Broad or outdated rules can repeatedly misclassify items  
D. They remove audit history  
**Answer: C**

20. Why should a reconciliation difference not be forced to zero?  
A. Zero is impossible  
B. Bank statements cannot reconcile  
C. Software will fix it later  
D. A forced adjustment can hide the real error  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 6 — Correcting Transactions, Audit Trails and User Controls
21. Why should corrections preserve traceability?  
A. Reviewers need to understand the original entry and correction  
B. To make reports longer  
C. To prevent all editing  
D. To remove source evidence  
**Answer: A**

22. What is a reversal?  
A. Customer statement  
B. Opposite entry used to cancel a prior posting  
C. Bank feed  
D. User role  
**Answer: B**

23. Why are closed-period changes sensitive?  
A. They change passwords  
B. They create suppliers  
C. They can alter previously reported or reconciled results  
D. They never affect reports  
**Answer: C**

24. What should correction documentation include?  
A. Only user name  
B. Only final amount  
C. No evidence  
D. Error, evidence, correct treatment, approval and reference  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 7 — Producing Reports and Exporting Evidence
25. What should be checked before exporting a report?  
A. Date range and filters  
B. Only filename  
C. Only page count  
D. Printer brand  
**Answer: A**

26. What should debtor ageing reconcile to?  
A. Fixed assets  
B. Relevant Debtors Control records  
C. Creditors only  
D. Capital  
**Answer: B**

27. What is a static exported report?  
A. A report that updates automatically  
B. A bank feed  
C. A snapshot that does not update when system data changes  
D. A journal template  
**Answer: C**

28. Why should financial reports be shared carefully?  
A. Reports have no sensitive information  
B. Every employee needs all reports  
C. PDFs are automatically public  
D. Reports may contain confidential financial or personal data  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 8 — QuickBooks-style End-to-End Practice Workflow
29. What should happen before batch transaction capture?  
A. Confirm setup and opening information  
B. Delete the chart of accounts  
C. Disable audit history  
D. Skip master-data checks  
**Answer: A**

30. If a supplier payment already exists when the bank feed imports it, what should happen?  
A. Add another expense  
B. Match the feed item to the existing payment  
C. Delete the supplier bill  
D. Post to Sales  
**Answer: B**

31. What should Debtors Control agree with?  
A. Supplier balances  
B. Bank only  
C. Total customer subsidiary balances  
D. Expense totals  
**Answer: C**

32. What should an end-to-end evidence pack prove?  
A. Only that reports were printed  
B. Only that transactions exist  
C. Only that software was used  
D. Transactions, corrections, reconciliations and reports are traceable and controlled  
**Answer: D**
