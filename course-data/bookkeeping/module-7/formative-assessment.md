# Module 7 Formative Assessment Bank
## Computerised Bookkeeping & QuickBooks Fundamentals

**Bank size:** 32 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 15 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Prerequisite:** Complete all eight Module 7 lessons.

## Lesson 1 — Moving from Manual to Computerised Bookkeeping
1. Does accounting software replace double-entry principles?  
A. Only for Sales  
B. No; the same accounting logic operates underneath  
C. Yes  
D. Only for Bank  
**Answer: B**

2. What is master data?  
A. Core records/settings such as customers, suppliers and accounts  
B. Bank reconciliation only  
C. Trial-balance total  
D. Printed reports  
**Answer: A**

3. A reviewer can trace a supplier payment from the invoice and approval to the ledger entry and bank transaction. What control does this demonstrate?  
A. A tax-rate check  
B. An audit trail  
C. A customer statement  
D. A supplier ageing calculation  
**Answer: B**

4. Why can automation increase error impact?  
A. Bank feeds are always correct  
B. Software never repeats rules  
C. Reports cannot be changed  
D. A bad setup or rule can be applied repeatedly  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 2 — Setting Up a Company File and Chart of Accounts
5. What should opening balances come from?  
A. Estimates typed quickly  
B. Approved reconciled source records  
C. Customer memory  
D. Bank balance only  
**Answer: B**

6. Why should duplicate accounts be avoided?  
A. They prevent invoicing  
B. They close periods  
C. They reduce VAT  
D. They split similar activity and weaken reporting consistency  
**Answer: D**

7. Why does account type matter?  
A. It affects user names  
B. It changes invoice colour  
C. It affects transaction logic and report presentation  
D. It only affects printing  
**Answer: C**

8. What should happen before live use?  
A. Delete opening balances  
B. Skip reporting  
C. Add every possible account  
D. Test sample transactions and review resulting reports  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 3 — Customers, Suppliers and Opening Information
9. Why search before creating a new supplier?  
A. To reduce duplicate master records  
B. To avoid supplier invoices  
C. To create more accounts  
D. To change opening balances  
**Answer: A**

10. What should opening customer balances reconcile to?  
A. Sales only  
B. Fixed-assets register  
C. Debtors Control and approved customer schedule  
D. Creditors Control  
**Answer: C**

11. Why may invoice-level opening detail matter?  
A. Later receipts may need allocation to specific invoices  
B. It changes bank balance  
C. It sets user permissions  
D. It prevents reports  
**Answer: A**

12. What should control changes to supplier master information?  
A. Customer preference  
B. Bank feed  
C. Approved verification and change procedures  
D. Any user  
**Answer: C**

## Lesson 4 — Capturing Sales, Purchases and Expenses
13. A credit customer sale should normally use:  
A. fixed-asset register only  
B. supplier-payment workflow  
C. bank-reconciliation screen  
D. the customer invoice workflow  
**Answer: D**

14. Why should payment of an existing supplier bill not create the expense again?  
A. Supplier payments increase Sales  
B. Bank never changes  
C. Expenses cannot be paid  
D. The bill already recorded the expense/asset and payable  
**Answer: D**

15. What should happen on a duplicate invoice warning?  
A. Pay it twice  
B. Change the invoice number  
C. Investigate before processing  
D. Override immediately  
**Answer: C**

16. Why use credit-note workflows?  
A. To increase balances  
B. To avoid audit trails  
C. To reduce balances through a traceable linked correction  
D. To hide original invoices  
**Answer: C**

## Lesson 5 — Banking, Bank Feeds and Reconciliation Workflows
17. What does Match mean in a bank feed?  
A. Create a duplicate transaction  
B. Link imported bank item to an existing accounting transaction  
C. Delete the bank entry  
D. Reopen the period  
**Answer: B**

18. What does Add/Categorise usually mean?  
A. Delete every unmatched item  
B. Create a new accounting transaction from an imported item where appropriate  
C. Link to existing entry only  
D. Reconcile automatically  
**Answer: B**

19. Why should bank rules be reviewed?  
A. They always expire  
B. They remove audit history  
C. Broad or outdated rules can repeatedly misclassify items  
D. Rules cannot change transactions  
**Answer: C**

20. Why should a reconciliation difference not be forced to zero?  
A. A forced adjustment can hide the real error  
B. Software will fix it later  
C. Zero is impossible  
D. Bank statements cannot reconcile  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 6 — Correcting Transactions, Audit Trails and User Controls
21. Why should corrections preserve traceability?  
A. Reviewers need to understand the original entry and correction  
B. To prevent all editing  
C. To remove source evidence  
D. To make reports longer  
**Answer: A**

22. Which entry is used to cancel the accounting effect of an earlier posting?  
A. User role  
B. Customer statement  
C. Opposite entry used to cancel a prior posting  
D. Bank feed  
**Answer: C**

23. Why are closed-period changes sensitive?  
A. They never affect reports  
B. They can alter previously reported or reconciled results  
C. They create suppliers  
D. They change passwords  
**Answer: B**

24. What should correction documentation include?  
A. Only final amount  
B. No evidence  
C. Only user name  
D. Error, evidence, correct treatment, approval and reference  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 7 — Producing Reports and Exporting Evidence
25. What should be checked before exporting a report?  
A. Only page count  
B. Date range and filters  
C. Printer brand  
D. Only filename  
**Answer: B**

26. What should debtor ageing reconcile to?  
A. Relevant Debtors Control records  
B. Fixed assets  
C. Creditors only  
D. Capital  
**Answer: A**

27. What is a static exported report?  
A. A snapshot that does not update when system data changes  
B. A bank feed  
C. A report that updates automatically  
D. A journal template  
**Answer: A**

28. Why should financial reports be shared carefully?  
A. PDFs are automatically public  
B. Reports have no sensitive information  
C. Every employee needs all reports  
D. Reports may contain confidential financial or personal data  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 8 — QuickBooks-style End-to-End Practice Workflow
29. What should happen before batch transaction capture?  
A. Confirm setup and opening information  
B. Disable audit history  
C. Delete the chart of accounts  
D. Skip master-data checks  
**Answer: A**

30. If a supplier payment already exists when the bank feed imports it, what should happen?  
A. Post to Sales  
B. Add another expense  
C. Match the feed item to the existing payment  
D. Delete the supplier bill  
**Answer: C**

31. What should Debtors Control agree with?  
A. Bank only  
B. Expense totals  
C. Supplier balances  
D. Total customer subsidiary balances  
**Answer: D**

32. What should an end-to-end evidence pack prove?  
A. Only that software was used  
B. Transactions, corrections, reconciliations and reports are traceable and controlled  
C. Only that transactions exist  
D. Only that reports were printed  
**Answer: B**
