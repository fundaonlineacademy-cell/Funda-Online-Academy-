# Module 10 Formative Assessment Bank
## Practical Bookkeeping Project

**Bank size:** 32 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 15 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Prerequisite:** Complete all eight Module 10 lessons.

## Lesson 1 — Capstone Brief and Simulated Business Setup
1. Why does the capstone use fictional data?  
A. To avoid controls  
B. To remove source documents  
C. To make bookkeeping unrealistic  
D. To practise safely without exposing real sensitive information  
**Answer: D**

2. What is an evidence index?  
A. A register linking project outputs to supporting evidence  
B. A salary schedule  
C. A tax return  
D. A bank statement  
**Answer: A**

3. Why should authority limits be defined?  
A. To allow the learner to approve everything  
B. To show which actions may be performed and which require approval or escalation  
C. To avoid journals  
D. To remove management  
**Answer: B**

4. What should be checked before current-period processing begins?  
A. Whether every account has activity  
B. Whether reports are already printed  
C. Whether opening debits and credits agree  
D. Only whether the bank balance is positive  
**Answer: C**

## Lesson 2 — Build the Chart of Accounts and Opening Controls
5. What is the main purpose of the chart of accounts?  
A. Replace source evidence  
B. Store passwords  
C. Provide structured ledger categories for consistent recording and reporting  
D. Approve transactions  
**Answer: C**

6. What should Debtors Control agree to?  
A. Net total of individual customer balances  
B. Total supplier balances  
C. Equity  
D. Bank balance  
**Answer: A**

7. Why are duplicate ledger accounts risky?  
A. Transactions may be split inconsistently between similar accounts  
B. They guarantee accuracy  
C. They eliminate reconciliation  
D. They make reports shorter  
**Answer: A**

8. Which is the normal balance of an expense account?  
A. Zero always  
B. Credit  
C. Liability  
D. Debit  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 3 — Process Source Documents and Routine Transactions
9. Before capture, what should be determined first?  
A. Which document looks newest  
B. Which account was used last time  
C. What happened economically and which accounts are affected  
D. Who is most senior  
**Answer: C**

10. A customer receipt against an existing invoice normally:  
A. increases expenses  
B. creates new sales again  
C. increases creditors  
D. reduces the debtor balance and increases bank  
**Answer: D**

11. What should happen with an unidentified receipt?  
A. Post it to the largest customer  
B. Record it as sales automatically  
C. Investigate before allocation  
D. Ignore it permanently  
**Answer: C**

12. Why should duplicate invoice numbers be investigated?  
A. They affect only filing  
B. They prove both invoices are valid  
C. They remove approval requirements  
D. They may indicate duplicate capture or payment risk  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 4 — Maintain Journals, Ledgers and Cashbooks
13. What must always be true of a journal entry?  
A. It uses only two accounts  
B. It affects bank  
C. Total debits equal total credits  
D. It is a correction  
**Answer: C**

14. What is a subsidiary ledger?  
A. A bank reconciliation  
B. A final report  
C. A tax table  
D. A detailed set of records supporting a general-ledger control account  
**Answer: D**

15. What is the purpose of posting references?  
A. Make transactions traceable between evidence, journals and ledgers  
B. Remove approvals  
C. Increase account numbers  
D. Replace balances  
**Answer: A**

16. What should happen when a control account does not reconcile?  
A. Investigate before posting any correcting entry  
B. Delete the subsidiary ledger  
C. Force a journal to make it agree  
D. Ignore the difference  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 5 — Reconcile Bank, Debtors and Creditors
17. Why update the cashbook before bank reconciliation?  
A. Bank statements are always wrong  
B. Internal records should include known valid bank items first  
C. It removes the need for reconciliation  
D. Timing items disappear  
**Answer: B**

18. What is a reconciling item?  
A. A difference explaining why related records do not yet agree  
B. A payroll deduction  
C. A permanent error account  
D. A tax invoice  
**Answer: A**

19. What should Creditors Control agree to?  
A. Sales  
B. Bank balance  
C. Net total of supplier balances  
D. Customer balances  
**Answer: C**

20. What should happen to unresolved reconciliation differences?  
A. Hide them  
B. Write them off automatically  
C. Leave them off the reconciliation  
D. Assign ownership and follow up until resolved or escalated  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 6 — Process Period-end Adjustments
21. What is a period-end adjustment?  
A. A bank transfer only  
B. An entry updating accounts before final reporting  
C. A customer receipt  
D. A supplier payment  
**Answer: B**

22. Services were received before year-end, but the supplier invoice will arrive next month. Which treatment may be needed for the current period?  
A. A duplicate transaction  
B. A future asset purchase  
C. An accrual for the supported expense and liability  
D. A cash withdrawal  
**Answer: C**

23. Why should depreciation follow an approved policy?  
A. It replaces the asset register  
B. Useful lives and methods should not be invented by the bookkeeper  
C. Depreciation never affects reports  
D. Every asset uses the same rate  
**Answer: B**

24. What should follow approved adjustments?  
A. Immediate bank payment  
B. An adjusted trial balance and review  
C. Supplier reconciliation only  
D. Deletion of the original trial balance  
**Answer: B**

## Lesson 7 — Prepare Basic Financial Reports and Control Schedules
25. Which trial balance should be used for final reports?  
A. Any trial balance that balances  
B. The first draft  
C. Opening trial balance only  
D. The final reviewed adjusted trial balance  
**Answer: D**

26. Which report presents income, expenses and the resulting profit or loss for a period?  
A. Income, expenses and resulting profit or loss for the period  
B. Bank timing differences  
C. Customer balances only  
D. Assets and liabilities only  
**Answer: A**

27. What should the reported bank balance agree to?  
A. Equity only  
B. The reconciled bank ledger/cashbook balance  
C. Revenue  
D. Supplier balances  
**Answer: B**

28. Why perform cross-document checks?  
A. To increase page count  
B. To avoid reconciliations  
C. To remove draft labels  
D. To detect inconsistencies between reports, ledgers and supporting schedules  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 8 — Final Portfolio, Handover and Continuous-improvement Review
29. What is the purpose of a final evidence index?  
A. Store passwords  
B. Replace all actual documents  
C. Trace required outputs to supporting evidence and locations  
D. Record only completed lessons  
**Answer: C**

30. Which item belongs in a bookkeeping handover?  
A. Outstanding items, deadlines, record locations and owners  
B. Unrelated office gossip  
C. Shared passwords  
D. Personal opinions about customers  
**Answer: A**

31. What is a root cause?  
A. A bank charge  
B. The underlying reason a problem keeps occurring  
C. A ledger balance  
D. A temporary workaround  
**Answer: B**

32. When should a bookkeeper refer a matter?  
A. Only when the trial balance fails  
B. When it exceeds role authority or requires specialist tax, legal, accounting-policy or management judgement  
C. Only after guessing  
D. Never  
**Answer: B**
