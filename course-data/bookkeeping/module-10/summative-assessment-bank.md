# Module 10 Summative Assessment Bank
## Practical Bookkeeping Project

**Bank size:** 52 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 25 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Coverage:** All eight Module 10 lessons.

1. What should be established before processing the capstone transactions?  
A. Business profile, opening balances, authority limits and control framework  
B. Final profit  
C. Tax advice for real clients  
D. Supplier payment file  
**Answer: A**

2. Why does the capstone use an evidence index?  
A. To replace source documents  
B. To link outputs and transactions to supporting evidence  
C. To calculate VAT automatically  
D. To store passwords  
**Answer: B**

3. What should happen if opening debits and credits do not agree?  
A. Post a balancing suspense entry immediately  
B. Ignore the difference until month-end  
C. Investigate the approved opening evidence before processing further  
D. Change equity until the totals match  
**Answer: C**

4. Which action best respects authority limits?  
A. Approving your own unusual journal  
B. Changing supplier banking details without verification  
C. Writing off debt because it is overdue  
D. Preparing the entry and routing it to the authorised approver  
**Answer: D**

5. What is the main purpose of the chart of accounts?  
A. Provide a consistent structure for classifying and reporting transactions  
B. Replace customer records  
C. Store source-document images only  
D. Approve transactions  
**Answer: A**

6. What should Debtors Control reconcile to?  
A. Supplier balances  
B. Net total of individual customer balances  
C. Bank statement  
D. Revenue accounts  
**Answer: B**

7. Which normal balance relationship is correct?  
A. Income normally debit  
B. Liabilities normally debit  
C. Expenses normally debit  
D. Assets normally credit  
**Answer: C**

8. Why should duplicate ledger accounts be avoided?  
A. They always cause tax penalties  
B. They make reports too short  
C. They prevent bank feeds  
D. They can split similar transactions and weaken reporting consistency  
**Answer: D**

9. What should a bookkeeper determine before choosing an account?  
A. What happened economically and which accounts are affected  
B. Which code was used most recently  
C. Which option creates the highest profit  
D. Which account has the shortest name  
**Answer: A**

10. A customer pays R4,000 against an existing invoice. What is the basic effect?  
A. Increase Sales again  
B. Increase Bank and reduce Debtors  
C. Increase Creditors  
D. Increase Expense  
**Answer: B**

11. What should happen when a supplier invoice number already exists in the system?  
A. Capture it again with a new number  
B. Pay it immediately  
C. Investigate whether it is a duplicate before processing  
D. Move it to petty cash  
**Answer: C**

12. How should an unidentified bank receipt be treated?  
A. Allocate to the largest debtor  
B. Record as sales automatically  
C. Ignore it permanently  
D. Keep it as an exception and investigate before allocation  
**Answer: D**

13. What must every journal entry preserve?  
A. Equal total debits and credits  
B. A bank movement  
C. Only two accounts  
D. A supplier reference  
**Answer: A**

14. What is the role of a subsidiary ledger?  
A. Prepare tax returns  
B. Provide detailed records supporting a control account  
C. Replace the general ledger  
D. Store only invoices  
**Answer: B**

15. Why are posting references important?  
A. They increase balances  
B. They replace approval  
C. They allow entries to be traced between source evidence, journals and ledgers  
D. They prevent all errors automatically  
**Answer: C**

16. Debtors Control is R500 higher than the customer schedule. What should happen?  
A. Add R500 to a customer account  
B. Reduce sales by R500 automatically  
C. Delete the control account  
D. Investigate postings and evidence before correcting  
**Answer: D**

17. Before bank reconciliation, what should be done first?  
A. Update the cashbook for valid known bank-statement items  
B. Change the bank statement  
C. Delete unmatched items  
D. Post all differences to Bank Charges  
**Answer: A**

18. What is an outstanding deposit?  
A. A customer invoice  
B. A receipt recorded internally but not yet reflected by the bank  
C. A bank error always  
D. A supplier payment  
**Answer: B**

19. What should Creditors Control reconcile to?  
A. Total customers  
B. Revenue  
C. Net total of supplier subsidiary balances  
D. Petty cash  
**Answer: C**

20. Why must unresolved reconciliation differences remain visible?  
A. To make reports longer  
B. To avoid adjusting anything  
C. To delay month-end  
D. So they have ownership, follow-up and escalation until resolved  
**Answer: D**

21. What is an accrual in the project context?  
A. Recognition of an item relating to the current period before settlement where applicable  
B. A customer overpayment  
C. A duplicate invoice  
D. An asset purchase only  
**Answer: A**

22. What is a prepayment?  
A. A payment made late  
B. A payment relating partly or wholly to a future period  
C. A debtor write-off  
D. A bank error  
**Answer: B**

23. Why should depreciation use the approved asset policy?  
A. Every asset uses the same rate  
B. The bookkeeper chooses the most convenient rate  
C. The method and useful-life assumptions must be authorised and supportable  
D. Depreciation does not affect reports  
**Answer: C**

24. Which statement about bad-debt write-offs is correct?  
A. Every overdue debtor should be written off  
B. The bookkeeper may decide alone  
C. Write-offs need no evidence  
D. They should follow the authorised write-off process and supporting evidence  
**Answer: D**

25. Which trial balance should feed the final reports?  
A. The final reviewed adjusted trial balance  
B. Opening trial balance  
C. First draft before corrections  
D. Any balanced trial balance  
**Answer: A**

26. What does the statement of profit or loss report?  
A. Only assets  
B. Income, expenses and resulting profit or loss for the period  
C. Only bank movements  
D. Only outstanding invoices  
**Answer: B**

27. What should the reported Debtors balance agree to?  
A. Supplier statement total  
B. Bank statement  
C. Reconciled Debtors Control and customer schedule  
D. Revenue only  
**Answer: C**

28. What does cross-document consistency mean?  
A. Every document has the same length  
B. Every report uses the same font  
C. All files are PDFs  
D. Related records agree on entity, period, references, balances and status  
**Answer: D**

29. What is the purpose of a control schedule?  
A. Provide supporting detail that explains a ledger or report balance  
B. Replace source evidence  
C. Set employee salaries  
D. Approve tax treatment  
**Answer: A**

30. Which result most strongly needs investigation before final reporting?  
A. Debtors agrees to customer schedule  
B. An unexplained material suspense or unusual balance  
C. Bank reconciliation agrees  
D. Trial-balance totals agree  
**Answer: B**

31. Why should final reports be marked draft before approval?  
A. To reduce accuracy  
B. To avoid reconciliation  
C. To prevent unfinished information being mistaken for final authorised reporting  
D. To avoid management review  
**Answer: C**

32. What should a management summary avoid?  
A. Explaining material exceptions  
B. Referring to supporting schedules  
C. Stating factual movements  
D. Unsupported claims or hidden unfavourable information  
**Answer: D**

33. What should the final evidence index demonstrate?  
A. Each required output is traceable to its supporting evidence and location  
B. Every source document has been deleted  
C. Passwords are available to the assessor  
D. Only final reports matter  
**Answer: A**

34. Which handover item is most useful?  
A. “Check accounts.”  
B. “Supplier X reconciliation has R1,250 unresolved; owner: Accounts Admin; supplier response due Tuesday.”  
C. “Busy month.”  
D. “See emails.”  
**Answer: B**

35. What is a root cause?  
A. A temporary workaround  
B. A ledger balance  
C. The underlying reason a recurring problem occurs  
D. A tax invoice  
**Answer: C**

36. What should a continuous-improvement plan contain?  
A. Only complaints  
B. Only deadlines  
C. Only manager names  
D. Problem, likely cause, proposed control, owner, target and success measure  
**Answer: D**

37. Why should passwords not appear in handover documents?  
A. Access should be transferred through authorised security processes  
B. Passwords are not needed for systems  
C. Handover never includes access information  
D. Passwords should instead be public  
**Answer: A**

38. What should happen if the financial-position Debtors figure differs from the reconciled debtor schedule?  
A. Use the larger amount  
B. Investigate and correct the underlying inconsistency before final release  
C. Average the balances  
D. Ignore it if profit is correct  
**Answer: B**

39. Why should exception patterns be reviewed?  
A. To identify employees to blame  
B. To create more journals  
C. Repeated exceptions can reveal process weaknesses suitable for improvement  
D. To remove approval controls  
**Answer: C**

40. When should the bookkeeper escalate specialist issues?  
A. Never  
B. Only after making a guess  
C. Only after year-end  
D. When tax, legal, accounting-policy or management judgement exceeds the role's authority  
**Answer: D**

41. Opening Bank is R30,000 Dr, Equipment R20,000 Dr, Debtors R5,000 Dr, Creditors R10,000 Cr and Equity R45,000 Cr. Do the opening balances agree?  
A. Yes, debits and credits both total R55,000  
B. No, debits are R50,000  
C. No, credits are R60,000  
D. Only if VAT is added  
**Answer: A**

42. A supplier invoice of R2,300 is captured on credit. Ignoring VAT, which entry is generally appropriate?  
A. Dr Creditors / Cr Expense  
B. Dr Expense or Asset / Cr Creditors  
C. Dr Bank / Cr Sales  
D. Dr Debtors / Cr Bank  
**Answer: B**

43. A supplier invoice was already recorded and is now paid. What should happen?  
A. Expense should be recorded again  
B. Revenue should increase  
C. Creditors and Bank should decrease through the payment entry  
D. Debtors should increase  
**Answer: C**

44. A bank charge of R115 appears on the statement but not the cashbook. What is the appropriate project response?  
A. Ignore it  
B. Treat it as a timing difference forever  
C. Add it to sales  
D. Capture the supported bank charge in the cashbook before reconciliation  
**Answer: D**

45. Customer accounts total R12,400 but Debtors Control is R13,100. What is the difference?  
A. R700  
B. R500  
C. R1,700  
D. R25,500  
**Answer: A**

46. Updated cashbook is R42,300 and bank statement R40,800. Outstanding deposit is R2,000 and unpresented payment R500. What reconciled amount results from the statement?  
A. R40,300  
B. R42,300  
C. R43,300  
D. R38,300  
**Answer: B**

47. Equipment cost is R24,000 and approved depreciation for the period is R4,800. Which credit is appropriate?  
A. Bank R4,800  
B. Equipment R24,000  
C. Accumulated Depreciation R4,800  
D. Sales R4,800  
**Answer: C**

48. Revenue is R110,000 and expenses are R78,000. What is profit?  
A. R188,000  
B. R78,000  
C. R110,000  
D. R32,000  
**Answer: D**

49. What does a balanced trial balance prove?  
A. Debit and credit arithmetic agrees, but not necessarily that every classification is correct  
B. Every source document is valid  
C. Bank reconciliation is complete  
D. No fraud can exist  
**Answer: A**

50. Which is the best response to a known classification error that still leaves the trial balance balanced?  
A. Ignore it because totals agree  
B. Correct it through the authorised traceable process  
C. Delete both accounts  
D. Change the bank balance  
**Answer: B**

51. Which outcome best demonstrates capstone readiness?  
A. Many documents with no references  
B. A balanced trial balance with unresolved control differences hidden  
C. Traceable evidence, reconciled controls, supported adjustments and consistent reports  
D. Fast capture without review  
**Answer: C**

52. What is the strongest principle across the full practical project?  
A. Speed before accuracy  
B. Software before evidence  
C. Memory before records  
D. Accuracy, evidence, authorisation, reconciliation and traceability  
**Answer: D**
