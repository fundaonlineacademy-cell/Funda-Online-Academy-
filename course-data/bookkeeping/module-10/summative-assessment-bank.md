# Module 10 Summative Assessment Bank
## Practical Bookkeeping Project

**Bank size:** 52 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 25 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Coverage:** All eight Module 10 lessons.

1. What should be established before processing the capstone transactions?  
A. Business profile, opening balances, authority limits and control framework  
B. Final profit  
C. Tax advice for real clients  
D. Supplier payment file  
**Answer: A**

2. Why does the capstone use an evidence index?  
A. To store passwords  
B. To replace source documents  
C. To calculate VAT automatically  
D. To link outputs and transactions to supporting evidence  
**Answer: D**

3. What should happen if opening debits and credits do not agree?  
A. Change equity until the totals match  
B. Ignore the difference until month-end  
C. Investigate the approved opening evidence before processing further  
D. Post a balancing suspense entry immediately  
**Answer: C**

4. Which action best respects authority limits?  
A. Preparing the entry and routing it to the authorised approver  
B. Changing supplier banking details without verification  
C. Writing off debt because it is overdue  
D. Approving your own unusual journal  
**Answer: A**

5. Which structure supports consistent classification and reporting of transactions?  
A. Replace customer records  
B. Approve transactions  
C. Provide a consistent structure for classifying and reporting transactions  
D. Store source-document images only  
**Answer: C**

6. What should Debtors Control reconcile to?  
A. Revenue accounts  
B. Supplier balances  
C. Net total of individual customer balances  
D. Bank statement  
**Answer: C**

7. Which normal balance relationship is correct?  
A. Expenses normally debit  
B. Assets normally credit  
C. Income normally debit  
D. Liabilities normally debit  
**Answer: A**

8. Why should duplicate ledger accounts be avoided?  
A. They prevent bank feeds  
B. They make reports too short  
C. They can split similar transactions and weaken reporting consistency  
D. They always cause tax penalties  
**Answer: C**

9. What should a bookkeeper determine before choosing an account?  
A. What happened economically and which accounts are affected  
B. Which account has the shortest name  
C. Which option creates the highest profit  
D. Which code was used most recently  
**Answer: A**

10. A customer pays R4,000 against an existing invoice. What is the basic effect?  
A. Increase Bank and reduce Debtors  
B. Increase Sales again  
C. Increase Creditors  
D. Increase Expense  
**Answer: A**

11. What should happen when a supplier invoice number already exists in the system?  
A. Capture it again with a new number  
B. Pay it immediately  
C. Investigate whether it is a duplicate before processing  
D. Move it to petty cash  
**Answer: C**

12. How should an unidentified bank receipt be treated?  
A. Ignore it permanently  
B. Keep it as an exception and investigate before allocation  
C. Record as sales automatically  
D. Allocate to the largest debtor  
**Answer: B**

13. What must every journal entry preserve?  
A. A bank movement  
B. Equal total debits and credits  
C. Only two accounts  
D. A supplier reference  
**Answer: B**

14. What is the role of a subsidiary ledger?  
A. Provide detailed records supporting a control account  
B. Prepare tax returns  
C. Replace the general ledger  
D. Store only invoices  
**Answer: A**

15. Why are posting references important?  
A. They replace approval  
B. They allow entries to be traced between source evidence, journals and ledgers  
C. They prevent all errors automatically  
D. They increase balances  
**Answer: B**

16. Debtors Control is R500 higher than the customer schedule. What should happen?  
A. Add R500 to a customer account  
B. Delete the control account  
C. Reduce sales by R500 automatically  
D. Investigate postings and evidence before correcting  
**Answer: D**

17. Before bank reconciliation, what should be done first?  
A. Post all differences to Bank Charges  
B. Change the bank statement  
C. Update the cashbook for valid known bank-statement items  
D. Delete unmatched items  
**Answer: C**

18. What is an outstanding deposit?  
A. A customer invoice  
B. A supplier payment  
C. A bank error always  
D. A receipt recorded internally but not yet reflected by the bank  
**Answer: D**

19. What should Creditors Control reconcile to?  
A. Revenue  
B. Total customers  
C. Petty cash  
D. Net total of supplier subsidiary balances  
**Answer: D**

20. Why must unresolved reconciliation differences remain visible?  
A. To avoid adjusting anything  
B. So they have ownership, follow-up and escalation until resolved  
C. To delay month-end  
D. To make reports longer  
**Answer: B**

21. What is an accrual in the project context?  
A. Recognition of an item relating to the current period before settlement where applicable  
B. An asset purchase only  
C. A customer overpayment  
D. A duplicate invoice  
**Answer: A**

22. At year-end, what is the portion of a payment that relates to a future period called?  
A. A payment relating partly or wholly to a future period  
B. A payment made late  
C. A bank error  
D. A debtor write-off  
**Answer: A**

23. Why should depreciation use the approved asset policy?  
A. Every asset uses the same rate  
B. The method and useful-life assumptions must be authorised and supportable  
C. The bookkeeper chooses the most convenient rate  
D. Depreciation does not affect reports  
**Answer: B**

24. Which statement about bad-debt write-offs is correct?  
A. Write-offs need no evidence  
B. They should follow the authorised write-off process and supporting evidence  
C. Every overdue debtor should be written off  
D. The bookkeeper may decide alone  
**Answer: B**

25. Which trial balance should feed the final reports?  
A. The final reviewed adjusted trial balance  
B. First draft before corrections  
C. Any balanced trial balance  
D. Opening trial balance  
**Answer: A**

26. A business reports revenue of R300,000 and expenses of R240,000 for the period. What result appears in the statement of profit or loss?  
A. Bank receipts of R300,000  
B. Assets of R540,000  
C. Outstanding invoices of R240,000  
D. Profit of R60,000  
**Answer: D**

27. What should the reported Debtors balance agree to?  
A. Supplier statement total  
B. Bank statement  
C. Revenue only  
D. Reconciled Debtors Control and customer schedule  
**Answer: D**

28. What does cross-document consistency mean?  
A. Related records agree on entity, period, references, balances and status  
B. All files are PDFs  
C. Every report uses the same font  
D. Every document has the same length  
**Answer: A**

29. What is the purpose of a control schedule?  
A. Set employee salaries  
B. Provide supporting detail that explains a ledger or report balance  
C. Approve tax treatment  
D. Replace source evidence  
**Answer: B**

30. Which result most strongly needs investigation before final reporting?  
A. Bank reconciliation agrees  
B. Debtors agrees to customer schedule  
C. An unexplained material suspense or unusual balance  
D. Trial-balance totals agree  
**Answer: C**

31. Why should final reports be marked draft before approval?  
A. To reduce accuracy  
B. To avoid management review  
C. To avoid reconciliation  
D. To prevent unfinished information being mistaken for final authorised reporting  
**Answer: D**

32. What should a management summary avoid?  
A. Referring to supporting schedules  
B. Stating factual movements  
C. Unsupported claims or hidden unfavourable information  
D. Explaining material exceptions  
**Answer: C**

33. What should the final evidence index demonstrate?  
A. Every source document has been deleted  
B. Only final reports matter  
C. Passwords are available to the assessor  
D. Each required output is traceable to its supporting evidence and location  
**Answer: D**

34. Which handover item is most useful?  
A. “Check accounts.”  
B. “See emails.”  
C. “Supplier X reconciliation has R1,250 unresolved; owner: Accounts Admin; supplier response due Tuesday.”  
D. “Busy month.”  
**Answer: C**

35. A reconciliation difference recurs every month. What term describes the underlying reason it keeps happening?  
A. A ledger balance  
B. The underlying reason a recurring problem occurs  
C. A tax invoice  
D. A temporary workaround  
**Answer: B**

36. What should a continuous-improvement plan contain?  
A. Only manager names  
B. Problem, likely cause, proposed control, owner, target and success measure  
C. Only complaints  
D. Only deadlines  
**Answer: B**

37. Why should passwords not appear in handover documents?  
A. Passwords are not needed for systems  
B. Handover never includes access information  
C. Access should be transferred through authorised security processes  
D. Passwords should instead be public  
**Answer: C**

38. What should happen if the financial-position Debtors figure differs from the reconciled debtor schedule?  
A. Average the balances  
B. Ignore it if profit is correct  
C. Use the larger amount  
D. Investigate and correct the underlying inconsistency before final release  
**Answer: D**

39. Why should exception patterns be reviewed?  
A. To create more journals  
B. To remove approval controls  
C. To identify employees to blame  
D. Repeated exceptions can reveal process weaknesses suitable for improvement  
**Answer: D**

40. When should the bookkeeper escalate specialist issues?  
A. Only after year-end  
B. When tax, legal, accounting-policy or management judgement exceeds the role's authority  
C. Only after making a guess  
D. Never  
**Answer: B**

41. Opening Bank is R30,000 Dr, Equipment R20,000 Dr, Debtors R5,000 Dr, Creditors R10,000 Cr and Equity R45,000 Cr. Do the opening balances agree?  
A. Yes, debits and credits both total R55,000  
B. No, credits are R60,000  
C. No, debits are R50,000  
D. Only if VAT is added  
**Answer: A**

42. A supplier invoice of R2,300 is captured on credit. Ignoring VAT, which entry is generally appropriate?  
A. Dr Debtors / Cr Bank  
B. Dr Creditors / Cr Expense  
C. Dr Expense or Asset / Cr Creditors  
D. Dr Bank / Cr Sales  
**Answer: C**

43. A supplier invoice was already recorded and is now paid. What should happen?  
A. Debtors should increase  
B. Revenue should increase  
C. Expense should be recorded again  
D. Creditors and Bank should decrease through the payment entry  
**Answer: D**

44. A bank charge of R115 appears on the statement but not the cashbook. What is the appropriate project response?  
A. Ignore it  
B. Add it to sales  
C. Capture the supported bank charge in the cashbook before reconciliation  
D. Treat it as a timing difference forever  
**Answer: C**

45. Customer accounts total R12,400 but Debtors Control is R13,100. What is the difference?  
A. R700  
B. R25,500  
C. R500  
D. R1,700  
**Answer: A**

46. Updated cashbook is R42,300 and bank statement R40,800. Outstanding deposit is R2,000 and unpresented payment R500. What reconciled amount results from the statement?  
A. R38,300  
B. R42,300  
C. R43,300  
D. R40,300  
**Answer: B**

47. Equipment cost is R24,000 and approved depreciation for the period is R4,800. Which credit is appropriate?  
A. Sales R4,800  
B. Accumulated Depreciation R4,800  
C. Bank R4,800  
D. Equipment R24,000  
**Answer: B**

48. Revenue is R110,000 and expenses are R78,000. What is profit?  
A. R110,000  
B. R78,000  
C. R188,000  
D. R32,000  
**Answer: D**

49. What does a balanced trial balance prove?  
A. No fraud can exist  
B. Debit and credit arithmetic agrees, but not necessarily that every classification is correct  
C. Bank reconciliation is complete  
D. Every source document is valid  
**Answer: B**

50. Which is the best response to a known classification error that still leaves the trial balance balanced?  
A. Delete both accounts  
B. Change the bank balance  
C. Ignore it because totals agree  
D. Correct it through the authorised traceable process  
**Answer: D**

51. Which outcome best demonstrates capstone readiness?  
A. Traceable evidence, reconciled controls, supported adjustments and consistent reports  
B. Fast capture without review  
C. Many documents with no references  
D. A balanced trial balance with unresolved control differences hidden  
**Answer: A**

52. What is the strongest principle across the full practical project?  
A. Memory before records  
B. Speed before accuracy  
C. Accuracy, evidence, authorisation, reconciliation and traceability  
D. Software before evidence  
**Answer: C**
