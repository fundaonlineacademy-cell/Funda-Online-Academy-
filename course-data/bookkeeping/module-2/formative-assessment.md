# Module 2 Formative Assessment Bank
## Source Documents & Transaction Analysis

**Bank size:** 32 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 15 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Prerequisite:** Complete all eight Module 2 lessons.

## Lesson 1 — Source Documents and Why They Matter

1. What is the main purpose of a source document?  
A. Replace the general ledger  
B. Provide evidence supporting a business transaction  
C. Approve every transaction automatically  
D. Show only cash movements  
**Answer: B**

2. Why can one supplier payment require several supporting documents?  
A. Every payment must have exactly six documents  
B. Bank evidence is never useful  
C. Different documents may prove approval, receipt, billing and payment stages  
D. Bookkeepers should duplicate evidence  
**Answer: C**

3. A duplicate invoice-number warning appears. What should the bookkeeper do?  
A. Override it immediately  
B. Capture the invoice under a different supplier  
C. Change the invoice number  
D. Investigate whether the transaction is already recorded  
**Answer: D**

4. What should happen when a required receipt is missing?  
A. Follow the authorised missing-document process rather than fabricate evidence  
B. Create a replacement receipt personally  
C. Delete the transaction  
D. Post it to Miscellaneous automatically  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 2 — Sales Invoices, Credit Notes, Receipts & Customer Documents

5. A credit sale normally increases which balance?  
A. Creditors  
B. Debtors / accounts receivable  
C. Loan payable  
D. Drawings  
**Answer: B**

6. What is the purpose of a sales credit note?  
A. Record a customer payment  
B. Create a new sale  
C. Reduce or reverse an authorised amount previously billed  
D. Increase the debtor balance  
**Answer: C**

7. A customer pays an invoice that was already recorded. How should the receipt be treated?  
A. As new sales income again  
B. As a supplier payment  
C. As owner capital  
D. As settlement of the debtor balance  
**Answer: D**

8. What should happen with an unidentified customer deposit?  
A. Investigate before allocating it  
B. Allocate it to the largest customer  
C. Post it to sales automatically  
D. Ignore it permanently  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 3 — Supplier Invoices, Debit Notes & Purchase Documents

9. A valid supplier invoice on credit normally increases:  
A. Debtors  
B. Creditors / accounts payable  
C. Sales income  
D. Owner capital  
**Answer: B**

10. What is three-way matching?  
A. Comparing three bank accounts  
B. Comparing three supplier statements  
C. Comparing purchase order, receipt evidence and supplier invoice  
D. Capturing one invoice three times  
**Answer: C**

11. Why should a supplier credit note be allocated correctly?  
A. It always creates new income  
B. It removes the need for invoices  
C. It increases the amount owed  
D. It should reduce the correct supplier obligation and related original transaction  
**Answer: D**

12. What is a supplier statement mainly used for?  
A. Reconciling the supplier's account with the business's creditor records  
B. Replacing all supplier invoices  
C. Approving purchases automatically  
D. Setting supplier bank details  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 4 — Cash, EFT, Card & Petty-cash Evidence

13. What does an EFT confirmation prove most directly?  
A. The purchase was authorised  
B. An electronic payment movement with details such as amount and beneficiary  
C. The expense account is correct  
D. VAT is deductible  
**Answer: B**

14. Why is an itemised supplier receipt useful for a company-card purchase?  
A. It proves the card password  
B. It replaces the bank statement  
C. It explains what was actually purchased for classification and control  
D. It guarantees the purchase was approved  
**Answer: C**

15. A petty-cash float is R2,000; cash remaining is R650 and valid vouchers total R1,300. What should happen?  
A. Replenish R1,350 without checking  
B. Ignore the difference  
C. Increase the float  
D. Investigate the unexplained R50 shortage  
**Answer: D**

16. Customer card sales are R10,000 but the bank receives R9,750 because of merchant fees. What is the BEST basic approach?  
A. Preserve gross sales and account separately for the merchant fee  
B. Record sales as R9,750 automatically  
C. Ignore the fee  
D. Add R250 to debtors  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 5 — Analysing Transactions into Accounts

17. Before choosing an account, what should the bookkeeper determine first?  
A. Which code was used last month regardless of facts  
B. What happened economically and which accounts are affected  
C. Which account has the shortest name  
D. Whether cash moved  
**Answer: B**

18. A supplier invoice was already captured, and the business now pays it. Which account is normally debited?  
A. Expense again  
B. Sales  
C. Creditors  
D. Debtors  
**Answer: C**

19. A business receives a loan. Which account normally increases with a credit?  
A. Bank  
B. Expense  
C. Debtors  
D. Loan Liability  
**Answer: D**

20. Why should a vague bank description not determine the account by itself?  
A. It may not explain the true business purpose of the transaction  
B. Bank statements are never reliable  
C. Bank entries do not affect bookkeeping  
D. Every bank payment is an expense  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 6 — VAT-Inclusive and VAT-Exclusive Transaction Analysis

21. At a 15% VAT rate, what is the VAT on R2,000 exclusive?  
A. R150  
B. R300  
C. R230  
D. R2,300  
**Answer: B**

22. At 15%, how do you extract VAT from an inclusive amount?  
A. Inclusive × 15%  
B. Inclusive ÷ 15  
C. Inclusive × 15/115  
D. Inclusive × 115/15  
**Answer: C**

23. Which statement is correct?  
A. Zero-rated and exempt always mean exactly the same thing  
B. Every purchase VAT amount is deductible  
C. Any business may charge VAT  
D. Zero-rated and exempt are different VAT categories  
**Answer: D**

24. What should a bookkeeper do when VAT treatment is unclear?  
A. Use approved tax guidance or refer to the authorised specialist  
B. Always use standard VAT  
C. Choose the code used most often  
D. Remove VAT from the transaction  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 7 — Document Filing, Audit Trails, POPIA & Record Control

25. What should a good financial filing system make possible?  
A. Access by every employee  
B. Reliable retrieval of the correct source evidence by authorised users  
C. Permanent retention of every duplicate  
D. Use of personal cloud accounts  
**Answer: B**

26. What does need-to-know access mean?  
A. Everyone in Finance receives full access  
B. Only managers can see documents  
C. Access is limited to people with a legitimate work requirement  
D. No documents may be shared  
**Answer: C**

27. Why should a bookkeeper not invent a retention period?  
A. Records never need disposal  
B. Retention is only for paper  
C. Every record has the same period  
D. Requirements can differ by record type, law, policy and holds  
**Answer: D**

28. Why are personal cloud accounts risky for finance records?  
A. They may fall outside organisational ownership, security and retention controls  
B. Cloud files cannot be opened  
C. Personal accounts always delete files  
D. They automatically publish information  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 8 — Source-document Practical Workshop

29. What should a document-batch checklist do?  
A. Maximise capture speed only  
B. Standardise verification, analysis, capture, exception handling and filing  
C. Remove the need for source evidence  
D. Eliminate all judgement  
**Answer: B**

30. Two PDF invoices have identical supplier, invoice number, date and amount. What should happen first?  
A. Capture both  
B. Rename the second one  
C. Check whether they are duplicate copies of the same transaction  
D. Pay both and reconcile later  
**Answer: C**

31. An unexplained R4,000 bank deposit appears. What is the safest accounting response?  
A. Record it as Sales  
B. Record it as Owner Capital  
C. Ignore it  
D. Investigate the source and transaction nature before classification  
**Answer: D**

32. What is the purpose of an exception register?  
A. Keep unresolved transactions visible with ownership and next actions  
B. Hide difficult transactions  
C. Replace the ledger  
D. Approve missing documents automatically  
**Answer: A**
