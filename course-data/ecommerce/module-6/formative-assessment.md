## Lesson 6 — Paid Advertising, Budgets & ROAS

### Q21
If an ad has 10,000 impressions and 200 clicks, what is CTR?
A. 0.2%
B. 2%
C. 20%
D. 50%

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** 200 ÷ 10,000 × 100 = 2%.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 6

### Q22
If advertising spend is R1,000 and produces 250 clicks, CPC is:
A. R2
B. R4
C. R25
D. R250

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** R1,000 ÷ 250 = R4.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 6

### Q23
What does ROAS compare?
A. Attributed revenue with advertising spend
B. Product weight with delivery cost
C. Refunds with stock
D. Likes with comments

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** ROAS evaluates advertising revenue relative to spend.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 6

### Q24
Why is ROAS not the same as profit?
A. Other costs still reduce profit
B. ROAS always equals margin
C. Advertising has no cost
D. Revenue and profit are identical

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Product and operating costs still matter.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 6
