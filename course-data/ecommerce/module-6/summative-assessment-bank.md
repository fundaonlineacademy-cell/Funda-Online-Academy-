## Questions 1–6 — Lesson 1: Digital Marketing Strategy & Customer Journey

### Q1
A store wants to launch a new product. What should it define before selecting channels?
A. Campaign objective
B. Courier vehicle
C. Warehouse shelf
D. Refund reference

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Channel and measurement choices should follow the business objective.

### Q2
Which stage is most focused on product comparison and evaluation?
A. Awareness
B. Consideration
C. Fulfilment
D. Reconciliation

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Consideration is where customers assess whether the offer suits them.

### Q3
Which activity is most likely to support conversion?
A. A relevant product landing page with a clear CTA
B. A random post unrelated to the offer
C. Warehouse counting
D. Courier reconciliation

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Conversion tactics help customers complete a desired action.

### Q4
What is retention marketing intended to do?
A. Encourage existing customers to return
B. Replace product listings
C. Change tax settings
D. Increase parcel weight

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Retention focuses on ongoing customer relationships.

### Q5
Why can a customer journey involve several channels?
A. Customers may discover, research and buy through different touchpoints
B. Every customer uses one channel only
C. Channels replace customer needs
D. Journey stages are always linear

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Real purchase journeys often involve multiple interactions.

### Q6
Which metric best fits a sales objective?
A. Completed purchases or revenue
B. Office attendance
C. Number of warehouse shelves
D. Product weight

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** KPIs should reflect the intended commercial outcome.

## Questions 7–12 — Lesson 2: Brand Messaging, Content & Promotional Copy

### Q7
Which statement is the best value proposition?
A. “We are amazing.”
B. “Insulated lunch bags designed to keep packed meals organised during daily commuting.”
C. “BUY NOW!!!”
D. “Best ever guaranteed.”

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** It communicates a specific customer benefit without unsupported exaggeration.

### Q8
Why is a claim such as “last 5 remaining” risky if stock is actually unlimited?
A. It creates false scarcity
B. It improves data accuracy
C. It changes the SKU
D. It reduces delivery cost

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Scarcity claims should reflect reality.

### Q9
What is a CTA?
A. A prompt asking the customer to take a next step
B. A stock code
C. A courier rule
D. A refund policy

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** CTAs direct customers toward actions such as shopping or learning more.

### Q10
Why use content pillars?
A. To organise recurring themes and reduce random posting
B. To eliminate creativity
C. To calculate VAT
D. To replace product categories

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Pillars create a repeatable content structure.

### Q11
A paid advert shows a discounted price that expired yesterday. What should happen?
A. Correct or stop the advert
B. Keep it running for traffic
C. Hide the real price until checkout
D. Increase the discount claim

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Marketing should match the current live offer.

### Q12
What should remain consistent when content is adapted to different formats?
A. The core facts and brand message
B. The exact character count
C. The image size only
D. The number of hashtags

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Format may change while the truth of the offer remains consistent.

## Questions 25–32 — Lesson 5: Email, SMS & South African Direct Marketing

### Q25
Which message is transactional rather than promotional?
A. “Your order #451 has shipped.”
B. “Buy our summer sale now.”
C. “20% off tonight.”
D. “New arrivals just launched.”

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** The message services an existing transaction rather than promoting a new sale.

### Q26
POPIA section 69 generally allows electronic direct marketing when:
A. There is consent or qualifying existing-customer circumstances
B. The contact was scraped from the internet
C. The customer never interacted with the business
D. The sender hides its identity

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** POPIA defines specific conditions for electronic direct marketing.

### Q27
For qualifying existing-customer marketing under POPIA section 69, marketing should concern:
A. The responsible party's own similar products or services
B. Any unrelated third-party product automatically
C. Political content only
D. No product at all

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** The existing-customer exception is limited by the Act.

### Q28
What should happen when a person opts out?
A. The opt-out should be respected across marketing systems
B. The address should be re-added next week
C. The person should be charged a fee
D. The person should receive more messages

### Q29
Why should sender identity be clear in direct marketing?
A. Recipients should know who is contacting them

C. It replaces consent
D. It changes product stock

**Correct answer:** A

### Q31
Why should the business know how a marketing contact was obtained?

C. It replaces customer consent automatically
D. It guarantees sales

### Q32
Which approach is strongest for electronic direct marketing?

D. Continue messaging after objection

## Questions 33–38 — Lesson 6: Paid Advertising & Budgets

### Q33
An ad receives 25,000 impressions and 500 clicks. CTR is:
A. 0.5%
B. 2%
C. 5%
D. 20%

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** 500 ÷ 25,000 × 100 = 2%.

### Q34
Spend is R4,000 and clicks are 800. CPC is:
A. R2
B. R4
C. R5
D. R20

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** R4,000 ÷ 800 = R5.

### Q35
Spend is R6,000 and 60 purchases are attributed. CPA is:
A. R10
B. R60
C. R100
D. R360

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** R6,000 ÷ 60 = R100.

### Q36
Spend is R5,000 and attributed revenue is R20,000. ROAS is:
A. 0.25
B. 2
C. 4
D. 20

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** R20,000 ÷ R5,000 = 4.

### Q37
Why can a campaign with good ROAS still be unprofitable?
A. Product and operational costs may be high
B. ROAS includes every business cost automatically
C. Revenue equals profit
D. Advertising spend is free

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Profitability requires broader cost analysis.

### Q38
Why should teams avoid changing audience, creative and landing page simultaneously during a test?
A. It becomes difficult to identify what caused the result
B. It always improves results
C. It reduces tracking accuracy to zero
D. It changes tax treatment

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Controlled tests improve learning.

## Questions 51–52 — Integrated Scenarios

### Q51
A store buys an unknown email list, sends a “48-hour sale” with no opt-out, and advertises a fake previous price. Which response is best?
A. Continue because sales are the objective
B. Stop and correct the campaign, review the lawful basis for contacts, provide compliant opt-out and sender information, and use genuine pricing claims
C. Send more messages
D. Hide the sender identity

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Responsible marketing combines privacy, consumer rights and truthful promotion.

### Q52
A campaign gets strong social engagement and 4% CTR but weak purchases. Analytics shows mobile checkout completion is far below desktop. What is the best next step?
A. Investigate and test mobile checkout usability before increasing ad spend
B. Assume the audience is wrong
C. Stop measuring conversions
D. Increase the ad budget immediately

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Funnel and device segmentation identify likely post-click friction.
