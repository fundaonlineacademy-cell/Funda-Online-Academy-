## Questions 1–6 — Lesson 1: Product Information, SKUs, Variants & Catalogue Data

### Q1
What is the primary purpose of a SKU in an e-commerce operation?
A. To provide a customer review score
B. To identify a specific sellable item or variant internally
C. To calculate a website conversion rate
D. To replace the product description

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** A SKU is an internal identifier used to distinguish and manage specific sellable items or variants.

### Q2
A T-shirt is sold in 3 colours and 4 sizes, and every combination is stocked separately. How many separately stocked variants exist?
A. 7
B. 10
C. 12
D. 16

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** Three colours multiplied by four sizes gives 12 sellable combinations.

### Q3
Which item is best classified as a specification rather than an attribute?
A. Brand
B. Colour
C. 1.8 kg weight
D. Style

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** A specification is a precise measurable or technical detail.

### Q4
A supplier spreadsheet lists a container as 20 L, while the current manufacturer specification lists it as 18 L. What should catalogue staff do?
A. Publish 20 L because it is larger
B. Average the two figures
C. Resolve the discrepancy using the approved source of truth before publishing
D. Omit all product information

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** Conflicting material product information should be verified rather than guessed.

### Q5
Why is consistent product-data terminology important?
A. It makes every product the same price
B. It improves filtering, reporting, integrations and customer understanding
C. It removes the need for stock management
D. It guarantees every visitor will buy

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Consistent values and units make catalogue data usable across customer-facing and operational systems.

### Q6
Which pre-publication issue presents the greatest immediate operational risk?
A. Two headings use slightly different font sizes
B. A variant SKU is linked to the wrong stock item
C. A product image has extra white space
D. A category contains only three products

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** An incorrect SKU-to-stock relationship can cause overselling, incorrect picking and fulfilment errors.

## Questions 7–12 — Lesson 2: Product Titles & Descriptions

### Q7
Which product title is most useful to a customer?
A. Amazing Deal!!!
B. Product 405
C. Women's Cotton Crew-Neck T-Shirt — Black
D. Best Shirt in South Africa Guaranteed

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** It identifies the product and meaningful characteristics clearly without unsupported promotional claims.

### Q8
What is the best distinction between a feature and a benefit?
A. A feature describes a characteristic; a benefit explains why that characteristic matters to the customer
B. A feature is always a price; a benefit is always a discount
C. A feature is an image; a benefit is a SKU
D. There is no meaningful difference

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Effective product copy can connect factual characteristics with relevant customer value.

### Q9
A seller cannot verify that a jacket is waterproof. What is the best action?
A. Call it waterproof because customers prefer that term
B. Publish the claim and correct it only if someone complains
C. Verify the claim before presenting it as fact
D. Replace “waterproof” with “100% waterproof guaranteed”

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** Material claims should be supported by reliable information before publication.

### Q10
Why should important product information not be hidden inside exaggerated marketing language?
A. Customers need clear information to make informed decisions
B. Search engines prohibit all descriptive language
C. Product descriptions may contain only numbers
D. Marketing language automatically changes the selling price

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Product copy should make material information easy to identify and understand.

### Q11
Which description practice is most appropriate for a technical product?
A. Replace specifications with emotional adjectives
B. Present verified specifications in a readable, structured way
C. Remove compatibility information
D. Copy claims from an unrelated competitor

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Technical products require accurate, structured information that supports comparison and correct selection.

### Q12
A product title says “Leather Office Chair,” but the verified material is synthetic PU. What should happen?
A. Keep “leather” because it sounds premium
B. Correct the title and description to reflect the verified material
C. Remove the material from internal records only
D. Increase the price so the claim becomes acceptable

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Customer-facing product information should match the actual product supplied.

### Q13
What is the main role of product images in e-commerce?
A. To replace accurate product information completely
B. To help customers inspect and understand what is being offered
C. To hide product limitations
D. To guarantee conversion

**Correct answer:** B

A. One distant image only
B. Multiple clear views including important details and relevant scale/context
C. Ten unrelated lifestyle images
D. A competitor's images with its logo removed

Why should variant images correspond to the selected variant where practical?

C. To remove the need for a product title

**Rationale:** Variant-specific presentation helps align customer expectations with the item selected.

### Q16
A retailer digitally enhances a product photo so the item appears to include an accessory that is not supplied. What is the key problem?
A. The image file may be too small
B. The presentation can create a misleading impression about the offer
C. The product will have too many variants

**Rationale:** Product media should accurately represent what is included or clearly distinguish illustrative content.

### Q17
What is a good mobile-commerce image practice?
A. Test whether important product details remain visible on smaller screens
B. Upload only very wide desktop banners
C. Put all specifications inside tiny image text

### Q18
Which accessibility practice best supports users who cannot see a product image?
A. Use meaningful alternative text where appropriate

## Questions 19–24 — Lesson 4: Categories, Collections, Tags, Filters & Navigation

### Q19
What is the main purpose of a product category?
A. To group related products in a structure customers can understand
B. To assign payment-card numbers
C. To replace product inventory
D. To calculate VAT automatically

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Categories support organised browsing and product discovery.

### Q20
Which catalogue structure is most customer-friendly?
A. Categories based only on internal supplier codes
B. Clear categories using language customers recognise
C. One category containing every product
D. A different category for every individual SKU

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Customer-facing navigation should reflect understandable shopping concepts.

### Q21
What is the main difference between a category and a filter?
A. A category provides a broader grouping; a filter narrows results by selected characteristics
B. A filter always changes a product's price
C. A category is invisible to customers
D. There is no difference

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Categories organise the catalogue while filters help customers refine a product set.

### Q22
A store uses “navy,” “Navy Blue,” “dark blue” and “blue/navy” as separate colour values for essentially the same colour. What problem can result?
A. More accurate stock counts
B. Fragmented filters and inconsistent reporting
C. Automatic price increases
D. Faster fulfilment

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Inconsistent attribute values weaken navigation, filtering and data quality.

### Q23
When is a collection especially useful?
A. For grouping products around a campaign, season or shopping theme
B. For storing customer passwords
C. For replacing all categories permanently
D. For assigning bank-account details

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Collections can create curated groups for campaigns, seasons or merchandising themes.

### Q24
A shopper looking for men's running shoes must click through seven vague category levels before seeing products. What is the most likely issue?
A. The navigation is unnecessarily complex
B. The store has too few SKUs
C. The selling price is automatically incorrect
D. The product images are necessarily misleading

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Excessive or unclear hierarchy can make product discovery difficult.

## Questions 25–32 — Lesson 5: Pricing, Mark-up, Margin & VAT

### Q25
A product costs R400 and the business adds a 25% mark-up on cost. What is the selling price before other adjustments?
A. R425
B. R480
C. R500
D. R525

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** 25% of R400 is R100; R400 + R100 = R500.

### Q26
A product sells for R800 and its cost is R600. What is the gross profit amount?
A. R100
B. R200
C. R600
D. R1,400

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Gross profit amount is selling price minus cost: R800 − R600 = R200.

### Q27
Using the figures in Q26, what is the gross margin percentage based on selling price?
A. 20%
B. 25%
C. 33.3%
D. 75%

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** R200 ÷ R800 × 100 = 25%.

### Q28
A product costs R300. A retailer wants a 40% gross margin on selling price. Which selling price achieves that margin, ignoring tax and other costs?
A. R420
B. R450
C. R500
D. R700

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** If cost is 60% of selling price, R300 ÷ 0.60 = R500.

### Q29
Why should an e-commerce business distinguish mark-up from margin?
A. They always produce identical percentages
B. They use different bases and can lead to different pricing conclusions
C. Margin applies only to images
D. Mark-up is a legal product identifier

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Mark-up is commonly measured against cost, while margin is measured against selling price.

### Q30
A VAT-registered South African seller is pricing goods subject to the standard VAT rate. Which principle is most important?
A. Ignore tax treatment until after the customer pays
B. Configure and present prices consistently with the business's applicable VAT obligations
C. Assume every item is VAT exempt
D. Use a different VAT rate for every customer without a legal basis

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Tax treatment should be correctly configured and consistently reflected in pricing and transaction records.

### Q31
A business copies a competitor's R699 price without checking its own R680 product cost, payment fees and fulfilment costs. What is the main risk?
A. The SKU becomes invalid
B. The business may sell at an unsustainable or inadequate profit
C. The competitor becomes the supplier
D. Product categories disappear

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Competitor prices are context, not a substitute for understanding the business's own economics.

### Q32
A product sells for R1,000 and costs R650. Which statement is correct before considering other expenses?
A. Gross profit amount is R350
B. Gross profit amount is R650
C. Gross margin is 65%
D. The business has no gross profit

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** R1,000 − R650 = R350 gross profit amount.

## Questions 33–38 — Lesson 6: Discounts, Promotions & Promotional Pricing

### Q33
A product normally sells for R800 and receives a genuine 15% discount. What is the promotional price?
A. R680
B. R685
C. R720
D. R785

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** 15% of R800 is R120; R800 − R120 = R680.

### Q34
Why should a business calculate promotion economics before launching a discount?
A. To check whether the promotion still supports acceptable commercial outcomes
B. To make every SKU identical
C. To avoid using product images
D. To guarantee the campaign will go viral

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Discounts reduce revenue per unit and may affect contribution or margin.

### Q35
What is the key risk of showing an inflated “was” price that was not a genuine reference price?
A. It may create a misleading impression of the saving
B. It increases product weight
C. It changes the product SKU
D. It improves catalogue accuracy

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Promotional price comparisons should not misrepresent the value of the claimed saving.

### Q36
A store offers “Buy 2, get 1 free.” What should be clear to customers?
A. Eligibility, qualifying products and important conditions
B. Only the colour of the promotional banner
C. The store's internal database password
D. Nothing; promotion conditions should remain hidden

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Customers should be able to understand how a promotion works before relying on it.

### Q37
A coupon expires at midnight, but the store continues displaying “Use today” for three days. What should the catalogue team do?
A. Leave it because expired messages increase urgency
B. Remove or correct the outdated promotional message
C. Increase every product price
D. Delete all product descriptions

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Expired promotional information should not remain active or create a false impression.

### Q38
A R500 product is discounted by 20%. What is the rand discount and final price?
A. R20 discount; R480 final
B. R50 discount; R450 final
C. R100 discount; R400 final
D. R120 discount; R380 final

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** 20% of R500 is R100, leaving a R400 selling price.

## Questions 39–44 — Lesson 7: Online Merchandising, Cross-selling & Upselling

### Q39
What is online merchandising?
A. Deliberately organising and presenting products to support discovery and commercial goals
B. Randomly changing product prices every hour
C. Removing navigation from the store
D. Creating accounting journal entries only

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Merchandising shapes how relevant products are presented and discovered.

### Q40
Which is the best example of cross-selling?
A. Recommending a laptop sleeve to someone buying a laptop
B. Recommending the same laptop at a higher random price
C. Removing all accessories
D. Showing an unrelated refrigerator beside a laptop

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Cross-selling recommends a complementary item.

### Q41
Which is the best example of upselling?
A. Offering a higher-capacity version that genuinely suits the customer's needs
B. Adding an unrelated item to the cart without consent
C. Hiding the original product
D. Showing an expired coupon

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Upselling presents a more capable or higher-value alternative when relevant.

### Q42
Why should “best seller” labels be based on a defensible rule?
A. So the label reflects genuine evidence rather than an arbitrary claim
B. Because every product must have the label
C. Because the label replaces stock information
D. Because labels determine VAT registration

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Merchandising claims should be accurate and supportable.

### Q43
A retailer places winter coats at the top of its homepage during a major cold period and links them to scarves and gloves. What techniques are being used?
A. Seasonal merchandising and cross-selling
B. SKU duplication and tax avoidance
C. Product deletion and data corruption
D. Only upselling

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** The store prioritises seasonally relevant products and recommends complementary items.

### Q44
Which merchandising decision is most customer-centred?
A. Recommend products because they are relevant to the customer's current shopping context
B. Recommend only the product with the highest margin regardless of relevance
C. Hide lower-priced options
D. Add items to carts automatically

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Effective merchandising balances commercial objectives with customer relevance and transparency.

## Questions 45–50 — Lesson 8: Catalogue Quality, Listing Maintenance & Product Performance

### Q45
Why is catalogue maintenance necessary after publication?
A. Product, supplier, stock, price and specification information can change over time
B. Published products can never be edited
C. Maintenance automatically doubles sales
D. It removes the need for customer service

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Catalogue information must remain current as products and operations change.

### Q46
A listing receives high traffic but unusually low conversion. What is the best interpretation?
A. The product must immediately be deleted
B. The data identifies a listing that deserves investigation, but does not prove the cause
C. The price is definitely the only problem
D. The SKU is definitely duplicated

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Performance indicators identify where investigation is needed; they do not automatically establish causation.

### Q47
Many customers return a shirt because they say it fits smaller than expected. What should the retailer investigate first?
A. Sizing information, variant data and customer expectations
B. The homepage logo colour only
C. Whether all products should be discontinued
D. Whether the return reason should be ignored

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** A repeated return reason can indicate a product-information or expectation problem requiring investigation.

### Q48
Which information belongs in a useful change log?
A. Date, product, field changed, old value, new value, reason and responsible person
B. Customer passwords
C. Only the product's image file size
D. Competitor employee names

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** A change log supports traceability and investigation of important catalogue updates.

### Q49
An audit finds an incorrect selling price, an inaccurate capacity specification and inconsistent thumbnail dimensions. What should normally receive priority?
A. Thumbnail dimensions only
B. Material price/specification errors before minor presentation inconsistencies
C. None of them
D. Whichever problem takes longest to fix

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Corrections should be prioritised according to customer, financial, operational and compliance risk.

### Q50
Which audit checks both customer-facing and operational accuracy?
A. Review storefront information plus SKU, stock, supplier and fulfilment records
B. Review the homepage colour only
C. Count social-media followers
D. Read competitor reviews only

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Catalogue quality includes both what customers see and the back-office records used to fulfil orders.

## Questions 51–52 — Integrated Whole-Module Scenarios

### Q51
A retailer launches a new blender. The page uses a clear title and attractive images, but the image shows two cups while only one is supplied. The page also lists 1,500 W although the verified specification is 1,200 W. The retailer plans to advertise “25% OFF” using an inflated reference price. What is the best corrective approach before launch?
A. Launch immediately because the page looks professional
B. Correct the media and specification, verify all product data, and use only a genuine, supportable promotional comparison
C. Keep the errors but add more customer reviews
D. Remove the SKU and category instead

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Product media, specifications and promotional claims should accurately represent the offer before publication.

### Q52
An online clothing store has high traffic but poor sales on a jacket category. An audit finds inconsistent colour filters, missing variant images, unclear size information, expired sale labels and several sold-out variants still appearing available. Which response best applies the complete module?
A. Increase all prices and make no catalogue changes
B. Correct product and stock data, standardise filters, improve variant presentation and sizing information, remove expired promotions, then monitor performance after the changes
C. Delete the entire category without investigation
D. Add “best seller” to every jacket

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** The strongest response integrates catalogue accuracy, navigation, presentation, promotion maintenance, stock control and performance monitoring.
