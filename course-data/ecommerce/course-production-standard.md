# Funda Online Academy — E-commerce & Online Store
## Premium Course Production Standard

Course ID: 98d4aefc-d757-447a-804c-002adf8787f5
Slug: e-commerce-online-store

## Architecture
- 10 modules.
- Lessons 1–8 in every module are teaching lessons.
- Entry 9 is the Formative Assessment.
- Entry 10 is the Summative Assessment.
- Total: 80 teaching lessons + 10 formative assessments + 10 summative assessments = 100 entries.

## Teaching lesson standard
Every lesson must contain: identification, 30–45 minute study-time guidance, 3–5 measurable learning outcomes, Why This Lesson Matters, 5–8 defined key terms, substantive main teaching content (minimum 500 words; normal target 700–1,000), worked example/demonstration, practical activity, informal knowledge check, feedback/explanation, key takeaways, and relevant visual-learning guidance where useful.

The lesson chain must remain aligned: title → outcomes → terminology → teaching → example → activity → knowledge check → takeaways → formal assessment.

## Assessment standard
### Formative
- Bank: 32 complete MCQs per module, normally 4 traceable questions per teaching lesson.
- Attempt: 15 questions selected from the bank.
- Four options A–D.
- Pass threshold: 70%.
- Maximum attempts: 3.
- Learner must pass Formative before Summative unlocks.

### Summative
- Bank: 52 complete MCQs per module, distributed across all eight teaching lessons.
- Attempt: 25 questions selected from the bank.
- Four options A–D.
- Pass threshold: 70%.
- Maximum attempts: 3.
- Broader and more application/judgement-oriented than the formative.

Questions may only formally assess knowledge, application or judgement that the teaching lessons have developed. Assessment items must not merely duplicate informal knowledge checks.

## E-commerce accuracy and South African context
The course must distinguish general e-commerce practice from legal/tax requirements. South African legal, consumer, privacy and tax claims require authoritative verification and careful wording. Core areas include the Consumer Protection Act, Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, POPIA, payment/fraud awareness, transparent online selling practices, and current SARS tax/VAT guidance. Platform-specific features must be taught as examples rather than permanent universal rules because platforms change.

## Practical learning philosophy
Learners should finish able to plan, build, operate and improve a credible small online store. Activities therefore progress from market and customer definition through store planning, product information, pricing, merchandising, payment/delivery workflows, customer service, marketing, analytics, privacy/security, and an integrated store-readiness project.

## Release rule
Build and QA the complete course on the protected branch before production release. Audit lesson completeness, assessment-bank counts, question traceability, progression gates, persistence, course isolation, mobile learner experience, and legal/technical accuracy before merge.
