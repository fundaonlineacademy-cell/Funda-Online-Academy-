## Lesson 1 — Order Lifecycle & Order Status Management

### Q1
Which status describes whether money has been collected successfully?
A. Fulfilment status
B. Payment status
C. Category status
D. Product status

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Payment status indicates whether a transaction is pending, paid, failed, refunded or in another payment state.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q2
An order exists, but payment is still pending. What should staff normally do before dispatch?
A. Treat the order as paid automatically
B. Verify payment through the approved system
C. Delete the order
D. Change the customer's address

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Order creation and payment confirmation are separate events.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q3
Why should fulfilment status not be changed to complete before the order is actually prepared and sent?
A. It can trigger incorrect actions and mislead staff or customers
B. It changes the product colour
C. It automatically refunds the customer
D. It increases courier speed

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Statuses should reflect real operational events.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q4
Which is an example of an order exception?
A. Correct payment and correct stock
B. Unavailable stock after an order is placed
C. A normal product title
D. A valid tracking number

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Unavailable stock interrupts the normal workflow and requires resolution.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1
