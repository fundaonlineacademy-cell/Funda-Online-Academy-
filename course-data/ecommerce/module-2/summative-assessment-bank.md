## Lesson 1 — Defining Store Requirements & Technical Needs

### Q1
A start-up chooses a store platform because an influencer recommended it. Two months later it discovers the platform cannot support its required local delivery workflow. What planning error occurred?
A. The logo was too small
B. Platform selection occurred before requirements were properly defined
C. The business had too many customers
D. The domain was too short

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Requirements should guide technology selection. Choosing technology first can create costly functional gaps.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q2
Which requirement is most directly related to integration?
A. The store must exchange order information with a courier service
B. The logo should use two colours
C. Staff should greet customers politely
D. Products should have appealing names

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Integration requirements describe how the store must connect or exchange information with another system or service.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q3
A business expects 20 products now but 5,000 within two years. Which planning factor is especially important?
A. Scalability
B. Office paint colour
C. Staff dress code
D. Printed receipt size

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Scalability concerns whether the solution can accommodate growth in products, users, transactions or operational complexity.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q4
Which is the best requirements statement?
A. The website must be amazing
B. Customers must be able to choose delivery or collection before confirming an order
C. We want something like a famous store
D. Make it modern somehow

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Good requirements are specific enough to design, configure and test.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q5
A merchant lists payment, inventory, customer-service and fulfilment needs but ignores who will maintain the system. What has been overlooked?
A. Operational ownership and resource requirements
B. Product photography only
C. Domain spelling
D. Customer age only

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Store planning must consider who will administer products, orders, integrations, updates and technical issues.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q6
Why should requirements be prioritised as essential, desirable and future needs?
A. To help make trade-offs when budget, time or platform capability is limited
B. To make the document longer
C. To eliminate customer research
D. To guarantee every feature is built immediately

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Prioritisation helps teams protect critical requirements while managing constraints and future development.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q7
Which question best tests whether a requirement is meaningful?
A. Can we explain why the business or customer needs it and how we will know it works?
B. Does the owner personally like the feature name?
C. Does it use the newest technology?
D. Does a competitor have it?

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Requirements should connect to a genuine need and be sufficiently clear to verify.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

## Lesson 2 — Hosted Platforms, Marketplaces & Self-hosted Stores

### Q8
A microbusiness has limited technical staff and needs to launch quickly. Which option may reduce infrastructure-management responsibility?
A. A suitable hosted e-commerce platform
B. Building an entire commerce stack from scratch
C. Running a server without maintenance
D. Avoiding all software updates

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Hosted services can reduce the technical burden of managing core hosting and platform infrastructure, though merchants still retain important configuration and operational responsibilities.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 2

### Q9
A merchant gains strong sales through a marketplace but customers mainly remember the marketplace brand. What strategic risk does this illustrate?
A. Dependence on a third-party channel and weaker direct brand relationship
B. Guaranteed ownership of customer traffic
C. Elimination of platform fees
D. Automatic domain registration

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Marketplaces can provide reach while limiting control over branding, customer relationships and platform rules.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 2

### Q10
Why might a business use both its own store and a marketplace?
A. To combine direct brand control with access to additional marketplace demand
B. Because every business is legally required to do so
C. To avoid maintaining product information
D. To guarantee identical fees

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** A multi-channel strategy can combine the strengths of different channels when operations can support it.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 2

### Q11
Which cost comparison is most useful when choosing a platform?
A. Total relevant cost including subscriptions, transaction costs, extensions, development and maintenance
B. Monthly subscription only
C. Logo-design cost only
D. Domain cost only

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Total cost of ownership provides a more realistic comparison than a single advertised fee.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 2

### Q12
A self-hosted store gives the merchant greater technical flexibility. What follows from that flexibility?
A. The merchant or technical team generally carries greater responsibility for maintenance and security
B. Security becomes unnecessary
C. Hosting becomes impossible
D. Customers must maintain the server

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Greater control generally brings greater technical responsibility.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 2

### Q13
A marketplace changes its fee structure and seller rules. Why should a merchant have anticipated this possibility?
A. Third-party channels are governed partly by policies outside the merchant's control
B. Marketplace rules can never change
C. Sellers own the marketplace infrastructure
D. Fees are fixed by domain registrars

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Platform dependency creates commercial risk because rules, fees, algorithms and access conditions may change.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 2

### Q14
What is the best platform-selection principle?
A. Match platform capabilities and trade-offs to the business's validated requirements
B. Select whichever platform has the most advertisements
C. Always choose the cheapest option
D. Always choose the most complex option

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Platform choice should be evidence-based and requirements-driven.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 2

## Lesson 6 — Mobile-first Design & Accessibility Basics

A. Readability and mobile usability
B. Inventory valuation
C. Courier selection
D. Product sourcing

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Mobile interfaces should support comfortable reading and interaction on smaller screens.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 6

### Q34
A customer navigating by keyboard cannot move focus away from a promotional pop-up. What is the issue?
A. A keyboard accessibility barrier
B. A product-pricing error
C. A shipping-zone error
D. A domain-renewal problem

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Interactive components should not trap keyboard users and prevent access to the rest of the interface.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 6

### Q35
A form shows invalid fields using red borders only. Why may this be insufficient?
A. Colour alone may not communicate the problem to every user; clear text feedback should also be provided
B. Red is never allowed online
C. Forms cannot use colour
D. Error messages are unnecessary

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Important state and error information should not depend on colour alone.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 6

### Q36

A. Provide appropriate text alternatives or equivalent information for meaningful visual content
B. Put all important product information only inside images
C. Remove product names

**Explanation:** Meaningful non-text content should have an appropriate alternative when needed so information is not exclusively visual.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 6

### Q37

B. Mobile customers cannot make payments

### Q39

**Explanation:** Trust signals must be genuine and verifiable. Fabricated claims can mislead customers and damage credibility.

A. Customers need a reliable route for questions and post-purchase support

**Explanation:** Accessible support channels improve trust and allow customers to resolve questions and problems.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 7

### Q42

## Lesson 8 — Testing & Launch-readiness of an Online Store

### Q47
During pre-launch testing, a successful payment creates no order in the administration system. How should this defect be classified?
A. High priority because payment and order records are not synchronised correctly
B. Cosmetic only
C. Irrelevant because payment succeeded
D. A marketing issue only

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** A paid transaction without a corresponding order record can create serious financial, fulfilment and customer-service problems.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 8

### Q48
What does end-to-end testing mean in an e-commerce context?
A. Testing a realistic customer and operational journey across connected stages from browsing through transaction and downstream handling
B. Testing the logo only
C. Testing the first page and assuming the rest works
D. Testing only warehouse lights

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** End-to-end testing checks whether connected steps and systems work together as a complete process.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 8

### Q49
A test order confirms correctly on-screen but no confirmation message reaches the customer. What does this demonstrate?
A. Testing should include customer communications as well as the visible checkout interface
B. Confirmation messages are never important
C. The homepage must be redesigned
D. The domain should be deleted

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Transaction communications are part of the complete customer journey and should be tested.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 8

### Q50
Which launch decision is most responsible?
A. Delay launch when a critical defect could cause incorrect charges or prevent order fulfilment
B. Launch every known critical defect and hope customers do not notice
C. Ignore security defects if the homepage looks good
D. Skip testing when the deadline is close

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Critical transaction, security, privacy and operational defects should be resolved or appropriately controlled before exposing customers to avoidable harm.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 8

### Q51
Why should store policies and contact links be included in launch testing?
A. A technically functional checkout is not sufficient if important customer information is missing or inaccessible
B. Policies cannot contain broken links
C. Contact information matters only after the first year
D. Launch testing applies only to payment buttons

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Launch readiness includes content, trust, support and compliance-related customer information as well as technical functionality.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 8

### Q52
After all critical launch tests pass, what is the best next practice?
A. Launch with monitoring, defined ownership and a process for responding to issues
B. Assume the store will never need maintenance
C. Delete the test records and all documentation
D. Remove support channels

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Launch is the start of live operations. Monitoring and clear responsibility help the team detect and respond to real-world issues.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 8
