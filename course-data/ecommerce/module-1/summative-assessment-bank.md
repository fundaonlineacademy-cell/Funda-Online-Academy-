## Questions 1–6 — Lesson 1: E-commerce Models, Ecosystem & Value Creation

### Q1
A manufacturer sells products directly to individual customers through its own website. Which model best describes the customer relationship?
A. B2C / direct-to-consumer
B. C2C only
C. Internal procurement
D. Government licensing

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** The business is selling directly to individual consumers.

### Q2
Which activity by itself is not enough to constitute a complete e-commerce operation?
A. Posting product advertisements on social media without a reliable transaction and fulfilment process
B. Accepting customer orders electronically
C. Processing approved payments
D. Fulfilling online orders

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Promotion alone does not provide the complete transaction and operational process.

### Q3
A marketplace provides access to existing shoppers but changes its seller fees. What risk does this illustrate?
A. Third-party platform dependency
B. Guaranteed profit
C. Inventory elimination
D. Automatic customer ownership

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Sellers depend partly on rules and costs controlled by the marketplace.

### Q4
Which is the strongest value proposition?
A. We sell everything
B. The best store ever
C. Affordable study bundles for South African university students with clear pricing and national courier delivery
D. Buy now because we say so

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** It identifies a defined customer and specific supportable value.

### Q5
Why is a courier part of the e-commerce ecosystem for a physical-goods store?
A. Delivery is part of fulfilling the customer transaction
B. Couriers determine product titles
C. Couriers replace payment providers
D. Delivery happens before product selection

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Physical orders depend on a fulfilment and delivery process.

### Q6
A store has strong traffic but cannot keep stock records accurate. What does this show?
A. Marketing success cannot compensate for a broken operational component
B. Traffic automatically fixes inventory
C. Stock accuracy matters only to suppliers
D. Product availability is unrelated to customers

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** The ecosystem must work as a connected system.

## Questions 7–12 — Lesson 2: Online Customers, Buying Behaviour & the Customer Journey

### Q7
A customer researches on a phone, leaves, then purchases on a laptop the next day. What principle does this demonstrate?
A. Customer journeys are not always linear
B. Mobile discovery is impossible
C. Only the final device matters
D. Awareness always happens after payment

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Customers may move between channels, devices and stages.

### Q8
Which change most directly reduces product-selection uncertainty?
A. Accurate images, specifications and size information
B. Hiding product details
C. Adding more unrelated pop-ups
D. Removing delivery information

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Useful information helps customers judge suitability before purchasing.

### Q9
A checkout requests the customer's telephone number three times without a business reason. What is this?
A. Unnecessary friction
B. Stronger product merchandising
C. Inventory control
D. Revenue recognition

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Repeated input adds effort without meaningful value.

### Q10
Why can a customer abandon after seeing an unexpected delivery charge?
A. The final transaction value differs from the expectation created earlier
B. Customers never care about total cost
C. Delivery charges improve trust automatically
D. The charge changes the SKU

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Late material charges can weaken trust and change the purchase decision.

### Q11
Which touchpoint occurs after the purchase?
A. Delivery update
B. Initial advertisement only
C. Domain registration
D. Supplier quotation only

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** The customer journey continues through fulfilment and support.

### Q12
A store has high cart abandonment. What is the best response?
A. Investigate possible causes such as cost, technical errors, payment options and usability
B. Assume every visitor was dishonest
C. Delete analytics
D. Increase all prices immediately

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** The metric identifies a problem area but does not reveal one automatic cause.

## Questions 13–18 — Lesson 3: Products, Services & Digital Commerce Offers

### Q13
What is the main availability constraint for an appointment-based online service?
A. Staff or service capacity
B. Product colour only
C. Courier parcel weight only
D. Website background colour

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Time and service capacity function like inventory constraints.

### Q14
A product has 5 colours and 3 sizes, all stocked separately. How many variants exist?
A. 8
B. 10
C. 15
D. 20

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** 5 × 3 = 15 combinations.

### Q15
Which information is especially important for a digital product?
A. Access method, licence or usage conditions and delivery mechanism
B. Courier box dimensions only
C. Warehouse aisle number only
D. Physical packaging weight only

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Customers need to understand how digital access is provided and under what conditions.

### Q16
Why should a seller verify compatibility information for an electronic accessory?
A. Customers may purchase the wrong item if material compatibility facts are inaccurate
B. Compatibility affects only the store logo
C. It is never relevant online
D. It replaces price information

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Accurate suitability information reduces wrong purchases and returns.

### Q17
Which product may require the most careful physical-delivery planning?
A. Fragile glassware
B. A downloadable PDF
C. A virtual consultation
D. An email notification

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Fragility affects packaging, handling and delivery risk.

### Q18
Why can offering hundreds of products before data and fulfilment processes are ready be risky?
A. Catalogue complexity can increase stock, content and fulfilment errors
B. More products automatically fix operations
C. Product count determines legal compliance
D. Large catalogues require no maintenance

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Scale increases the need for structured data and reliable processes.

## Questions 19–24 — Lesson 4: Revenue, Costs, Profitability & Basic E-commerce Metrics

### Q19
A store records R75,000 revenue and R48,000 cost of goods sold. What is simplified gross profit before other expenses?
A. R27,000
B. R48,000
C. R75,000
D. R123,000

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** R75,000 − R48,000 = R27,000.

### Q20
Revenue of R36,000 comes from 60 orders. What is average order value?
A. R360
B. R500
C. R600
D. R2,160

**Correct answer:** C
**Rationale:** R36,000 ÷ 60 = R600.

### Q21
Thirty purchases result from 1,200 measured sessions. What is the simplified purchase conversion rate?
A. 0.4%
B. 2.5%
C. 4%
D. 25%

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** 30 ÷ 1,200 × 100 = 2.5%.

### Q22
A campaign costs R6,000 and produces 20 newly acquired customers under the business's chosen method. What is simplified acquisition cost per customer?
A. R120
B. R300
C. R600
D. R1,200

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** R6,000 ÷ 20 = R300.

### Q23
Why might rising AOV fail to improve profit?
A. Larger orders may also include heavy discounts, shipping subsidies or high return costs
B. AOV always equals profit
C. Larger orders have no costs
D. AOV measures only website colour

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Order value must be interpreted together with cost and margin.

### Q24
Which is most clearly a vanity metric when viewed without any business context?
A. Social follower count alone
B. Gross profit amount
C. Refund value
D. Delivery cost

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Followers may show attention but do not by themselves establish valuable customer outcomes.

## Questions 25–30 — Lesson 5: South African E-commerce Law, Ethics & Consumer Protection Foundations

Which legislation is particularly relevant to electronic transactions and online-supplier information in South Africa?
A. ECTA
B. A courier's private handbook only
C. A social-media platform's style guide

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** ECTA governs important aspects of electronic communications and transactions.

### Q26
Which law is especially relevant to fair consumer treatment and product quality rights where applicable?
A. CPA
B. POPIA only
C. A domain registrar agreement only
D. Warehouse policy only

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** The Consumer Protection Act addresses consumer rights in applicable transactions.

### Q27
A store collects customer addresses for delivery. Which principle is strongest?
A. Handle the information responsibly for legitimate purposes and protect access
B. Publish all addresses publicly
C. Use the data for any unrelated purpose automatically
D. Give every staff member permanent access

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Customer information should be processed responsibly and with appropriate controls.

### Q28
A store displays invented five-star reviews. What is the main problem?
A. It creates a false impression of genuine customer opinion
B. It improves factual accuracy
C. It reduces marketing risk
D. It is required for online sales

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Fabricated reviews can mislead customers.

### Q29
Why is “our policy says no refunds under any circumstances” not a safe universal rule?
A. Applicable statutory rights may still require remedies in qualifying cases
B. Policies automatically override all law
C. Customers can never receive refunds
D. Refunds are controlled only by couriers

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Store policy does not eliminate rights provided by applicable legislation.

### Q30
A privacy complaint involves possible unauthorised disclosure of customer data. What should frontline staff do?
A. Record and escalate through the approved privacy/incident process
B. Delete all evidence
C. Post the complaint publicly
D. Guess that no law applies

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Sensitive cases require controlled investigation and escalation.

## Questions 31–36 — Lesson 6: E-commerce Operations, Inventory, Fulfilment & Customer Service Overview

### Q31
Which sequence is most appropriate for a normal paid physical order?
A. Order → verify payment → allocate stock → pick → pack → dispatch
B. Dispatch → create order → collect payment
C. Refund → pick → advertise
D. Deliver → create stock → payment

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Payment and stock should be controlled before fulfilment proceeds.

### Q32
An order is paid but the exact size ordered is out of stock. What should happen?
A. Record and resolve the stock exception before sending a substitute
B. Send any size without asking
C. Mark delivered
D. Delete the payment

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Substitution should not bypass approved resolution and customer communication.

### Q33
Why should payment status and fulfilment status be separate?
A. They describe different parts of the transaction
B. They are always identical
C. Payment status describes product colour
D. Fulfilment status calculates tax

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Money collection and physical preparation are separate operational states.

### Q34
Which record best supports investigation of a delivery complaint?
A. Order and tracking evidence
B. Staff guesses only
C. Unrelated advertising metrics
D. Competitor product titles

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Verified transaction and delivery records support fact-based service.

### Q35
What can repeated wrong-item complaints indicate?
A. A fulfilment or product-identification control weakness
B. Guaranteed customer fraud
C. Better inventory accuracy
D. Stronger search ranking

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Repeat patterns can reveal systemic operational problems.

### Q36
Why should an unresolved failed delivery remain visible?
A. Changing the status does not resolve the operational problem
B. All failed deliveries are complete
C. Visibility changes the product price
D. It removes the need for support

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Accurate records should reflect the real unresolved state.

## Questions 37–42 — Lesson 7: Security, Fraud & Privacy Foundations for Online Stores

### Q37
A staff member uses the same password for business email and the store administrator account. What risk increases?
A. Credential reuse can allow one compromise to affect multiple services
B. Product titles become inaccurate
C. Delivery zones disappear automatically
D. Conversion rate increases

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Reused credentials create chain risk.

### Q38
Which account should receive strong MFA protection where supported?
A. Primary business email and store administration
B. A printed shelf label only
C. A product colour value only
D. A courier parcel only

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** High-impact accounts can affect recovery, data and store control.

### Q39
An employee receives an unexpected MFA approval request. What is safest?
A. Do not approve it; treat it as suspicious and follow the incident process
B. Approve it to stop the notification
C. Share the code publicly
D. Disable all logging

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** The prompt may indicate an unauthorised login attempt.

### Q40
Which practice best reflects least privilege?
A. Give a warehouse picker fulfilment access but not payment-administration rights
B. Give all workers owner access
C. Share one administrator password
D. Retain every former employee account

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Permissions should match the user's authorised duties.

### Q41
Which combination reasonably justifies additional fraud review?
A. Repeated failed payments followed by an unusually high-value order and address change request
B. Correct payment and ordinary order behaviour
C. One normal low-value purchase
D. Accurate delivery information

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Several unusual signals can justify proportionate review.

### Q42
Why should full payment-card credentials not be requested through ordinary chat?
A. It exposes sensitive payment information unnecessarily
B. It improves security
C. It is needed for product search
D. It reduces fulfilment risk

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Sensitive payment information should remain within approved secure processes.

## Questions 43–48 — Lesson 8: E-commerce Business Planning & Readiness

### Q43
Which requirement is easiest to verify?
A. The site should feel premium
B. Customers can review the final order total before confirming checkout
C. Make it impressive
D. Use modern technology

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** It defines an observable customer capability.

### Q44
A business has limited launch budget. Which requirement should receive priority?
A. A working secure checkout and correct order process
B. An optional animated homepage effect
C. Five decorative fonts
D. A non-essential loyalty feature

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Launch-critical transaction requirements should be protected before optional features.

### Q45
Which is an operational requirement?
A. Define who monitors and processes new orders
B. Choose the owner's favourite colour
C. Increase the number of social profiles regardless of capacity
D. Copy another store's policies

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Operational requirements define how the business will run the service.

### Q46
Why should projected courier coverage be verified before platform configuration?
A. The delivery model depends on real service availability and cost
B. Courier coverage is purely decorative
C. It affects only product photography
D. Every courier serves every location

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** An unsupported delivery promise can make the business model unworkable.

### Q47
A business expects 20 products now and 4,000 within two years. What requirement deserves attention?
A. Scalability
B. Office furniture colour
C. Employee uniform only
D. Social username length only

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** The selected design should reasonably accommodate foreseeable catalogue growth.

### Q48
Which readiness statement is strongest?
A. The store is ready because the logo is complete
B. The business has defined customer, offer, essential requirements, payment, fulfilment, support, budget and major risks
C. The store is ready because a competitor has a website
D. Readiness requires no operational owner

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Readiness combines commercial, technical and operational preparation.

## Questions 49–52 — Integrated Module Scenarios

### Q49
A new store receives many visitors from an advert, but conversion is low. Customers complain that delivery charges appear only at the final step and mobile checkout fields overlap. What is the best interpretation?
A. Traffic alone is not enough; customer-journey friction and mobile defects need investigation
B. The business should buy more traffic immediately
C. Delivery and usability cannot affect conversion
D. The customer complaints should be ignored

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Marketing performance must be connected to the quality of the transaction experience.

### Q50
A retailer wants to sell fragile products nationally. It has no packaging process, has not verified courier coverage and selected a platform only because it is cheap. What should happen first?
A. Revisit requirements, fulfilment capability and delivery assumptions before launch
B. Launch immediately and learn from damaged orders
C. Remove product descriptions
D. Give every employee administrator access

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Operational readiness and requirements should be resolved before accepting customer orders.

### Q51
A paid order is marked complete, but stock was never allocated and there is no dispatch record. What principle has failed?
A. Operational status accuracy and reconciliation
B. Product photography
C. Brand colour consistency only
D. Search-engine optimisation only

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** The order, payment and fulfilment records do not tell a consistent story.

### Q52
A staff member receives a suspicious login message and simultaneously notices a new administrator account. What is the best response?
A. Follow the approved security-incident process, preserve evidence, secure access and escalate promptly
B. Ignore both events
C. Delete all logs
D. Send passwords through chat for verification

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Multiple security indicators justify immediate controlled response rather than improvisation.
