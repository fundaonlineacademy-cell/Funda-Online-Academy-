### Q1
A business uses a website to accept orders and payments, but goods are collected from a physical store. Is this still e-commerce?

A. No, because delivery must be digital  
B. Yes, because the commercial transaction is conducted through a digital channel  
C. No, because collection is offline  
D. Only if the product is downloadable

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** E-commerce concerns the commercial transaction process, which may include digital ordering and payment even when fulfilment is physical.

**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q2
Which feature most clearly distinguishes e-commerce from simply having an informational website?

A. A logo  
B. Digital commercial activity such as ordering, payment or transaction facilitation  
C. A contact page  
D. A company history page

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** E-commerce involves conducting or enabling commercial transactions, not merely publishing information.

**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q3
Which business activity is least likely to be part of an e-commerce operating system?

A. Digital marketing  
B. Order processing  
C. Customer service  
D. Ignoring post-purchase issues

**Correct answer:** D

**Explanation:** Post-purchase service is a core part of the customer experience and cannot be ignored in a functioning e-commerce operation.

**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q4

A. Correct; online sales eliminate operations  
B. Incorrect; many online businesses still require inventory, packing, delivery, returns and service

D. Correct if customers pay electronically

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** Digital selling does not remove physical fulfilment obligations for physical products.

**Traceability:** Lesson 1
