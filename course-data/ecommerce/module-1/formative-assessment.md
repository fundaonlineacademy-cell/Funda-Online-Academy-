## Lesson 1 — E-commerce Models, Ecosystem & Value Creation

### Q1
Which description best defines e-commerce?
A. Any use of social media by a business
B. Buying and selling through electronic transaction processes supported by digital systems
C. Printing a price list
D. Delivering products without taking orders

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** E-commerce includes the electronic transaction and supporting commercial process, not promotion alone.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q2
A retailer sells directly to individual shoppers through its website. Which model is this primarily?
A. B2C
B. B2B
C. C2C
D. Internal procurement

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Business-to-consumer commerce involves a business selling to individual consumers.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q3
Which participant is part of a typical e-commerce ecosystem?
A. Payment provider
B. Only the store logo
C. Office paint supplier only
D. None of the systems used after checkout

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Payment providers, platforms, suppliers, couriers and customers are common ecosystem participants.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q4
Why does online reach not automatically guarantee profit?
A. Online stores have no costs
B. Revenue must still support product, transaction, fulfilment, marketing and service costs
C. Every website visitor must buy
D. Profit depends only on follower count

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** E-commerce has real operating and transaction costs that must be supported by revenue and margin.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 1

## Lesson 2 — Online Customers, Buying Behaviour & the Customer Journey

### Q5
What is a customer touchpoint?
A. A place where the customer encounters or interacts with the business

C. A tax calculation
D. An employee password

### Q6

A. Clear delivery selection
B. Requiring the same information repeatedly without reason
C. Accurate order totals
D. Understandable error messages

A. They reduce uncertainty
B. They can create an unexpected final cost and weaken trust
C. They make product images larger
D. They improve accessibility

**Traceability:** Lesson 2

### Q8
Why should post-purchase experience be included in the customer journey?

B. The transaction ends before payment
C. Returns have no effect on loyalty

**Explanation:** Fulfilment and support materially affect customer satisfaction and repeat purchase.

## Lesson 4 — Revenue, Costs, Profitability & Basic E-commerce Metrics

### Q13
A store records R30,000 sales revenue and R18,000 cost of goods sold. What is simplified gross profit before other expenses?
A. R12,000
B. R18,000
C. R30,000
D. R48,000

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** R30,000 − R18,000 = R12,000.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 4

### Q14
A store earns R24,000 from 40 orders. What is average order value?
A. R400
B. R500
C. R600
D. R960

**Correct answer:** C  
**Explanation:** R24,000 ÷ 40 = R600.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 4

### Q15
Twenty purchases result from 800 measured sessions. What is the simplified conversion rate?
A. 0.25%
B. 2.5%
C. 4%
D. 25%

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** 20 ÷ 800 × 100 = 2.5%.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 4

### Q16
Which is an e-commerce cost that can reduce commercial performance even when sales increase?
A. Payment and delivery costs
B. Product title length only
C. Number of menu headings only
D. Screen brightness

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Transaction and fulfilment costs affect the economics of each order.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 4

## Lesson 7 — Security, Fraud & Privacy Foundations for Online Stores

### Q25
What is least privilege?
A. Giving every employee administrator rights
B. Giving users only the access required for authorised work
C. Sharing one password across a team
D. Disabling all security controls

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Least privilege limits unnecessary access and reduces risk.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 7

### Q26
Why is MFA useful?
A. A stolen password alone may no longer be enough to access the account
B. It guarantees that phishing cannot occur
C. It replaces strong access control
D. It makes every order legitimate

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** MFA adds another verification factor beyond the password.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 7

### Q27
Which is a phishing warning sign?
A. Unexpected urgency asking the user to follow a suspicious login link
B. A normal saved bookmark
C. A verified internal procedure
D. An expected order number

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Urgency and unexpected credential requests are common phishing indicators.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 7

### Q28
Does one unusual transaction automatically prove fraud?
A. Yes
B. No; it may justify review but requires context and evidence
C. Only if the order is new
D. Only if the customer uses a phone

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Fraud indicators should trigger proportionate review, not unsupported conclusions.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 7
