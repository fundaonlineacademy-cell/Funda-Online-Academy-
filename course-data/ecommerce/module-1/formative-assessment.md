# E-commerce & Online Store — Module 1 Formative Assessment Bank

**Module:** E-commerce Foundations & Digital Business Models  
**Bank size:** 32 questions  
**Questions per learner attempt:** 15  
**Pass requirement:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Coverage:** Lessons 1–8, four questions per lesson.  

---

### Q1
Which statement best describes e-commerce?

A. Any business that uses a computer internally  
B. Buying, selling or enabling commercial transactions through digital channels  
C. Only selling products through social media  
D. Only selling downloadable products

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** E-commerce includes commercial transactions conducted or enabled through digital channels, including websites, marketplaces, apps and other online systems.

**Traceability:** Lesson 1 — Understanding E-commerce & the Digital Marketplace

### Q2
A small retailer accepts orders on a website, takes electronic payment and delivers physical goods. This is best described as:

A. E-commerce involving physical fulfilment  
B. A purely offline business  
C. A non-commercial digital service  
D. Internal enterprise software

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** The transaction is completed digitally even though the final product is physical and requires fulfilment.

**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q3
Which is the strongest reason a business should understand the wider digital marketplace before launching?

A. To copy the first competitor it finds  
B. To understand customer behaviour, channels, competition and transaction expectations  
C. To avoid using any technology  
D. To guarantee immediate profit

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** Understanding the digital marketplace supports realistic decisions about channels, customers, competition, service expectations and positioning.

**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q4
Which statement is most accurate?

A. E-commerce success depends only on having a website  
B. E-commerce combines commercial, operational, customer and digital activities  
C. E-commerce eliminates the need for customer service  
D. Online businesses do not need fulfilment processes

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** A functioning e-commerce business integrates marketing, selling, payment, fulfilment, service, data and operational controls.

**Traceability:** Lesson 1

### Q5
A business sells directly to individual consumers through its own online store. Which model is this?

A. B2B  
B. B2C  
C. C2C  
D. Government procurement

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** Business-to-consumer (B2C) describes a business selling directly to individual consumers.

**Traceability:** Lesson 2 — E-commerce Business Models & Revenue Models

### Q6
A wholesaler sells bulk packaging materials online to restaurants. This is primarily:

A. B2C  
B. C2C  
C. B2B  
D. Subscription-to-consumer only

**Correct answer:** C

**Explanation:** The transaction is business-to-business because both seller and buyer are businesses.

**Traceability:** Lesson 2

### Q7
Which revenue model is based on customers paying repeatedly for continued access to a product or service?

A. Subscription  
B. One-time resale only  
C. Donation only  
D. Barter

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** Subscription models generate recurring revenue in exchange for continued access or recurring supply.

**Traceability:** Lesson 2

### Q8
Why should an entrepreneur distinguish between a business model and a revenue model?

A. They are exactly the same thing  
B. The business model explains how value is created and delivered, while the revenue model explains how money is earned  
C. Revenue models apply only to large companies  
D. Business models do not involve customers

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** A business model is broader than the revenue mechanism; it includes customers, channels, activities and value delivery.

**Traceability:** Lesson 2

### Q9
A value proposition should primarily explain:

A. Why the owner likes the product  
B. Why a target customer should choose the offer  
C. The business registration number only  
D. The full accounting records of the business

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** A value proposition connects a relevant customer need or problem to the value delivered by the offer.

**Traceability:** Lesson 3 — Customer Needs, Value Propositions & Market Fit

### Q10
Which is the strongest evidence of customer need?

A. The owner assumes people will buy  
B. Repeated customer interviews and observed buying behaviour showing the same problem  
C. A logo receives compliments  
D. A competitor has many followers

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** Repeated evidence from target customers is stronger than personal assumptions or vanity indicators.

**Traceability:** Lesson 3

### Q11
A store receives traffic but very few visitors buy because the offer does not solve the problem customers expected. This most directly suggests weak:

A. Market fit  
B. Warehouse lighting  
C. Payroll administration  
D. Office furniture

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** Poor alignment between the offer and customer expectations is a market-fit problem.

**Traceability:** Lesson 3

### Q12
Which approach is best when defining a target customer?

A. “Everyone with internet access”  
B. A specific group with shared needs, behaviours or purchasing context  
C. Only the business owner's friends  
D. Anyone who visits the homepage

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** Useful targeting identifies a meaningful customer segment rather than an unrealistically broad audience.

**Traceability:** Lesson 3

### Q13
When evaluating a possible product niche, which combination is most useful?

A. Customer demand, competition, margin potential and operational feasibility  
B. Colour preference only  
C. Number of social posts only  
D. The cheapest supplier only

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** A viable niche must be attractive to customers and practical to source, price, fulfil and support.

**Traceability:** Lesson 4 — Choosing Products, Services & Niches

### Q14
Why can a very broad product range be risky for a new online store?

A. It always creates legal problems  
B. It can increase complexity in sourcing, stock, positioning and marketing  
C. Customers never like variety  
D. Search engines prohibit large catalogues

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** A broad range can dilute positioning and create operational complexity before the business has stable processes.

**Traceability:** Lesson 4

### Q15
Which product is generally more operationally complex to sell online?

A. A simple digital guide  
B. A fragile physical item requiring careful packing and delivery  
C. A standard downloadable template  
D. A recorded webinar with no physical fulfilment

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** Fragile physical goods introduce packaging, stock, damage and delivery risk.

**Traceability:** Lesson 4

### Q16
What is the best reason to test a niche before investing heavily?

A. Testing guarantees success  
B. Testing can reduce uncertainty by checking real customer response before larger commitments  
C. Testing removes the need for budgeting  
D. Testing replaces customer service

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** Early testing helps validate assumptions before major spending on inventory, technology or marketing.

**Traceability:** Lesson 4

### Q17
The most useful competitor research focuses on:

A. Copying branding exactly  
B. Understanding offers, pricing, customer experience, positioning and gaps  
C. Reporting competitors without evidence  
D. Ignoring customer reviews

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** Competitor analysis should generate insight, not imitation.

**Traceability:** Lesson 5 — Competitor Research & Market Validation

### Q18
Which source can provide particularly useful evidence about competitor weaknesses?

A. Customer reviews and complaints  
B. The competitor’s logo colour only  
C. Office address alone  
D. Number of employees alone

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** Reviews and complaints can reveal recurring problems that competitors are not solving well.

**Traceability:** Lesson 5

### Q19
A landing page attracts 500 target visitors but almost nobody clicks “Buy” or “Join waitlist.” What is the most sensible conclusion?

A. The idea is definitely successful  
B. The test indicates weak response and the offer, audience or message needs investigation  
C. The business should immediately purchase large inventory  
D. The result proves the product is illegal

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** Low engagement is evidence to investigate and refine, not evidence to scale immediately.

**Traceability:** Lesson 5

### Q20
Market validation is strongest when it uses:

A. Only the founder’s opinion  
B. Evidence from real target customers and realistic buying behaviour  
C. A single social-media like  
D. A supplier’s promise that the product will sell

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** Validation should be based on customer evidence and, where possible, behaviour close to real purchase decisions.

**Traceability:** Lesson 5

### Q21
Which sequence best represents a simplified digital customer journey?

A. Awareness → consideration → purchase → post-purchase experience  
B. Purchase → awareness → cancellation → discovery  
C. Refund → launch → product idea  
D. Shipping → website creation → awareness

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** Customers generally become aware, evaluate, buy and then experience post-purchase service and fulfilment.

**Traceability:** Lesson 6 — Digital Customer Journeys & Buying Behaviour

### Q22
A customer abandons checkout after discovering an unexpected delivery fee. This is best described as:

A. A friction point in the customer journey  
B. A product manufacturing defect  
C. A payroll error  
D. A website domain transfer

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** Unexpected costs at checkout create friction and can reduce conversion.

**Traceability:** Lesson 6

### Q23
Why are trust signals important during online buying?

A. Customers cannot physically inspect the business in the same way as a traditional store  
B. Trust signals automatically remove all fraud  
C. They replace the need for accurate product information  
D. They make pricing irrelevant

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** Online customers rely on information, design, policies, reviews and other signals to judge credibility and risk.

**Traceability:** Lesson 6

### Q24
Which action is most likely to improve a confusing customer journey?

A. Add more unnecessary steps  
B. Identify where customers hesitate or leave and simplify those points  
C. Hide delivery information  
D. Remove contact options

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** Journey improvement requires identifying friction and making the process clearer and easier.

**Traceability:** Lesson 6

### Q25
Which practice best demonstrates ethical e-commerce?

A. Hiding important fees until the final payment screen  
B. Providing accurate claims, transparent terms and honest communication  
C. Publishing fabricated reviews  
D. Using false scarcity messages

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** Ethical commerce depends on honesty, transparency and fair treatment of customers.

### Q26
Why is it risky to use fake reviews?

A. They can mislead customers and damage trust  
B. They always increase repeat purchase  
C. They are required for online stores  
D. They improve product accuracy

**Explanation:** Fabricated social proof can mislead customers and undermine long-term credibility.

### Q27
A professional online seller discovers a product description contains an incorrect specification. What should the seller do?

A. Leave it because some customers may not notice  
B. Correct the information promptly and address affected customers appropriately  
C. Delete customer messages  
D. Increase the price to offset the error

**Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:** Professional conduct requires prompt correction and responsible customer communication.

**Traceability:** Lesson 7

### Q28
Which trust-building practice is strongest?

A. Clear identity, contact details, policies and consistent fulfilment  
B. Anonymous ownership with no contact method  
C. Different terms on every page  
D. Unverifiable claims

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** Customers are more likely to trust a business that is identifiable, transparent and operationally reliable.

**Traceability:** Lesson 7

### Q29
A viable online business concept should connect:

A. Customer need, value proposition, revenue logic and operational feasibility  
B. A logo and a social-media account only  
C. Website colours and office location only  
D. Product enthusiasm without evidence

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** Viability depends on a coherent connection between customer demand, value creation, revenue and the ability to deliver.

**Traceability:** Lesson 8 — Planning a Viable Online Business Concept

### Q30
Which assumption should be tested early in an online business plan?

A. Whether target customers will value and pay for the proposed offer  
B. Whether the owner prefers one font over another  
C. Whether the owner can work without records  
D. Whether competitors can be ignored

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** Willingness to buy is a critical commercial assumption that should be validated early.

**Traceability:** Lesson 8

### Q31
A business concept has strong customer interest but delivery costs are so high that each sale loses money. The main issue is:

A. Operational and financial viability  
B. Customer awareness only  
C. Logo quality  
D. Number of social followers

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** Demand alone is not enough; the model must also be economically and operationally workable.

**Traceability:** Lesson 8

### Q32
Which is the best final step before committing major resources to a new online business concept?

A. Review evidence, assumptions, costs, risks and the test results together  
B. Ignore all negative findings  
C. Order maximum inventory immediately  
D. Copy a competitor’s entire store

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** A disciplined decision combines market evidence, commercial logic, operational feasibility and risk before scaling.

**Traceability:** Lesson 8
