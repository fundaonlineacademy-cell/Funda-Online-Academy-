# E-commerce & Online Store
## Module 8: Practical Online Store Project
### Lesson 6: Build the Launch Marketing Plan

**Estimated study time:** 40–45 minutes

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:
1. Define a launch campaign objective for the capstone store.
2. Build a simple customer journey across digital channels.
3. Create campaign content and a launch calendar.
4. Set a realistic budget and KPIs.
5. Apply responsible direct-marketing and promotional practices.

## Why This Lesson Matters
The practical store should not launch without a plan for attracting customers. The project campaign does not need a large budget, but it should show strategic thinking: who the store wants to reach, what message will be used, where the message will appear, what action customers should take and how results will be measured.

## Key Terms / Vocabulary
- **Launch campaign** — Coordinated marketing activity introducing the store or offer to customers.
- **Audience** — The group the campaign is intended to reach.
- **Campaign asset** — A marketing item such as an advert, email, video or social post.
- **Media budget** — Money allocated to paid marketing placements.
- **KPI** — A key performance indicator linked to the campaign objective.
- **Campaign calendar** — A schedule of campaign activity and deadlines.

## Main Lesson Content
### 1. Set one main launch objective
The project should choose a specific objective such as generating the first 30 test or simulated orders, driving qualified traffic to a launch collection or building awareness among a defined audience. One clear objective makes measurement easier.

### 2. Map the launch journey
A customer might see a social post, click to a campaign landing page, review a product, join a permission-based email list and purchase. The project should explain how each channel supports the next stage.

### 3. Create campaign assets
Build at least three launch assets, for example a social post, an email and a paid-ad concept. Each should use accurate product and promotional information, a clear CTA and consistent brand messaging.

### 4. Plan direct marketing carefully
If email or SMS is used, the project should demonstrate a legitimate contact source, appropriate consent or existing-customer basis where applicable, sender identity and a practical opt-out mechanism. The project should not use scraped or invented personal data.

### 5. Set a budget
If paid advertising is included, define a realistic test budget and what result would justify continuing. The project may estimate CPC, CPA or ROAS scenarios to demonstrate commercial reasoning.

### 6. Define KPIs
Choose measures that match the objective: reach, qualified visits, add-to-cart rate, purchases, conversion rate, CPA or revenue. Avoid judging a sales campaign only by likes.

## Worked Example / Demonstration
StudyBox SA runs a two-week launch campaign targeting South African university students. Organic social posts demonstrate bundle contents, a paid campaign sends traffic to a dedicated landing page, and an email is sent only to properly collected contacts. The learner tracks visits, add-to-cart actions, completed test purchases and simulated CPA.

## Practical Activity
Create a two-week campaign calendar containing at least six campaign activities. Add audience, message, CTA, channel, KPI and owner for each activity. Include one paid-ad budget calculation and one direct-marketing compliance check.

## Knowledge Check
1. Why should a launch campaign have a clear objective?
2. What is a campaign asset?
3. Why should email/SMS contacts have a legitimate basis?
4. Why are likes insufficient for measuring a sales campaign?
5. What should a media budget include besides the total amount?

## Feedback / Explanation
1. The objective guides message, channels and measurement.
2. A specific piece of marketing content used in the campaign.
3. Electronic direct marketing is subject to privacy and consumer-protection requirements.
4. Likes do not necessarily lead to traffic or purchases.
5. A review point or expected result that helps decide whether to continue.

## Key Takeaways
- The project launch needs a defined audience and objective.
- Campaign channels should form a connected customer journey.
- Promotional assets must remain truthful and consistent.
- Direct marketing should use responsible, traceable contact practices.
- KPIs should match the intended commercial outcome.
