## Lesson 3 — Set Pricing, Promotions & Merchandising

### Q9
A product costs R200 and sells for R320. Gross profit is:
A. R80
B. R100
C. R120
D. R520

**Correct answer:** C  
**Explanation:** R320 − R200 = R120.  
**Traceability:** Lesson 3

### Q10
Using the same figures, gross margin is:
A. 20%
B. 37.5%
C. 60%
D. 120%

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** R120 ÷ R320 × 100 = 37.5%.
