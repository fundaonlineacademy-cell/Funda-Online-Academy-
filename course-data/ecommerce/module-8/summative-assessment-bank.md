# E-commerce & Online Store
## Module 8: Practical Online Store Project
### Summative Assessment Bank — 52 Questions

## Assessment Rules
- **Question bank:** 52 multiple-choice questions.
- **Coverage:** All 8 Module 8 lessons.
- **Assessment focus:** Project planning, catalogue build, pricing, trust/compliance, operations, marketing, testing and final performance planning.
- **Question design:** One best answer per question.
- **Purpose:** Confirm that the learner can integrate the complete e-commerce course into a practical online-store project.

## Questions 1–6 — Lesson 1: Capstone Brief & Requirements

### Q1
Why should a store project begin with a written brief?
A. It defines the customer, requirements, scope and success criteria
B. It replaces all testing
C. It guarantees profit
D. It removes the need for products

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** A brief provides the reference point for the entire build.

### Q2
Which target customer is more useful for planning?
A. Everyone
B. South African university students seeking affordable study bundles
C. Anyone online
D. All adults everywhere

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Specific audiences support clearer product and marketing decisions.

### Q3
Which is a functional requirement?
A. Customers can add products to a cart and checkout
B. The owner likes blue
C. The project should feel nice
D. Staff prefer short meetings

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Functional requirements define what the store must do.

### Q4
Which is a commercial requirement?
A. Defined pricing and margin targets
B. Button colour
C. Office location only
D. Image file name

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Commercial requirements relate to business viability and pricing.

### Q5
Why should scope be limited?
A. To ensure the project can be completed and tested properly
B. To make the store incomplete
C. To avoid all features
D. To reduce customer clarity

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** A focused project demonstrates quality better than untested complexity.

### Q6
Which is the strongest success criterion?
A. “The store looks good.”
B. “A customer can find a product, review accurate information, complete a test checkout and receive correct confirmation.”
C. “The owner likes it.”
D. “There are many pages.”

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** It is measurable and connected to the customer journey.

## Questions 13–18 — Lesson 3: Pricing, Promotions & Merchandising

### Q13
A product costs R250 and sells for R400. Gross profit is:
A. R100
B. R150
C. R250
D. R650

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** R400 − R250 = R150.

### Q14
Using the same figures, gross margin is:
A. 25%
B. 37.5%
C. 60%
D. 150%

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** R150 ÷ R400 × 100 = 37.5%.

### Q15
What is a genuine promotional approach?
A. A clearly defined 10% discount from the real normal price
B. Inflating the normal price first
C. Hiding delivery fees until after payment
D. Claiming fake scarcity

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Promotional savings should be real and transparent.

### Q16
Which is an upsell?
A. Offering a larger-capacity bundle that suits the same need
B. Offering pens with a notebook
C. Deleting the original product
D. Adding an unrelated item without consent

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Upselling offers a higher-value or more capable alternative.

### Q17
Which is a cross-sell?
A. Recommending a laptop sleeve with a laptop
B. Recommending the same laptop at a random higher price
C. Removing accessories
D. Hiding lower-cost options

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Cross-selling offers complementary products.

### Q18
Why should merchandising remain customer-relevant?
A. Irrelevant recommendations create clutter and reduce usefulness
B. Relevance reduces sales automatically
C. Customers should see every product everywhere
D. Merchandising has no effect on discovery

**Correct answer:** A
**Rationale:** Merchandising should support product discovery and customer decisions.

## Questions 51–52 — Integrated Whole-course Scenarios

### Q51
A capstone store has accurate products and attractive marketing, but checkout applies the wrong delivery charge, the return policy promises a process the team cannot perform, and a shared administrator password is used by all staff. What is the best launch decision?
A. Go because marketing is ready
B. No-go until delivery, policy/operations and administrator-access risks are corrected and retested
C. Launch and fix later
D. Hide the policy

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Serious transaction, operational and security weaknesses remain unresolved.

### Q52
After launch, a store sees strong traffic but weak conversion, frequent wrong-size returns and many “where is my order?” enquiries. What is the strongest improvement approach?
A. Increase ad spend only
B. Analyse the funnel, improve size information, review fulfilment/tracking communication, prioritise root causes and re-measure results
C. Stop accepting returns
D. Remove customer service

**Correct answer:** B
**Rationale:** Whole-course improvement requires evidence across marketing, product data, operations and service.
