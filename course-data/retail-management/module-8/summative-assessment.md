# Module 8 Summative Assessment Bank — Retail Management Capstone & Career Leadership

**Question bank:** 52 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 25 questions per attempt  
**Pass mark:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3

1. A capstone differs from an isolated quiz because it primarily requires the learner to:
A. Integrate knowledge and management decisions across functions
B. Recall one definition at a time
C. Repeat lesson headings
D. Memorise company names
**Correct answer: A**

2. The brief reports rising queue complaints but does not explain why. The cause should initially be treated as:
A. A fact
B. An assumption requiring investigation
C. A disciplinary finding
D. A final conclusion
**Correct answer: B**

3. Which diagnostic approach is strongest?
A. Review sales only
B. Ask one employee
C. Compare customer, people, operational, commercial, stock and technology evidence
D. Copy last month's action plan
**Correct answer: C**

4. A blocked emergency exit and a minor display imperfection occur together. The manager should generally:
A. Fix the display first
B. Wait for the next meeting
C. Treat both as equal
D. prioritise the safety risk immediately
**Correct answer: D**

5. A good diagnostic question after finding low conversion is:
A. What evidence about availability, service, queues and customer experience may explain it?
B. Which employee should be blamed?
C. How can the target be reduced?
D. Why should traffic no longer be measured?
**Correct answer: A**

6. Triangulation means:
A. Drawing a store layout
B. comparing multiple evidence sources before concluding
C. Using three employees per shift
D. Calculating margin
**Correct answer: B**

7. A measurable service-improvement action should include:
A. “Try harder”
B. “Be friendlier”
C. a defined intervention, owner and performance measure
D. No review date
**Correct answer: C**

8. A corrective action that does not improve the intended result should:
A. Be called successful because it was completed
B. Never be discussed again
C. Be hidden from reports
D. be reviewed and adjusted based on evidence
**Correct answer: D**

9. Peak-time staffing should be planned using:
A. Expected demand, critical tasks, capability and control requirements
B. Employee friendships
C. Alphabetical order
D. Last year's roster only
**Correct answer: A**

10. An employee is delegated responsibility for promotional checks. The manager should:
A. Never review the work
B. retain appropriate oversight and follow-up
C. Assume delegation guarantees completion
D. Perform all checks again personally every hour
**Correct answer: B**

11. Routine coaching is most effective when feedback is based on:
A. Personality
B. Rumours
C. observable behaviour and required standards
D. Personal preference
**Correct answer: C**

12. A potential formal misconduct issue should be handled through:
A. Public confrontation
B. Automatic dismissal
C. Informal punishment invented by the supervisor
D. approved organisational/HR processes and current applicable guidance
**Correct answer: D**

13. A store has stable traffic but lower transactions. Which measure is particularly relevant?
A. Conversion
B. Supplier count
C. Store floor area
D. Employee age
**Correct answer: A**

14. Strong sales accompanied by declining gross margin should prompt investigation of:
A. Only customer traffic
B. product mix, discounting/markdown and other margin drivers
C. Only staff attendance
D. Only opening time
**Correct answer: B**

15. Backroom stock exists but shelves are empty. The likely immediate control area is:
A. Payroll
B. Recruitment
C. replenishment execution
D. Store rent
**Correct answer: C**

16. Unexplained stock variance should not automatically result in an allegation because:
A. Variance never matters
B. Theft cannot occur in retail
C. Stock records are always wrong
D. receiving, adjustment, damage, fulfilment and other process errors may contribute
**Correct answer: D**

17. A useful management report begins with:
A. the material status, results, exceptions and actions requiring attention
B. Every transaction record
C. Employee personal details
D. Ten pages of background
**Correct answer: A**

18. Which statement separates evidence from interpretation correctly?
A. “Staff are lazy; therefore conversion fell.”
B. “Traffic was stable and conversion fell; service and availability are being investigated as possible drivers.”
C. “Sales fell, so the economy caused it.”
D. “The report proves poor leadership.”
**Correct answer: B**

19. A decision log should record:
A. Only meeting attendees
B. Customer complaints only
C. decisions, responsible owners and follow-up timing
D. Employee salaries
**Correct answer: C**

20. A useful escalation should avoid:
A. Factual evidence
B. Operational impact
C. A clear support request
D. unsupported accusations and vague complaints
**Correct answer: D**

21. A portfolio competency statement is strongest when it:
A. describes credible action and evidence
B. Says “excellent manager” without examples
C. Exaggerates authority
D. Hides whether work was simulated
**Correct answer: A**

22. A learner completed a simulated stock investigation in the capstone. On a CV it should be described as:
A. Three years of stock-management employment
B. a simulated/capstone stock-control project
C. An external audit
D. Paid consulting
**Correct answer: B**

23. In STAR, the “A” represents:
A. Achievement
B. Assessment
C. Action
D. Authority
**Correct answer: C**

24. A professional portfolio should protect:
A. Only layout diagrams
B. Course titles
C. Public job advertisements
D. confidential and personal information
**Correct answer: D**

25. During the final simulation a delivery discrepancy and customer queue issue occur together. The manager should:
A. assess risk, allocate capable owners and monitor both priorities
B. Ignore receiving controls to serve customers
C. Personally solve every issue
D. Close the store automatically
**Correct answer: A**

26. A promotional price mismatch begins generating complaints. The manager should:
A. Ignore it until close
B. verify the mismatch, protect customer handling and correct/escalate the pricing cause through approved process
C. Delete the promotion
D. Blame cashiers
**Correct answer: B**

27. One experienced cashier becomes unavailable before peak. The best first response is to:
A. Cancel all breaks permanently
B. Share manager POS credentials
C. reassess deployment, capability and peak coverage within approved arrangements
D. Ignore the absence
**Correct answer: C**

28. A POS outage during peak should be managed using:
A. Unrecorded cash sales
B. Shared passwords
C. Customer devices
D. approved incident, continuity and escalation controls
**Correct answer: D**

29. If click-and-collect orders are repeatedly late because no one owns afternoon picking, the management action should include:
A. clear ownership and capacity planning for the affected period
B. Hiding collection times
C. Removing online orders from reports
D. Blaming customers for arriving
**Correct answer: A**

30. If conversion remains weak after queue improvements, management should:
A. Declare the problem solved
B. continue diagnosis of other drivers such as availability, offer and service
C. Stop measuring conversion
D. Reduce customer traffic
**Correct answer: B**

31. An after-action review is strongest when it examines:
A. Only failures
B. Only successes
C. expected versus actual results, causes, lessons and next actions
D. Employee personality
**Correct answer: C**

32. An effective improvement that consistently produces better results should be considered for:
A. Deletion
B. Confidentiality from the team
C. Immediate replacement
D. standardisation into normal practice where appropriate
**Correct answer: D**

33. A store action plan contains 20 actions but no priorities. The main weakness is:
A. Resources may be spread without clear focus on highest-impact risks
B. It is too measurable
C. It has too few tasks
D. It will automatically improve everything
**Correct answer: A**

34. Which outcome is more measurable?
A. “Improve queues.”
B. “Reduce average peak waiting time against the defined baseline and review daily.”
C. “Work faster.”
D. “Keep customers happy.”
**Correct answer: B**

35. Recognition is most credible when it is:
A. Given only to favourites
B. Unrelated to performance
C. timely, specific and linked to meaningful behaviour
D. Used instead of coaching
**Correct answer: C**

36. A strong handover should not include:
A. Material unresolved customer matters
B. Stock/POS exceptions
C. Outstanding action owners
D. irrelevant gossip about employees
**Correct answer: D**

37. A manager wants to increase ATV. Which action is appropriate?
A. Improve legitimate needs-based selling and relevant product recommendations
B. Add items without customer agreement
C. Hide prices
D. Prevent low-value purchases
**Correct answer: A**

38. A promotion increases sales volume but reduces margin sharply. The manager should:
A. Look only at units sold
B. review the total commercial effect rather than volume alone
C. Ignore margin
D. Stop measuring promotion performance
**Correct answer: B**

39. When receiving discrepancies repeat with one supplier, a useful response is to:
A. Accept all deliveries without checking
B. Delete discrepancy records
C. compile evidence, strengthen receiving controls and escalate the supplier exception appropriately
D. Accuse the driver personally
**Correct answer: C**

40. Shrinkage control should combine:
A. Accusations and surveillance only
B. Fewer records
C. No stock adjustments
D. sound processes, evidence, access/control discipline and appropriate investigation
**Correct answer: D**

41. A regional manager asks why sales are down. The store manager should:
A. provide material evidence, likely drivers, actions and support needs
B. Say “the team is bad”
C. Blame customers
D. Send raw data with no interpretation
**Correct answer: A**

42. Which meeting agenda is most action-oriented?
A. Introductions → long discussion → close
B. Results → exceptions → causes → actions → decisions → owners → review
C. Complaints → blame → punishment
D. Reports → reports → reports
**Correct answer: B**

43. A technology incident escalation should include:
A. Employee medical information
B. Customer passwords
C. affected service, timestamps/evidence, impact, actions taken and support needed
D. Unverified rumours
**Correct answer: C**

44. A manager should share a confidential employee matter with:
A. The whole team
B. Customers
C. Friends outside work
D. authorised stakeholders with a legitimate need through approved channels
**Correct answer: D**

45. The purpose of a self-assessment against course capabilities is to:
A. identify development priorities and evidence gaps
B. Guarantee a management job
C. Replace employer assessment
D. Produce a perfect score
**Correct answer: A**

46. In the first 30 days of a new management role, a sensible emphasis is to:
A. Change every process immediately
B. learn standards, people, customers, risks, routines and measures
C. Avoid the shop floor
D. Remove existing controls
**Correct answer: B**

47. Continuous improvement differs from uncontrolled change because it:
A. Requires no measurement
B. Avoids standardisation
C. uses evidence, review and disciplined follow-through
D. Changes priorities daily
**Correct answer: C**

48. During peak trading, moving every stock employee to tills may be risky because:
A. More tills are always harmful
B. Stock does not matter during peak
C. Employees cannot change tasks
D. solving queue capacity could create replenishment/availability failures elsewhere
**Correct answer: D**

49. Which capstone submission best demonstrates management thinking?
A. A concise evidence pack showing diagnosis, priorities, decisions, controls, results and learning
B. A list of definitions
C. A report with no owners
D. A copy of the lesson notes
**Correct answer: A**

50. If an action plan requires authority the store manager does not have, the manager should:
A. Proceed secretly
B. escalate with evidence and a clear decision/support request
C. Ignore the issue
D. Alter records
**Correct answer: B**

51. The final simulation primarily assesses whether the learner can:
A. Avoid every unexpected event
B. Achieve perfect sales
C. integrate priorities and make controlled, evidence-based management decisions
D. Work without a team
**Correct answer: C**

52. The strongest end point for the course is:
A. Certification with no further learning
B. Memorising the capstone answers
C. Avoiding future feedback
D. applying the management cycle continuously while building further workplace competence
**Correct answer: D**
