# Module 8 Formative Assessment — Retail Management Capstone & Career Leadership

**Question bank:** 32 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 15 questions per attempt  
**Pass mark:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3

1. The main purpose of the capstone is to:
A. Demonstrate integrated retail-management capability
B. Memorise isolated definitions
C. Replace all workplace experience
D. Test typing speed
**Correct answer: A**

2. A statement that has not yet been verified should be labelled as:
A. A result
B. An assumption
C. A policy
D. A target
**Correct answer: B**

3. A useful store diagnosis should examine:
A. Sales only
B. Employees only
C. Multiple connected performance areas and evidence sources
D. Customer complaints only
**Correct answer: C**

4. High-impact, high-urgency issues should generally be:
A. Ignored until month end
B. Delegated without follow-up
C. Removed from reports
D. Prioritised for prompt management attention
**Correct answer: D**

5. A strong action plan links a problem to:
A. Action, owner, timing, measure and follow-up
B. A slogan
C. An employee name only
D. A lower target
**Correct answer: A**

6. A service standard is easiest to coach when it is:
A. Secret
B. Observable and clearly explained
C. Different for every shift
D. Based on personality
**Correct answer: B**

7. Shift deployment should be based mainly on:
A. Habit
B. Employee friendships
C. Demand, priorities, capability and controls
D. Random rotation
**Correct answer: C**

8. Delegating a task means the manager:
A. Has no further responsibility
B. Cannot monitor progress
C. Must perform it personally
D. Assigns responsibility while retaining appropriate oversight
**Correct answer: D**

9. When sales are below target but traffic is stable, a manager should:
A. Investigate conversion and related operational drivers
B. Assume the economy is the only cause
C. Stop measuring sales
D. Reduce the target immediately
**Correct answer: A**

10. Stock variance should be:
A. Treated as automatic proof of theft
B. Investigated using relevant movement and control evidence
C. Ignored if sales are strong
D. Deleted from the system
**Correct answer: B**

11. A management report should clearly separate evidence from:
A. Dates
B. Store names
C. Interpretation and recommendation
D. Arithmetic
**Correct answer: C**

12. A useful escalation includes:
A. Only the manager's frustration
B. No requested action
C. Unverified allegations
D. Issue, impact, evidence, action taken and support required
**Correct answer: D**

13. Career portfolio statements are strongest when they:
A. Demonstrate capability with credible evidence
B. Use many adjectives
C. Exaggerate experience
D. Hide course simulations
**Correct answer: A**

14. Simulated capstone experience should be presented as:
A. Paid employment
B. Simulation/capstone evidence
C. A customer reference
D. Management tenure
**Correct answer: B**

15. STAR stands for:
A. Sales, Target, Action, Retail
B. Standard, Task, Audit, Review
C. Situation, Task, Action, Result
D. Store, Team, Activity, Record
**Correct answer: C**

16. During a disruption, critical controls should:
A. Always be suspended
B. Be replaced by personal shortcuts
C. Be ignored until close
D. Remain protected through approved processes and continuity arrangements
**Correct answer: D**

17. The first step in the capstone management cycle is to:
A. Observe the situation
B. Blame someone
C. Change the target
D. Write the final report
**Correct answer: A**

18. Root-cause analysis helps managers:
A. Prove the first assumption
B. address underlying contributors rather than symptoms alone
C. Avoid collecting evidence
D. Eliminate all risk
**Correct answer: B**

19. An operational measure should be selected because it:
A. Looks impressive
B. Is easy to collect
C. shows whether the priority or intervention is improving
D. Is used by every competitor
**Correct answer: C**

20. A handover is important because it:
A. Replaces the next manager
B. Removes accountability
C. Is mainly a social conversation
D. transfers material outstanding information and responsibilities
**Correct answer: D**

21. A markdown decision should consider:
A. Approved authority and margin/profit implications
B. Only customer requests
C. Employee preference
D. Competitor rumours
**Correct answer: A**

22. If stock exists in the backroom but not on shelf, the first commercial response should examine:
A. Payroll
B. replenishment execution and ownership
C. Store rent
D. Recruitment
**Correct answer: B**

23. A productive management meeting should end with:
A. More data only
B. No disagreements
C. decisions, owners, deadlines and review points
D. A longer agenda
**Correct answer: C**

24. Confidential information should be shared:
A. With anyone attending a meeting
B. Through personal messaging groups
C. Whenever convenient
D. only with authorised stakeholders for legitimate purposes
**Correct answer: D**

25. A 90-day career plan is useful because it:
A. Structures learning, execution and development priorities
B. Guarantees promotion
C. Replaces performance management
D. Removes the need for feedback
**Correct answer: A**

26. In a final simulation, a manager should respond to changing conditions by:
A. Abandoning the original priorities completely
B. reassessing risk and reallocating resources while protecting controls
C. Ignoring new events
D. Performing every task personally
**Correct answer: B**

27. An after-action review asks:
A. Who can be blamed?
B. How can the report be shortened?
C. what happened, what worked, what failed, why and what should change
D. Whether targets should be removed
**Correct answer: C**

28. Continuous improvement means:
A. Changing procedures every day
B. Never standardising anything
C. Waiting for annual audits
D. repeatedly measuring, learning and strengthening performance
**Correct answer: D**

29. An action can be completed but ineffective when:
A. The task was done but the intended outcome did not improve
B. It has an owner
C. It was documented
D. It was reviewed
**Correct answer: A**

30. A manager's professional portfolio should exclude:
A. Genuine certificates
B. unprotected confidential customer/employer information
C. Capstone evidence clearly labelled as simulated
D. Development plans
**Correct answer: B**

31. The strongest final capstone evidence demonstrates:
A. Perfect results in every area
B. No need for escalation
C. sound reasoning, controlled action, measurement and learning
D. The longest possible report
**Correct answer: C**

32. The management improvement cycle ends by:
A. Stopping measurement
B. Removing ownership
C. Assuming success continues
D. reviewing, improving and standardising effective practice before the next cycle
**Correct answer: D**
