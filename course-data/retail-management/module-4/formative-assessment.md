# Module 4 Formative Assessment — Team Leadership & People Management

**Delivery:** 15 questions randomly selected from this 32-question bank  
**Pass mark:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3

1. What is the strongest first step when a new retail leader takes responsibility for a team?
A. Clarify expectations, roles and priorities
B. Replace existing routines immediately
C. Avoid discussing performance
D. Focus only on sales
**Answer: A**

2. A performance standard should primarily tell an employee:
A. Who is most popular on the team
B. What acceptable performance looks like
C. Which colleagues earn more
D. How to avoid all feedback
**Answer: B**

3. Effective delegation requires the manager to:
A. Transfer all accountability permanently
B. Give vague instructions
C. Explain the task, expected result and authority
D. Complete the task personally afterwards
**Answer: C**

4. Which is the best example of constructive feedback?
A. “You always get everything wrong.”
B. “Everyone is unhappy with you.”
C. “Try harder next time.”
D. Describe the observed behaviour, its impact and the required improvement
**Answer: D**

5. Recognition is most effective when it is:
A. Specific and linked to useful behaviour or results
B. Given only once a year
C. Used instead of fair performance management
D. Reserved for managers
**Answer: A**

6. When allocating shift work, a manager should primarily consider:
A. Friendship groups
B. Trading needs, required skills and fair allocation
C. Personal preference only
D. Who complains least
**Answer: B**

7. Coaching differs from simply giving an instruction because coaching:
A. Removes responsibility from the employee
B. Guarantees promotion
C. develops understanding and capability through guidance and practice
D. avoids discussing standards
**Answer: C**

8. A manager facing a team conflict should first:
A. Publicly blame one person
B. Ignore it until it disappears
C. Transfer both employees
D. Establish the facts and address the issue impartially
**Answer: D**

9. Psychological safety in a retail team is strengthened when leaders:
A. Allow respectful questions and concerns to be raised
B. Punish every disagreement
C. Hide operational problems
D. discourage feedback
**Answer: A**

10. A useful pre-shift briefing should include:
A. Private employee disciplinary history
B. priorities, targets, risks and role allocation
C. unrelated personal matters
D. only yesterday’s sales
**Answer: B**

11. Which action best supports on-the-job development?
A. Giving difficult tasks without guidance
B. Waiting for annual training only
C. Demonstration, supervised practice and feedback
D. Removing all responsibility
**Answer: C**

12. When discussing poor performance, a manager should avoid:
A. Using evidence
B. Allowing the employee to respond
C. Agreeing improvement actions
D. humiliating the employee in front of customers or colleagues
**Answer: D**

13. Team engagement is more likely when employees:
A. Understand how their work contributes to store goals
B. Never receive information
C. Have no clear standards
D. compete for basic information
**Answer: A**

14. A fair attendance-management approach begins with:
A. automatic dismissal
B. accurate records and understanding the circumstances
C. public criticism
D. reducing pay informally
**Answer: B**

15. A manager should escalate a people matter when:
A. it is inconvenient
B. the manager dislikes the employee
C. policy, authority or seriousness requires HR/senior support
D. a customer is nearby
**Answer: C**

16. Which is the best example of measurable performance evidence?
A. “I have a feeling.”
B. workplace gossip
C. personal popularity
D. documented attendance, task, service or KPI records
**Answer: D**

17. A manager who consistently models the expected standard is demonstrating:
A. leadership by example
B. favouritism
C. avoidance
D. micromanagement automatically
**Answer: A**

18. Why should delegated work be followed up?
A. To take the task back immediately
B. To confirm progress, quality and support needs
C. To prove distrust
D. To remove employee ownership
**Answer: B**

19. What is the strongest response when an employee makes a reasonable mistake while learning a new task?
A. Immediate public punishment
B. Permanent removal from all tasks
C. Correct, coach and provide appropriate supervised practice
D. Ignore the mistake completely
**Answer: C**

20. Fair workplace process requires managers to:
A. decide outcomes before hearing facts
B. use rumours as evidence
C. apply different rules secretly
D. establish facts and follow applicable policy and procedure
**Answer: D**

21. Motivation is best supported by:
A. clear purpose, fair treatment, feedback and meaningful recognition
B. threats as the main management tool
C. unclear expectations
D. constant competition between colleagues
**Answer: A**

22. A manager notices one employee carrying much more work than others. The best response is to:
A. praise the employee and do nothing else
B. review workload allocation and rebalance it appropriately
C. hide the imbalance
D. add more work to the same employee
**Answer: B**

23. A difficult conversation should normally be held:
A. at the till during peak trade
B. in front of customers
C. privately and professionally, with relevant facts available
D. through workplace gossip
**Answer: C**

24. The purpose of an improvement plan is to:
A. guarantee punishment
B. embarrass the employee
C. replace normal supervision
D. define required improvement, support, measures and review points
**Answer: D**

25. When employees understand decision boundaries, delegation improves because:
A. they know what they may decide and when to escalate
B. they never need management support
C. policies no longer apply
D. all employees become managers
**Answer: A**

26. Which practice helps reduce misunderstanding between shifts?
A. Relying on memory
B. Structured handovers with important outstanding actions and exceptions
C. Avoiding written records
D. Removing accountability
**Answer: B**

27. A high-performance retail team combines:
A. sales pressure only
B. friendship only
C. clear standards, capability, accountability and constructive leadership
D. no performance measurement
**Answer: C**

28. If an employee raises a serious concern, the manager should:
A. promise secrecy regardless of the issue
B. dismiss it immediately
C. discuss it publicly
D. listen, record relevant facts and escalate according to policy where required
**Answer: D**

29. Why should managers distinguish misconduct from capability or performance problems?
A. They may require different management responses and procedures
B. They are always identical
C. Neither requires evidence
D. Only misconduct matters
**Answer: A**

30. What is a practical indicator that coaching has worked?
A. The employee attended the conversation
B. The employee can perform the required task more consistently to standard
C. The manager spoke for a long time
D. A form was printed
**Answer: B**

31. When planning staffing for a busy promotion, the manager should:
A. copy a quiet-day roster
B. schedule everyone identically
C. align coverage and skills with expected customer demand and operational tasks
D. ignore breaks and role requirements
**Answer: C**

32. Which statement best reflects responsible people leadership?
A. Results matter but fairness does not
B. Fairness matters but results do not
C. Policies should be applied only when convenient
D. Managers must pursue performance while treating people fairly and following applicable rules
**Answer: D**
