# Module 4 Summative Assessment Bank — Team Leadership & People Management

**Delivery:** 25 questions randomly selected from this 52-question bank  
**Pass mark:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3

1. A newly promoted supervisor wants immediate credibility. What is the best approach?
A. Set clear expectations, act consistently and model required standards
B. Change every process on day one
C. Avoid making decisions
D. Use authority to win arguments
**Answer: A**

2. Which statement best describes accountability?
A. Delegating a task removes all manager responsibility
B. People understand expected results and are answerable for agreed responsibilities
C. Only senior managers are accountable
D. Accountability means punishment
**Answer: B**

3. A good performance expectation should be:
A. based on personality
B. intentionally flexible and hidden
C. clear, job-relevant and capable of being observed or measured
D. changed after the work is completed
**Answer: C**

4. A manager needs a cashier to train on a new procedure. What should the manager do?
A. Send an unexplained manual
B. Assume prior knowledge
C. Criticise mistakes publicly
D. Explain, demonstrate, allow practice and give feedback
**Answer: D**

5. Which action most improves a shift plan?
A. Match staffing and skills to workload, trading patterns and critical tasks
B. Allocate roles randomly
C. Ignore peak periods
D. Keep the same plan regardless of demand
**Answer: A**

6. Delegation fails most predictably when:
A. follow-up points are agreed
B. expected outcomes and authority are unclear
C. resources are available
D. employees understand escalation boundaries
**Answer: B**

7. An employee repeatedly misses a service standard. The manager’s best first management response is to:
A. label the employee lazy
B. discuss it with other employees
C. establish facts, clarify the standard and identify the cause
D. immediately remove the employee
**Answer: C**

8. Effective coaching is primarily intended to:
A. document blame
B. replace all formal training
C. increase dependence on the manager
D. improve capability and performance
**Answer: D**

9. Recognition should ideally be:
A. timely, sincere and connected to specific contribution
B. unrelated to performance
C. available only to top sellers
D. used to avoid difficult feedback
**Answer: A**

10. Which management behaviour is most likely to damage trust?
A. explaining decisions
B. applying standards inconsistently because of favouritism
C. keeping commitments
D. admitting a mistake
**Answer: B**

11. A disagreement between two employees begins affecting customers. The manager should:
A. ignore it
B. choose a favourite employee
C. intervene, establish facts and facilitate a professional resolution
D. discuss it with customers
**Answer: C**

12. During a difficult conversation, the manager should:
A. rely on hearsay
B. threaten the employee
C. speak only about personality
D. focus on facts, standards, impact and required next steps
**Answer: D**

13. Why are pre-shift briefings valuable?
A. They align the team on priorities, targets, risks and responsibilities
B. They replace all training
C. They should contain confidential disciplinary details
D. They eliminate the need for managers on the floor
**Answer: A**

14. What is the strongest basis for performance feedback?
A. rumours
B. specific observed or recorded evidence
C. personal preference
D. assumptions about attitude
**Answer: B**

15. An employee has not understood a delegated task. The manager should:
A. wait for failure
B. complain to others
C. clarify the outcome and check understanding before work proceeds
D. take all future work away
**Answer: C**

16. Which behaviour supports psychological safety?
A. mocking questions
B. punishing respectful disagreement
C. concealing errors
D. responding constructively when employees raise genuine concerns
**Answer: D**

17. What is a useful purpose of a one-to-one check-in?
A. Review progress, obstacles, feedback and development needs
B. Replace every team meeting
C. discuss other employees’ confidential matters
D. avoid measurable goals
**Answer: A**

18. A manager should use workplace policies because they:
A. guarantee identical outcomes in every situation
B. provide approved standards, procedures and decision boundaries
C. remove the need for judgement
D. replace applicable law
**Answer: B**

19. Why distinguish a skills gap from unwillingness to perform?
A. There is no difference
B. Both always require punishment
C. The cause affects the appropriate management response
D. Skills cannot be developed
**Answer: C**

20. A fair investigation should avoid:
A. collecting relevant records
B. hearing the employee’s explanation
C. considering context
D. deciding guilt before establishing the facts
**Answer: D**

21. A strong team leader monitors workload because:
A. overload or poor allocation can damage service, safety and performance
B. workload never affects performance
C. only employees control workload
D. it eliminates the need for planning
**Answer: A**

22. A manager wants to improve employee ownership. Which action is strongest?
A. make every decision personally
B. delegate meaningful responsibility with clear boundaries
C. remove access to information
D. correct every minor action immediately
**Answer: B**

23. A team member performs a task correctly after coaching. What should happen next?
A. ignore the improvement
B. change the standard
C. reinforce the correct behaviour and continue appropriate follow-up
D. immediately give a warning
**Answer: C**

24. When conflict becomes threatening or involves serious allegations, the manager should:
A. mediate casually regardless of risk
B. post about it in a group chat
C. promise a particular outcome
D. follow required safety, HR and escalation procedures
**Answer: D**

25. What is the clearest sign of a well-run delegation cycle?
A. Clear outcome, suitable person, authority/resources, check-ins and completion review
B. A task is mentioned once
C. The manager secretly completes the task
D. Nobody knows who owns the work
**Answer: A**

26. Which is the best way to make a performance conversation less subjective?
A. discuss personality traits
B. compare evidence against an established standard
C. use team gossip
D. avoid dates and examples
**Answer: B**

27. An employee challenges a manager’s idea respectfully and provides useful evidence. A strong leader should:
A. treat it as misconduct automatically
B. end the conversation
C. consider the evidence and respond professionally
D. reduce the employee’s responsibilities
**Answer: C**

28. Why are written handovers important?
A. They make verbal communication illegal
B. They guarantee no errors
C. They replace supervision
D. They preserve key information, ownership and outstanding actions across shifts
**Answer: D**

29. A retail manager notices declining morale. The best diagnostic approach is to:
A. examine evidence, speak with the team and identify likely causes before acting
B. organise a party immediately
C. assume employees are ungrateful
D. ignore it unless sales collapse
**Answer: A**

30. Which is an example of intrinsic motivation?
A. commission only
B. pride in mastering a role and contributing to team success
C. a once-off voucher only
D. avoiding a warning
**Answer: B**

31. When should performance expectations be clarified?
A. only after failure
B. only at annual review
C. before and during the work, especially when roles or priorities change
D. never if employees are experienced
**Answer: C**

32. A manager receives confidential employee information. The correct approach is to:
A. share it with the whole team
B. discuss it socially
C. leave it visible
D. handle it only for legitimate purposes and according to approved controls
**Answer: D**

33. Why should managers document significant performance discussions appropriately?
A. To create an accurate record of issues, expectations, support and agreed actions
B. To embarrass employees
C. To avoid speaking to employees
D. To guarantee dismissal
**Answer: A**

34. A roster consistently gives undesirable shifts to one employee without a business reason. This creates a risk of:
A. better engagement
B. perceived unfairness and damaged trust
C. automatic higher sales
D. improved coaching
**Answer: B**

35. A useful development goal should identify:
A. only a course name
B. a vague ambition
C. capability to improve, action to take and evidence of progress
D. another employee’s weakness
**Answer: C**

36. What should a manager do after assigning an unfamiliar high-risk task?
A. provide no supervision
B. assume competence
C. lower all standards
D. ensure required competence, instruction and appropriate oversight
**Answer: D**

37. A high-performance culture requires:
A. both results and responsible behaviour
B. results at any cost
C. no measurement
D. competition without cooperation
**Answer: A**

38. A customer rush creates pressure and an employee becomes visibly overwhelmed. The manager should:
A. criticise the employee publicly
B. stabilise workload and provide operational support while maintaining standards
C. send everyone home
D. ignore the situation
**Answer: B**

39. Why should a leader explain the reason behind an important change?
A. Employees must agree with every decision
B. Explanations eliminate resistance completely
C. Understanding purpose can improve alignment and implementation
D. Policies become optional
**Answer: C**

40. Which action best protects fairness in a conduct matter?
A. Different standards for favourites
B. Public accusations
C. predetermined sanction
D. consistent principles, relevant facts and appropriate procedure
**Answer: D**

41. A manager who asks “What support do you need to meet this standard?” is demonstrating:
A. performance coaching
B. avoidance
C. favouritism
D. lack of standards
**Answer: A**

42. What is the primary purpose of corrective action?
A. revenge
B. address unacceptable behaviour/performance and support required correction where appropriate
C. entertain the team
D. remove all discretion
**Answer: B**

43. An employee’s performance drops after a process change. What should the manager investigate?
A. only attitude
B. only attendance
C. whether expectations, training, resources or process understanding contributed
D. nothing; performance is solely personal
**Answer: C**

44. A team meeting is most productive when it:
A. has no purpose
B. focuses on gossip
C. excludes action owners
D. has clear objectives, relevant discussion, decisions and actions
**Answer: D**

45. Which practice supports consistent management across supervisors?
A. Shared standards, documented procedures and aligned leadership expectations
B. Each supervisor inventing separate rules
C. Avoiding handovers
D. keeping policies secret
**Answer: A**

46. A manager must give negative feedback. Which opening is strongest?
A. “Everyone thinks you are a problem.”
B. “I want to discuss the specific standard and what I observed on yesterday’s shift.”
C. “You never do anything properly.”
D. “Guess why you are here.”
**Answer: B**

47. Why should a manager allow an employee to respond during a serious workplace discussion?
A. It guarantees agreement
B. It transfers the decision to the employee
C. Relevant context and the employee’s explanation are important to a fair, informed process
D. It removes the need for evidence
**Answer: C**

48. What is the best response to a minor operational error that reveals a widespread process misunderstanding?
A. discipline one employee and stop there
B. hide the error
C. lower the standard
D. correct the immediate issue and address the wider training/process gap
**Answer: D**

49. Which is the strongest succession-development practice?
A. Give emerging employees progressively more complex responsibilities with coaching and review
B. Wait until a manager resigns
C. Keep all knowledge with one person
D. Promote without assessing readiness
**Answer: A**

50. What does fair recognition require?
A. everyone receives identical praise regardless of contribution
B. recognition criteria are credible and applied consistently
C. only financial rewards matter
D. managers recognise friends first
**Answer: B**

51. A manager wants a team to solve more day-to-day problems independently. The best approach is to:
A. stop providing standards
B. let employees ignore policy
C. teach problem-solving, define decision boundaries and coach judgement
D. withdraw all support
**Answer: C**

52. Which statement best summarises responsible retail people management?
A. People management is separate from store performance
B. Strong sales excuse poor leadership
C. Managers should avoid difficult conversations
D. Sustainable performance comes from clear standards, capable people, fair leadership, evidence-based decisions and appropriate accountability
**Answer: D**
