# Module 5 Formative Assessment — Retail Finance & Commercial Performance

**Question bank:** 32 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 15 questions per attempt  
**Pass mark:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Correct-answer distribution:** A=8, B=8, C=8, D=8

1. Which statement best describes retail revenue?
A. Value generated from sales before relevant deductions and costs are analysed
B. Cash physically held in the safe
C. Gross profit after labour cost
D. Stock value at cost
**Answer: A**

2. A store sells 100 units at R50 each. Sales revenue is:
A. R500
B. R5,000
C. R50,000
D. R150
**Answer: B**

3. Gross profit is generally calculated as:
A. Selling price plus cost
B. Sales divided by transactions
C. Sales revenue less cost of goods sold
D. Units sold less stock received
**Answer: C**

4. Which is the best reason for managers to distinguish margin from mark-up?
A. They are always legally identical
B. Both use sales as the denominator
C. Mark-up is only used for wages
D. They use different bases and can produce different percentages
**Answer: D**

5. If an item sells for R200 and costs R120, gross profit per unit is:
A. R80
B. R120
C. R200
D. R320
**Answer: A**

6. A budget variance is:
A. The store's bank balance
B. The difference between actual and planned performance
C. A supplier invoice
D. A customer refund
**Answer: B**

7. Which management response is strongest when sales are below budget?
A. Blame the team immediately
B. Reduce all prices
C. Diagnose the drivers and controllable causes before selecting action
D. Ignore the result until month end
**Answer: C**

8. Labour productivity should be managed by:
A. Minimising staffing regardless of service
B. Scheduling the same hours every day
C. Looking only at wage cost
D. Matching productive labour to workload while protecting service and controls
**Answer: D**

9. ATV means:
A. Average transaction value
B. Annual trading variance
C. Available total volume
D. Adjusted till value
**Answer: A**

10. ATV is calculated as:
A. Units divided by sales
B. Sales value divided by transactions
C. Transactions divided by traffic
D. Gross profit divided by units
**Answer: B**

11. UPT is calculated as:
A. Traffic divided by units
B. Sales divided by staff
C. Units sold divided by transactions
D. Transactions divided by units
**Answer: C**

12. Conversion normally compares transactions with:
A. Gross margin
B. Stock received
C. Labour hours
D. Relevant customer traffic or selling opportunities under an approved definition
**Answer: D**

13. Why should a manager use ATV, UPT and conversion together?
A. They describe different components of trading performance
B. They always have the same target
C. They eliminate the need for observation
D. They measure only payroll
**Answer: A**

14. Ethical basket building means:
A. Selling the highest-priced item regardless of need
B. Recommending relevant products that genuinely support the customer's need
C. Hiding cheaper alternatives
D. Requiring every customer to buy two items
**Answer: B**

15. A promotion should primarily be evaluated against:
A. How colourful the signage was
B. Whether staff enjoyed it
C. Its intended commercial objective and resulting performance
D. The number of posters printed
**Answer: C**

16. If a R500 item is discounted by 20%, the new selling price is:
A. R300
B. R450
C. R480
D. R400
**Answer: D**

17. If an item sells for R400 and costs R300, gross profit per unit is:
A. R100
B. R300
C. R400
D. R700
**Answer: A**

18. Why can a promotion increase sales but reduce gross profit quality?
A. More transactions always remove cost
B. Discounting may reduce profit per unit significantly
C. Promotions prevent stock movement
D. Sales and gross profit are identical
**Answer: B**

19. Which is an operational promotion risk?
A. Accurate POS setup
B. Sufficient replenishment
C. Incorrect tickets or unavailable promotional stock
D. Clear staff briefing
**Answer: C**

20. Markdown decisions should:
A. Be made secretly by any employee
B. Ignore product cost
C. Always reduce price by 50%
D. Follow approved authority and evidence-based commercial reasoning
**Answer: D**

21. The purpose of separation of duties is to:
A. Reduce the risk of one person controlling incompatible sensitive steps
B. Prevent teamwork
C. Increase queue time
D. Remove manager accountability
**Answer: A**

22. A till variance should initially be treated as:
A. Automatic proof of theft
B. An exception requiring factual review under procedure
C. A reason to dismiss an employee immediately
D. A marketing expense
**Answer: B**

23. Which transaction may warrant controlled exception review?
A. Standard shelf replenishment
B. Normal customer greeting
C. Refund or void
D. Cleaning a fixture
**Answer: C**

24. Password sharing at a POS is risky because it:
A. Improves accountability
B. Makes reports more accurate
C. Reduces fraud opportunities
D. Weakens traceability of who performed transactions
**Answer: D**

25. A balanced retail scorecard should include:
A. Multiple relevant commercial, customer, stock, operational and people measures
B. Only total sales
C. Only staff attendance
D. Only customer complaints
**Answer: A**

26. A leading or diagnostic measure is useful because it can:
A. Replace all financial results
B. Help explain or anticipate performance outcomes
C. Guarantee future sales
D. Eliminate management judgement
**Answer: B**

27. When prioritising scorecard variances, a manager should consider:
A. Which number is easiest to fix
B. Only the colour on the dashboard
C. impact, trend, risk, urgency and controllability
D. employee popularity
**Answer: C**

28. Which action-plan element identifies responsibility?
A. Variance
B. Benchmark
C. Margin
D. Owner
**Answer: D**

29. Which sequence best represents a sound management control rhythm?
A. Review → diagnose → prioritise → act → verify
B. Act → blame → report → ignore
C. Discount → sell → forget → repeat
D. Recruit → dismiss → budget → count
**Answer: A**

30. Sales are below target, ATV is strong, but conversion and availability are weak. The manager should first investigate:
A. Whether baskets are too large
B. barriers to conversion and product availability
C. only the selling price
D. only payroll tax
**Answer: B**

31. Why is data quality important when reading a scorecard?
A. Poor data always increases sales
B. Managers should never question reports
C. Incomplete or inconsistent data can lead to incorrect decisions
D. It removes the need for targets
**Answer: C**

32. The strongest purpose of commercial KPIs is to:
A. create more reports
B. punish low performers automatically
C. replace customer observation
D. help managers diagnose performance and choose evidence-based actions
**Answer: D**
