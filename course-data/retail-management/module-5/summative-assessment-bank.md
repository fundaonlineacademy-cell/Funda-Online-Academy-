1. A retail manager is reviewing whether the store achieved its commercial objective. Which figure most directly represents value from sales activity before cost analysis?
A. Sales revenue
B. Stock variance
C. Labour hours
D. Till float
**Answer: A**

2. A store sells 750 units at an average selling price of R80. Revenue is:
A. R6,000
B. R60,000
C. R600,000
D. R830
**Answer: B**

3. Which formula best represents gross profit in a simplified retail example?
A. Cost of goods sold minus revenue
B. Revenue plus cost of goods sold
C. Revenue minus cost of goods sold
D. Revenue divided by labour hours
**Answer: C**

4. An item costs R240 and sells for R400. Gross profit per unit is:
A. R640
B. R240
C. R400
D. R160
**Answer: D**

5. Which statement about margin and mark-up is correct?
A. Their percentage denominators differ
B. They are always the same percentage
C. Neither relates to cost
D. Margin is calculated only from units
**Answer: A**

6. A manager receives a sales target of R1,200,000 and actual sales of R1,080,000. The most useful next step is to:
A. Assume the team did not work hard
B. analyse the R120,000 adverse variance and its drivers
C. remove all targets
D. increase every product price
**Answer: B**

7. Which is the best example of a controllable store cost?
A. National interest-rate policy
B. A competitor opening nationally
C. avoidable overtime created by poor local scheduling, within policy
D. Exchange rates set by markets
**Answer: C**

8. Labour productivity management should balance:
A. minimum staffing and maximum queues
B. wage cost only
C. headcount only
D. workload, productive hours, service, safety and control needs
**Answer: D**

9. A store records R225,000 sales from 1,500 transactions. ATV is:
A. R150
B. R1,500
C. R225
D. R3,375
**Answer: A**

10. A store sells 3,600 units through 2,000 transactions. UPT is:
A. 0.56
B. 1.8
C. 2.0
D. 5.6
**Answer: B**

11. If 800 transactions result from 2,500 relevant visits, conversion is:
A. 3.2%
B. 25%
C. 32%
D. 80%
**Answer: C**

12. Why must the traffic denominator be consistently defined when calculating conversion?
A. It determines product cost
B. It changes gross margin
C. It sets employee wages
D. Inconsistent traffic definitions can distort the rate
**Answer: D**

13. Traffic increases strongly while transactions remain almost flat. Which KPI deserves particular investigation?
A. Conversion
B. Opening float
C. Supplier account number
D. Payroll frequency
**Answer: A**

14. Conversion is weak, ATV is strong and UPT is on target. Which response is most appropriate?
A. Force every buyer to purchase an extra unit
B. investigate why customer opportunities are not becoming transactions
C. focus only on increasing ATV
D. ignore customer traffic
**Answer: B**

15. Which is a responsible way to improve UPT?
A. Add unwanted items without consent
B. Hide single-item options
C. improve relevant complementary recommendations and availability
D. penalise customers who buy one item
**Answer: C**

16. Why should commercial selling remain customer-centred?
A. Customer needs are irrelevant to profitability
B. It guarantees every transaction
C. It eliminates stock-outs
D. Trust and relevant value support sustainable performance
**Answer: D**

17. A 10% discount on R800 equals:
A. R80
B. R8
C. R720
D. R880
**Answer: A**

18. After the 10% discount in Question 17, the selling price is:
A. R80
B. R720
C. R800
D. R880
**Answer: B**

19. An item costs R500 and normally sells for R800. If it is discounted to R650, promotional gross profit per unit is:
A. R300
B. R650
C. R150
D. R1,150
**Answer: C**

20. Why can discounting require a large increase in volume to maintain gross-profit amount?
A. Unit cost always disappears
B. Transactions become free
C. Revenue is irrelevant
D. gross profit earned on each discounted unit may be materially lower

21. A promotion intended to clear seasonal inventory should be reviewed against:
A. stock movement plus its broader commercial and margin effects
B. sales value only
C. poster quantity only
D. employee attendance only

22. Which is most likely to undermine an otherwise good promotion?
A. Accurate POS prices
B. repeated stock-outs on promoted lines
C. correct tickets
D. staff understanding the offer

23. Which information is useful before recommending a markdown?
A. Manager's favourite colour
B. Employee birthdays

**Answer: C**

24. Unauthorised price reductions are problematic primarily because they:
A. always increase profit
B. improve auditability

25. Which principle best supports secure till operation?
A. Individual credentials and controlled permissions
B. Shared passwords

27. Which could legitimately cause a till variance?

29. Which is a typical transaction exception for management review?
