# Professional Certificate in Retail Management & Team Leadership — Rebuild Plan

## Course standard
Rebuild the existing 8-module / 32-lesson course to 8 modules × 8 substantive lessons = 64 lessons, followed by a 32-question formative bank and 52-question summative bank for every module. Formative delivery: 15 questions. Summative delivery: 25 questions. Pass standard: 70%. Maximum attempts: 3.

The programme is designed for South African retail supervisors, department leaders, assistant managers and aspiring store managers. It emphasises operational control, customer experience, people leadership, commercial judgement, stock, loss prevention, POS/data use and practical store-management decision-making.

## Lesson quality standard
Each lesson normally includes estimated study time, learning outcomes, why the lesson matters, key terms, structured teaching content, realistic South African retail examples, practical activity, knowledge check with feedback, key takeaways and visual learning guidance. Narrow lessons may be about 500–700 words; deeper lessons may be 700–1,000+ words where justified. Do not pad lessons to hit a word count.

## South African grounding
The course should reflect realistic South African store conditions and terminology. Retail-manager responsibilities should include product mix/stock levels, service standards, financial records/budgets, staff supervision and occupational safety awareness, consistent with the occupational role described by Statistics South Africa. Wholesale/retail learning should meaningfully cover customer service and stock control. Current legal or regulatory specifics must be verified before exact claims are taught.
