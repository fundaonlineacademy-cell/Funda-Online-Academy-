# Module 3 Formative Assessment — Customer Experience & Service Leadership

**Question bank:** 32 multiple-choice questions  
**Delivery:** 15 questions  
**Pass mark:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3

1. Who owns customer experience at store-management level?
A. The manager and team through daily execution
B. Only head office
C. Only security
D. Only customers
**Correct answer: A**

2. A moment of truth is best described as:
A. A stock count
B. An interaction that shapes the customer's view of the retailer
C. A payroll calculation
D. A supplier invoice
**Correct answer: B**

3. The best first step when discovering a customer's need is to:
A. Recommend the most expensive item immediately
B. Avoid questions
C. Ask relevant questions and listen carefully
D. Refer every customer to a manager
**Correct answer: C**

4. When a customer complains, the employee should first:
A. Argue about who is responsible
B. Promise any remedy requested
C. Ignore emotion and continue working
D. Listen, clarify the issue and follow the approved process
**Correct answer: D**

5. Consistent service standards are important because they:
A. Give teams a clear and repeatable expectation
B. Remove all need for judgement
C. Guarantee every customer will buy
D. Replace training
**Correct answer: A**

6. A service-recovery response should normally be based on:
A. The employee's personal preference
B. Facts, policy, authority and an appropriate remedy
C. The customer's social-media following
D. The value of the employee's sales target
**Correct answer: B**

7. Which behaviour best demonstrates active listening?
A. Interrupting quickly
B. Looking at another task
C. Summarising the customer's concern to confirm understanding
D. Assuming the solution before hearing the details
**Correct answer: C**

8. An omnichannel customer may:
A. Use only cash
B. Shop only during weekends
C. Never enter a physical store
D. Research online and collect or return through a store
**Correct answer: D**

9. A customer-service standard should be:
A. Observable and understandable by employees
B. Secret from employees
C. Different for every shift without reason
D. Based only on individual personality
**Correct answer: A**

10. If a complaint exceeds an employee's authority, the employee should:
A. Invent an exception
B. Escalate through the approved channel
C. Tell the customer nothing can be done
D. Delete the complaint
**Correct answer: B**

11. The purpose of needs discovery is to:
A. Delay the interaction
B. Avoid product knowledge
C. understand what outcome the customer is trying to achieve
D. maximise queue length
**Correct answer: C**

12. A difficult customer situation should be managed by:
A. matching the customer's anger
B. threatening the customer
C. making unsupported legal claims
D. remaining calm, setting boundaries and escalating risk appropriately
**Correct answer: D**

13. Which measure can help a manager identify recurring service problems?
A. Complaint themes
B. Employee shoe size
C. Supplier logo colour
D. Store music volume alone
**Correct answer: A**

14. Coaching for service consistency should focus on:
A. blame after every error
B. expected behaviour, observed gaps, practice and follow-up
C. avoiding feedback
D. only top performers
**Correct answer: B**

15. A customer who is angry should automatically receive:
A. a cash refund regardless of facts
B. a free product
C. respectful attention and the remedy supported by applicable policy and rights
D. access to staff-only areas
**Correct answer: C**

16. Why should service incidents be documented when required?
A. To create unnecessary paperwork
B. To embarrass staff
C. To replace customer communication
D. To support follow-up, accountability and trend analysis
**Correct answer: D**

17. Customer experience includes:
A. the combined effect of interactions across the shopping journey
B. only the checkout interaction
C. only advertising
D. only product price
**Correct answer: A**

18. A queue problem is best managed by:
A. ignoring it until closing
B. monitoring demand and deploying approved capacity responses
C. blaming customers
D. closing additional tills
**Correct answer: B**

19. Which is an example of poor service communication?
A. Confirming what the customer needs
B. Explaining next steps
C. Using dismissive language and unexplained jargon
D. Checking understanding
**Correct answer: C**

20. If a return request may involve statutory consumer rights, staff should:
A. rely on personal opinion
B. automatically reject it
C. create a new store rule
D. follow current approved policy and escalate legal uncertainty
**Correct answer: D**

21. The manager's role in service culture includes:
A. modelling the expected behaviour
B. delegating all customer matters permanently
C. avoiding the shop floor
D. measuring sales only
**Correct answer: A**

22. A useful service KPI should:
A. be unrelated to customer outcomes
B. help reveal performance or experience trends
C. replace observation completely
D. be changed every day
**Correct answer: B**

23. The best response to a recurring complaint theme is to:
A. hide the records
B. blame one employee immediately
C. investigate root causes and implement corrective action
D. stop accepting complaints
**Correct answer: C**

24. During a threatening customer incident, the priority is to:
A. win the argument
B. protect the sale at any cost
C. continue normal service regardless of risk
D. protect safety and use approved escalation/security procedures
**Correct answer: D**

25. Good service recovery can:
A. restore trust when a problem is handled fairly and effectively
B. guarantee future purchases
C. remove all company policies
D. eliminate every operational error
**Correct answer: A**

26. Product knowledge helps customer service because it:
A. allows staff to pressure customers
B. supports accurate and relevant guidance
C. removes the need to listen
D. guarantees stock availability
**Correct answer: B**

27. A service briefing before peak trading should include:
A. private employee gossip
B. unrelated financial history
C. priorities, likely pressure points, roles and escalation routes
D. only yesterday's weather
**Correct answer: C**

28. Which approach is strongest when a customer says, “Nobody is helping me”?
A. Explain that the store is busy
B. Tell them to find another employee
C. Challenge the statement
D. acknowledge the concern, clarify the need and take ownership of the next step
**Correct answer: D**

29. Mystery-shopper or customer-feedback data is most useful when managers:
A. combine it with other evidence and coach specific behaviours
B. treat one result as absolute truth
C. keep it secret from relevant supervisors
D. use it only for punishment
**Correct answer: A**

30. An employee should avoid promising a customer an outcome when:
A. the customer is polite
B. the outcome is outside the employee's authority or not yet verified
C. the store is quiet
D. the product is inexpensive
**Correct answer: B**

31. A service-improvement plan should identify:
A. only a slogan
B. only the manager's opinion
C. the problem, action, owner, measure and review point
D. no deadlines or ownership
**Correct answer: C**

32. The strongest service culture is created when:
A. standards exist only in a manual
B. managers speak about service once a year
C. complaints are hidden
D. leaders model, coach, measure and reinforce customer-focused execution
**Correct answer: D**
