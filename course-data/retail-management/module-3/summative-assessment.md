# Module 3 Summative Assessment — Customer Experience & Service Leadership

**Question bank:** 52 multiple-choice questions  
**Delivery:** 25 questions  
**Pass mark:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3

1. Customer experience is best understood as:
A. the combined customer perception formed across interactions with the retailer
B. a single greeting
C. only the refund process
D. only advertising
**Correct answer: A**

2. A manager improves service consistency primarily by:
A. relying on personality
B. setting, modelling, coaching and checking clear standards
C. avoiding measurement
D. allowing each shift to invent its own rules
**Correct answer: B**

3. A “moment of truth” occurs when:
A. a supplier sends an invoice
B. payroll closes
C. an interaction meaningfully shapes the customer's perception
D. a stockroom light is switched off
**Correct answer: C**

4. The first priority in a threatening customer situation is:
A. completing the sale
B. protecting the queue target
C. winning the argument
D. safety and approved escalation
**Correct answer: D**

5. Needs discovery helps an employee:
A. understand the customer's intended outcome
B. avoid listening
C. sell only premium products
D. shorten every interaction regardless of need
**Correct answer: A**

6. Active listening includes:
A. preparing a rebuttal while the customer speaks
B. clarifying and confirming what has been heard
C. interrupting repeatedly
D. ignoring non-verbal cues
**Correct answer: B**

7. When a customer asks for an exception beyond an employee's authority, the correct action is to:
A. promise it immediately
B. reject the customer without explanation
C. escalate according to the approved process
D. alter the transaction record
**Correct answer: C**

8. Service recovery should aim to:
A. prove the customer wrong
B. avoid recording the incident
C. protect the employee from all feedback
D. resolve the issue fairly within rights, policy and authority
**Correct answer: D**

9. A useful service standard is one that employees can:
A. understand and demonstrate in practice
B. interpret differently every day
C. keep hidden from customers and supervisors
D. ignore during peak periods
**Correct answer: A**

10. Complaint records are valuable because they can:
A. replace all manager observation
B. reveal patterns requiring corrective action
C. guarantee legal compliance
D. remove the need for coaching
**Correct answer: B**

11. Which behaviour is most likely to escalate an angry customer?
A. Calm acknowledgement
B. Clear boundaries
C. Defensive argument and blame
D. Appropriate escalation
**Correct answer: C**

12. Omnichannel service means the store may need to support:
A. only walk-in purchases
B. only online customers
C. no returns
D. connected customer journeys across physical and digital channels
**Correct answer: D**

13. The manager creates service culture by:
A. demonstrating expected behaviour and holding teams accountable
B. leaving service entirely to junior staff
C. discussing service only after complaints
D. focusing only on sales value
**Correct answer: A**

14. A customer asks a question the employee cannot answer accurately. The employee should:
A. guess confidently
B. verify the information or seek authorised assistance
C. change the subject
D. tell the customer to search online
**Correct answer: B**

15. A service KPI is most useful when it:
A. measures something easy but irrelevant
B. replaces all judgement
C. helps management understand an important customer outcome or process
D. is never reviewed
**Correct answer: C**

16. When statutory consumer rights may apply to a return, a supervisor should:
A. rely on an old verbal rule
B. make a personal legal interpretation
C. automatically approve everything
D. use current approved guidance and escalate uncertainty
**Correct answer: D**

17. A strong opening service briefing should establish:
A. priorities, roles and escalation expectations
B. personal opinions about customers
C. only the day's sales target
D. no ownership
**Correct answer: A**

18. If queue time rises sharply, the manager should first:
A. wait until closing
B. assess demand, capacity and available approved responses
C. stop measuring queues
D. blame checkout staff publicly
**Correct answer: B**

19. Root-cause analysis is useful because it:
A. guarantees no future errors
B. identifies who to punish
C. looks beyond the symptom to why the problem occurred
D. replaces corrective action
**Correct answer: C**

20. An appropriate response to abusive language is to:
A. respond with similar language
B. continue regardless of safety
C. offer unlimited compensation
D. remain professional, set boundaries and escalate according to procedure
**Correct answer: D**

21. Which is the strongest example of ownership?
A. “I will confirm the next step and make sure the matter reaches the correct person.”
B. “That is not my department.”
C. “Try again tomorrow.”
D. “You must find the manager yourself.”
**Correct answer: A**

22. Customer feedback should be:
A. ignored if sales are high
B. analysed alongside operational evidence
C. used only to discipline employees
D. treated as statistically perfect in every case
**Correct answer: B**

23. Service coaching is most effective when feedback is:
A. vague and delayed
B. public and embarrassing
C. specific, evidence-based and followed by practice or action
D. unrelated to standards
**Correct answer: C**

24. A manager should promise a remedy only when:
A. the customer demands it loudly
B. the product is cheap
C. no record will be created
D. the remedy is supported by authority, policy and applicable rights
**Correct answer: D**

25. Product availability affects customer experience because:
A. customers cannot buy an unavailable item at that location/channel at that moment
B. it affects employees only
C. it has no relationship to service
D. customers never notice gaps
**Correct answer: A**

26. If staff repeatedly give different answers about the same return scenario, the manager should:
A. accept the inconsistency
B. clarify approved guidance and coach the team
C. stop accepting returns
D. remove all discretion without reviewing policy
**Correct answer: B**

27. A customer journey map helps managers:
A. calculate payroll
B. count warehouse pallets only
C. identify important interactions and friction points across the journey
D. replace service standards
**Correct answer: C**

28. During a system outage affecting online collections, staff should:
A. invent collection confirmations
B. hide the outage
C. release goods without required controls
D. follow continuity procedures and communicate verified information
**Correct answer: D**

29. Good service communication should be:
A. clear, respectful and relevant
B. filled with unexplained jargon
C. intentionally vague
D. based on assumptions
**Correct answer: A**

30. If a complaint reveals a process failure affecting many customers, management should:
A. close the complaint after one refund
B. correct the wider process as well as the individual case
C. delete similar complaints
D. wait for more customers to complain before investigating
**Correct answer: B**

31. Which is a useful sign of service inconsistency?
A. All teams follow the same verified procedure
B. Customer information is accurate
C. Outcomes vary widely because employees interpret the same standard differently
D. Managers coach regularly
**Correct answer: C**

32. A manager observing a service interaction should focus on:
A. personal friendship with the employee
B. unrelated tasks
C. whether the customer spends enough
D. observable behaviour against the expected standard
**Correct answer: D**

33. Service recovery begins with:
A. understanding the problem accurately
B. calculating staff commission
C. assuming the customer caused it
D. offering the maximum remedy
**Correct answer: A**

34. Which action supports accessibility and respectful service?
A. Assuming every customer needs the same assistance
B. Listening to the customer's stated needs and using approved accommodations/processes
C. refusing reasonable communication support
D. speaking only to a companion rather than the customer
**Correct answer: B**

35. A recurring queue problem at the same time every Saturday suggests the manager should:
A. treat each Saturday as unrelated
B. stop collecting data
C. review traffic patterns, scheduling and checkout capacity
D. remove the queue target
**Correct answer: C**

36. When a customer posts a complaint online, store employees should:
A. argue from personal accounts
B. disclose transaction details publicly
C. ignore company social-media rules
D. follow authorised digital-response and escalation procedures
**Correct answer: D**

37. The purpose of measuring service is to:
A. support learning, control and improvement
B. generate numbers without action
C. replace leadership
D. eliminate customer conversations
**Correct answer: A**

38. An employee who does not know a service standard needs:
A. immediate dismissal automatically
B. clarification, training or coaching appropriate to the circumstances
C. no feedback
D. a lower customer target only
**Correct answer: B**

39. If a customer's request conflicts with safety controls, the manager should:
A. ignore safety to preserve the sale
B. let the customer decide the procedure
C. protect safety while explaining and offering authorised alternatives where possible
D. alter the safety record
**Correct answer: C**

40. The best indicator of an effective complaint process is:
A. zero recorded complaints because staff delete them
B. every complaint receiving the same remedy
C. no escalation routes
D. issues are handled consistently, fairly, recorded where required and used for improvement
**Correct answer: D**

41. A manager notices staff greet customers warmly but frequently provide inaccurate product information. The priority is to:
A. improve knowledge accuracy while preserving positive engagement
B. stop greetings
C. ignore accuracy because customers seem happy
D. remove all product information
**Correct answer: A**

42. Why should managers distinguish a service symptom from a root cause?
A. To make reports longer
B. So corrective action addresses what is driving the problem
C. To avoid accountability
D. To remove employee involvement
**Correct answer: B**

43. Which response best demonstrates empathy without admitting unsupported liability?
A. “It is definitely our legal fault.”
B. “You are overreacting.”
C. “I can understand why that experience was frustrating; let me establish what happened and what we can do.”
D. “There is nothing I can do.”
**Correct answer: C**

44. Customer information obtained during a complaint should:
A. be shared with the whole team for interest
B. be posted in a group chat
C. be used for unrelated marketing automatically
D. be handled according to approved information-protection processes
**Correct answer: D**

45. A service-improvement action needs an owner because:
A. responsibility for execution and follow-up must be clear
B. ownership removes the need for deadlines
C. only managers may act
D. it prevents measurement
**Correct answer: A**

46. A strong manager response to a service failure by a new employee is to:
A. assume deliberate misconduct
B. determine what happened, clarify the standard, coach and apply appropriate process
C. ignore the failure
D. discuss it with customers publicly
**Correct answer: B**

47. A customer says the website promised store collection today, but the order is not ready. The store should:
A. blame the website
B. hand over another customer's order
C. verify order status, explain accurately and follow the approved fulfilment/escalation process
D. mark it collected
**Correct answer: C**

48. Which approach best protects service during peak trading?
A. Waiting until queues become severe
B. Scheduling every employee identically
C. Suspending all coaching indefinitely
D. anticipating pressure points and preparing flexible, trained capacity
**Correct answer: D**

49. The strongest evidence of a service culture is:
A. consistent customer-focused behaviour even when managers are not standing nearby
B. posters in the staff room only
C. one annual training session
D. a slogan on receipts
**Correct answer: A**

50. If a manager changes a local service process, the change should:
A. contradict company policy if convenient
B. remain within authority and approved standards
C. be hidden from the team
D. remove necessary controls
**Correct answer: B**

51. An improvement review should ask:
A. who can be blamed
B. whether the report looks impressive
C. whether the action was implemented and whether the intended result improved
D. whether measurement can now stop permanently
**Correct answer: C**

52. Sustainable service leadership requires managers to:
A. personally handle every customer
B. focus only on complaints
C. rely on goodwill rather than standards
D. align standards, people, processes, evidence and continuous improvement
**Correct answer: D**
