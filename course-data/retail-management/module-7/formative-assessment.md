# Module 7 Formative Assessment — POS, Digital Retail & Management Information

**Question bank:** 32 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 15 questions per attempt  
**Pass mark:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3

1. The main purpose of a POS system in retail is to:
A. Record and control sales transactions accurately
B. Replace all management decisions
C. Eliminate the need for stock controls
D. Guarantee customer satisfaction
**Correct answer: A**

2. A till variance should first be treated as:
A. Automatic proof of theft
B. An exception requiring verification and investigation
C. A marketing problem
D. A supplier error
**Correct answer: B**

3. A refund override should normally require:
A. No supporting evidence
B. The cashier's private approval
C. Appropriate authorisation under store procedure
D. Removal from the POS record
**Correct answer: C**

4. A management dashboard is most useful when it:
A. Contains as much data as possible
B. Replaces store walks
C. Shows only positive results
D. Highlights relevant measures, trends and exceptions
**Correct answer: D**

5. A void recorded after an item was entered at the till is best understood as:
A. A transaction exception that should remain traceable
B. A stock delivery
C. A customer loyalty reward
D. A merchandising standard
**Correct answer: A**

6. When several unusual refunds occur under one operator, the manager should:
A. Ignore them if daily sales are high
B. Review the pattern and supporting evidence
C. Immediately accuse the operator publicly
D. Delete the transactions
**Correct answer: B**

7. A useful sales report can help a manager identify:
A. Employee medical diagnoses
B. Private customer passwords
C. Trading patterns and performance exceptions
D. Guaranteed future sales
**Correct answer: C**

8. The strongest management response to a dashboard exception is to:
A. Assume the system is wrong
B. Change the target
C. Wait until month end regardless of severity
D. Verify the data, investigate causes and act appropriately
**Correct answer: D**

9. POS access should be:
A. Assigned according to authorised roles and responsibilities
B. Shared freely during busy periods
C. Written on the till for convenience
D. Identical for every employee
**Correct answer: A**

10. Conversion rate generally helps a retailer understand:
A. How many suppliers delivered
B. How effectively customer traffic becomes transactions
C. How many tills are installed
D. The number of staff contracts
**Correct answer: B**

11. When sales are strong but stock availability is falling, the manager should examine:
A. Only the staff uniform
B. Only the store music
C. Replenishment and stock-flow information
D. Only last year's budget
**Correct answer: C**

12. Omnichannel retail aims to:
A. Keep every sales channel completely separate
B. Remove physical stores
C. Prevent customers changing channels
D. Create a coordinated customer experience across channels
**Correct answer: D**

13. A manager reviewing a high number of price overrides should:
A. Investigate causes such as ticketing, promotion or control failures
B. Assume every override is fraudulent
C. Stop selling the affected category permanently
D. Delete the report
**Correct answer: A**

14. Good retail data should be:
A. Secret from every manager
B. Accurate, relevant and timely enough for the decision
C. Based mainly on rumours
D. Changed to match expectations
**Correct answer: B**

15. Click-and-collect requires coordination between:
A. Payroll and recruitment only
B. Security and maintenance only
C. Digital ordering, stock, fulfilment and customer handover
D. Advertising and uniforms only
**Correct answer: C**

16. Customer information should be accessed:
A. Whenever an employee is curious
B. Through shared personal accounts
C. Without business purpose
D. Only for authorised, legitimate work purposes
**Correct answer: D**

17. If the POS network fails during trading, the manager should first:
A. Follow approved continuity and escalation procedures
B. Invent an unofficial payment process
C. Ask customers for passwords
D. Ignore the outage
**Correct answer: A**

18. A data-led manager combines reports with:
A. Guesswork only
B. Operational observation and verification
C. Personal preference only
D. Unverified social media comments
**Correct answer: B**

19. A transaction log is valuable because it:
A. Prevents all errors
B. Replaces authorisation controls
C. Provides traceable evidence of transaction activity
D. Removes the need for supervision
**Correct answer: C**

20. A technology incident should be escalated according to:
A. The customer's preferred process
B. An employee's personal opinion
C. Competitor procedures
D. The organisation's incident and continuity requirements
**Correct answer: D**

21. A sudden fall in unit sales for a normally strong item should prompt the manager to:
A. Check availability, pricing, display and relevant data
B. Immediately remove the category
C. Ignore stock records
D. Change the employee roster only
**Correct answer: A**

22. The best reason to restrict manager overrides is to:
A. Slow down customer service
B. Maintain accountability over higher-risk exceptions
C. Prevent managers working
D. Eliminate all refunds
**Correct answer: B**

23. A dashboard KPI shown in red should normally be treated as:
A. Proof of misconduct
B. A guaranteed financial loss
C. A signal requiring interpretation and possible action
D. A reason to hide the dashboard
**Correct answer: C**

24. When customer data is accidentally sent to the wrong recipient, staff should:
A. Delete local evidence and say nothing
B. Post about it publicly
C. Contact the customer from a private account
D. Follow the approved security-compromise reporting and escalation process
**Correct answer: D**

25. Sales data by hour can support:
A. Better staffing and service-capacity decisions
B. Employee disciplinary outcomes by itself
C. Elimination of all store walks
D. Automatic supplier selection
**Correct answer: A**

26. An effective digital fulfilment control is to:
A. Mark orders complete before picking
B. Verify the correct order, items and customer handover
C. Allow substitutions without approved rules
D. Ignore stock discrepancies
**Correct answer: B**

27. A manager comparing sales and stock data is trying to understand:
A. Only payroll costs
B. Only customer complaints
C. How inventory availability relates to trading performance
D. Only supplier addresses
**Correct answer: C**

28. During a system outage, manual workarounds should be used only when they are:
A. Faster than all controls
B. Suggested by any customer
C. Unrecorded
D. Approved and controlled by organisational procedure
**Correct answer: D**

29. An exception report is designed primarily to:
A. Focus attention on activity outside expected parameters
B. Replace all detailed reports
C. Praise every employee
D. Record staff leave
**Correct answer: A**

30. When analysing retail information, correlation between two measures:
A. Always proves one caused the other
B. May justify investigation but does not automatically prove cause
C. Should be ignored
D. Guarantees a corrective action
**Correct answer: B**

31. A useful management action plan should link an identified issue to:
A. A slogan only
B. An unverified assumption
C. Action, ownership, timing and follow-up
D. A permanent target reduction
**Correct answer: C**

32. The strongest use of retail technology is to:
A. Replace human accountability
B. Collect data without purpose
C. Avoid customer interaction
D. strengthen accurate transactions, operational control and informed decisions
**Correct answer: D**
