# Module 7 Summative Assessment Bank — POS, Digital Retail & Management Information

**Question bank:** 52 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 25 questions per attempt  
**Pass mark:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3

1. Which control best supports individual POS accountability?
A. Unique authorised user access
B. Shared passwords
C. Unrecorded overrides
D. Open manager credentials
**Correct answer: A**

2. A cashier reports a R150 till shortage. The manager should first:
A. Deduct it immediately from wages
B. Verify the reconciliation and investigate according to procedure
C. Accuse the cashier of theft
D. Delete the variance
**Correct answer: B**

3. Refund, void and override reports are particularly useful for:
A. Choosing store music
B. Setting annual leave
C. Monitoring transaction exceptions and control risk
D. Calculating store floor area
**Correct answer: C**

4. A dashboard should help a store manager move from information to:
A. More paperwork only
B. Automatic blame
C. Fewer controls
D. Prioritised management action
**Correct answer: D**

5. The strongest control over a high-risk POS override is:
A. Appropriate authority plus a traceable record
B. Verbal permission from any colleague
C. Shared credentials
D. No supporting reason
**Correct answer: A**

6. A large increase in voids after a new promotion begins may indicate:
A. Guaranteed fraud
B. A process, pricing or training issue requiring investigation
C. A payroll problem
D. Successful stock receiving
**Correct answer: B**

7. Sales by category and time period can help identify:
A. Private staff matters
B. Legal guilt
C. Trading patterns and areas needing attention
D. Exact future demand
**Correct answer: C**

8. Before acting on an unusual KPI, a manager should:
A. Publicly identify a responsible employee
B. Change the KPI
C. Assume the report is complete
D. Validate context and investigate likely drivers
**Correct answer: D**

9. Why should employees not share POS credentials?
A. It weakens traceability and accountability
B. It makes tills run faster
C. It improves audit evidence
D. It increases authorisation control
**Correct answer: A**

10. A store receives 1,000 visitors and records 250 transactions. Its conversion rate is:
A. 10%
B. 25%
C. 40%
D. 75%
**Correct answer: B**

11. If a dashboard shows high customer traffic but low conversion, management should examine:
A. Only supplier delivery times
B. Only payroll tax
C. availability, service, queues and customer-experience factors
D. Only store rent
**Correct answer: C**

12. Omnichannel control is strongest when:
A. Online orders are invisible to stores
B. Channels use conflicting policies
C. Customers must restart every interaction
D. channels coordinate stock, fulfilment, service and information
**Correct answer: D**

13. A high rate of manual price overrides should trigger:
A. Review of price files, tickets, promotions, training and authorisation
B. Automatic dismissal of the cashier
C. Deletion of override records
D. Closure of the department
**Correct answer: A**

14. Management information is most useful when it is:
A. Extensive regardless of relevance
B. sufficiently accurate, relevant and timely for the decision
C. based on memory
D. limited to sales totals
**Correct answer: B**

15. A click-and-collect order should normally be handed over after:
A. The customer enters the stockroom
B. Payment is ignored
C. approved order/customer verification requirements are satisfied
D. The order is removed from the system
**Correct answer: C**

16. Which principle best protects customer information?
A. Everyone should access everything
B. Information should be copied to personal devices
C. Passwords should be shared during peak periods
D. Access should be limited to authorised business need
**Correct answer: D**

17. When the POS becomes unavailable, the first management priority is to:
A. Activate approved incident, continuity and escalation procedures
B. Create an undocumented workaround
C. Allow uncontrolled cash sales
D. Disable reconciliation
**Correct answer: A**

18. A report shows sales falling 15% while customer traffic is unchanged. A sound next step is to:
A. Assume customers have less money
B. investigate conversion, availability, service and transaction measures
C. Reduce all targets
D. Ignore the report until year end
**Correct answer: B**

19. Transaction records support control because they provide:
A. Guaranteed prevention of fraud
B. Unlimited customer information
C. an evidence trail for review and reconciliation
D. Permission to bypass procedures
**Correct answer: C**

20. A manager discovers an employee wrote a shared system password on a noticeboard. The best response is to:
A. Leave it for convenience
B. Photograph and share it
C. Use the password personally
D. secure the credential and follow access/security procedures
**Correct answer: D**

21. Sales for a product fall sharply after a shelf move. The manager should:
A. Review availability, visibility, location and relevant sales evidence
B. Immediately blame the supplier
C. Delete historic sales
D. Stop measuring the product
**Correct answer: A**

22. Separation of duties around sensitive POS activity helps:
A. Increase shared access
B. reduce the risk of one person controlling an entire high-risk process
C. Eliminate supervision
D. Prevent legitimate refunds
**Correct answer: B**

23. A KPI target is best used as:
A. Proof that employees caused any miss
B. A replacement for observation
C. a reference point for assessing performance and investigating variance

24. If customer information may have been exposed, staff should:
A. Decide privately whether it matters
B. Contact the media

25. Hourly transaction data can help managers:
A. Align resources more closely with trading demand
B. Determine employee guilt
C. Remove all breaks

26. When fulfilling an online order from store stock, an important control is:

27. If stock records show 20 units but none are physically available, the manager should:

29. Which report most directly helps identify unusual manager-authorised till activity?

31. Data should lead to corrective action only after:
A. A target has been missed once
