# Module 2 Formative Assessment Bank
## Professional Communication

**Bank size:** 32 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 15 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Prerequisite:** Complete all eight Module 2 lessons.

## Lesson 1 — Communication Principles in the Workplace
1. Before choosing a communication channel, what should an administrator identify FIRST?  
A. The longest available message format  
B. Purpose, audience and required action  
C. The most senior person to copy  
D. Whether jargon can be added  
**Answer: B**

2. Which channel is MOST suitable when an approval needs a clear written record?  
A. Informal corridor conversation only  
B. Unrecorded personal call  
C. An authorised written channel such as business email or workflow system  
D. Public social media  
**Answer: C**

3. Which is a communication barrier?  
A. Confirming a date  
B. Summarising an instruction  
C. Using plain language  
D. Unexplained jargon the receiver does not understand  
**Answer: D**

4. Why might an urgent changed meeting require confirmation?  
A. The impact of a missed message is high  
B. All emails legally require confirmation  
C. Confirmation removes the need to update the calendar  
D. It allows incorrect details to remain  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 2 — Professional Telephone Skills
5. Why establish a caller's purpose before transferring?  
A. To make the call longer  
B. To route it appropriately and reduce unnecessary transfers  
C. To collect private information unrelated to the call  
D. To avoid taking messages  
**Answer: B**

6. Which telephone message is MOST complete?  
A. “Someone called.”  
B. “Supplier unhappy.”  
C. “Lebo from ABC Supplies, 021 xxx xxxx, called at 10:15 about invoice 784; requests update before 15:00.”  
D. “Please phone back sometime.”  
**Answer: C**

7. A caller asks why a colleague is absent. What should the administrator generally do?  
A. Explain private medical details  
B. Give the colleague's personal number  
C. Guess the reason  
D. Protect private information and provide only an authorised availability response  
**Answer: D**

8. What should happen if a hold becomes unexpectedly long?  
A. Return to the caller with an update and appropriate alternative  
B. Leave the caller indefinitely  
C. Disconnect without explanation  
D. Transfer randomly  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 3 — Business Email & Digital Communication
9. What is the BEST use of Cc?  
A. Copy everyone in the organisation  
B. Include someone who genuinely needs visibility but is not the primary actor  
C. Pressure a colleague by always copying executives  
D. Hide all recipients  
**Answer: B**

10. Before sending an attachment, what is MOST important?  
A. Rename it “final” without checking  
B. Send first and verify later  
C. Confirm the correct current file and authorised recipients  
D. Copy it to a personal email account  
**Answer: C**

11. When should Reply All be used?  
A. For every email  
B. Whenever a senior manager is copied  
C. To make the message appear important  
D. When all included recipients genuinely need the response  
**Answer: D**

12. Which request is MOST actionable?  
A. “Please send the signed attendance list by 14:00 today for the payroll submission.”  
B. “Send that thing soon.”  
C. “Can someone do something?”  
D. “Urgent!!!”  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 4 — Business Writing
13. What should determine the choice between memo, notice and other correspondence?  
A. Which format looks most complicated  
B. Purpose, audience and organisational requirements  
C. The administrator's favourite template  
D. Document length only  
**Answer: B**

14. A workplace notice should make which information clear?  
A. Only the author's job title  
B. Unrelated background history  
C. What is happening, who is affected, timing and required action  
D. Every internal discussion that led to the notice  
**Answer: C**

15. Why should proofreading include names, dates and amounts?  
A. Spelling is never important  
B. It makes documents longer  
C. Templates guarantee facts  
D. A grammatically correct document can still contain serious factual errors  
**Answer: D**

16. An approved letter needs a material factual change. What is the BEST action?  
A. Update it and return it for appropriate review/approval  
B. Change it secretly after signature  
C. Send both versions  
D. Ignore the error  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 5 — Listening, Questioning & Clarifying Information
17. Which is an open question?  
A. “Is the meeting at 10:00?”  
B. “What outcome do you need from the meeting?”  
C. “Should I invite Finance, yes or no?”  
D. “Is this invoice 417?”  
**Answer: B**

18. A manager says “send it to everyone.” What should the administrator do if the intended recipients are unclear?  
A. Send it to every contact available  
B. Ignore the instruction  
C. Clarify who “everyone” means before sending  
D. Post it publicly  
**Answer: C**

19. What is the purpose of paraphrasing a complex instruction?  
A. To make it sound more formal  
B. To avoid recording it  
C. To change the manager's decision  
D. To confirm shared understanding before action  
**Answer: D**

20. Before asking a manager a routine clarification, an efficient administrator should:  
A. first check relevant information already available  
B. ask the same question several times  
C. guess regardless of risk  
D. wait until the deadline  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 6 — Communicating with Managers, Colleagues & Clients
21. Which status update is MOST decision-ready?  
A. “The venue is a problem.”  
B. “Venue A is unavailable; Venue B costs R1,200 more; decision needed by 14:00 to hold it.”  
C. “Please see below.”  
D. “I don't know what to do.”  
**Answer: B**

22. How should an administrator coordinate colleagues who do not report to them?  
A. Pretend to be their manager  
B. Threaten them with the director's name  
C. Use clear requests, deadlines, follow-up and appropriate escalation  
D. Avoid all follow-up  
**Answer: C**

23. Why is promising an unconfirmed customer outcome poor service?  
A. Customers prefer uncertainty  
B. It saves too much time  
C. Managers never make decisions  
D. It creates an expectation the administrator may not be able to meet  
**Answer: D**

24. Need-to-know means:  
A. sharing information only with people who legitimately require it  
B. sharing all information with all employees  
C. keeping every work detail secret from everyone  
D. sending confidential information through personal accounts  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 7 — Handling Difficult Conversations
25. What should an administrator do before defending against a complaint?  
A. End the conversation immediately  
B. Listen and establish the main concern and facts  
C. Promise compensation  
D. Blame another department  
**Answer: B**

26. Which response acknowledges frustration without automatically accepting liability?  
A. “Everything is our fault.”  
B. “You are wrong.”  
C. “I understand that waiting without an update has been frustrating.”  
D. “Calm down.”  
**Answer: C**

27. When a requested remedy exceeds your authority, you should:  
A. approve it anyway  
B. invent a policy  
C. avoid recording the request  
D. explain the appropriate next step and escalate to the authorised role  
**Answer: D**

28. A caller becomes threatening. What is the BEST response?  
A. Follow workplace safety/escalation procedures and appropriate boundaries  
B. Threaten the caller back  
C. Disclose private staff information to calm them  
D. Continue regardless of safety  
**Answer: A**

## Lesson 8 — Communication Records, Follow-up & Escalation
29. Which communication is MOST likely to require a record?  
A. A casual greeting  
B. A manager's approval and associated deadline  
C. A joke between colleagues  
D. A discussion about lunch preferences  
**Answer: B**

30. A useful action item should normally identify:  
A. only the meeting name  
B. every person in the organisation  
C. the action, owner and due date  
D. no deadline  
**Answer: C**

31. Why should follow-up sometimes happen before the final deadline?  
A. To create unnecessary pressure  
B. To avoid task registers  
C. Because deadlines are optional  
D. To leave recovery time if the required input is missing or wrong  
**Answer: D**

32. What is the primary purpose of escalation?  
A. Obtain the authority, attention or support needed to protect the outcome  
B. Embarrass a colleague  
C. Forward long email chains without explanation  
D. Avoid taking responsibility for follow-up  
**Answer: A**
