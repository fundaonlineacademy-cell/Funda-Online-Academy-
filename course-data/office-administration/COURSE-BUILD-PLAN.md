## Lesson quality standard
Each lesson should normally include estimated study time, learning outcomes, why the lesson matters, key terms, structured teaching content, practical examples, an activity, knowledge check with feedback, key takeaways and useful visual guidance. Narrow lessons may be approximately 500–700 words; deeper lessons may reach 700–1,000+ words where justified.

## Assessment standard
Each module will receive:
- Formative bank: 32 MCQs, exactly four traceable to each of the eight lessons, with 15 delivered per attempt.
- Summative bank: 52 MCQs covering all eight lessons, with 25 delivered per attempt.
- 70% pass standard and maximum three attempts in the LMS progression model.
- One clearly best answer per question, plausible distractors and balanced answer positions.

## South African workplace context
Examples should use realistic South African office settings and rand where money examples are required. POPIA and other legal/compliance material should be scoped carefully, verified where necessary and taught as workplace awareness rather than individual legal advice.

## Deployment approach
The current database contains ten lesson records per module, including legacy generic lessons and placeholder records. The rebuilt repository will be treated as the approved source. Production records should only be reconciled after the module content and assessments are complete and validated, preserving learner data wherever required.
