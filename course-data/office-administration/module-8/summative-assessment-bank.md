# Module 8 Summative Assessment Bank
## Practical Admin-to-PA Project

**Bank size:** 52 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 25 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Coverage:** All eight Module 8 lessons and integrated capstone application.

1. What is the main purpose of a capstone project?  
A. Demonstrate integrated application of the course skills  
B. Repeat every lesson word for word  
C. Use real confidential company information  
D. Produce the largest possible number of files  
**Answer: A**

2. Why should the capstone use a fictional organisation?  
A. Because real offices have no workflows  
B. To allow realistic practice without exposing live organisational data  
C. To avoid applying administrative skills  
D. Because fictional data requires no controls  
**Answer: B**

3. What is the purpose of a stakeholder and authority matrix?  
A. Replace the organisation chart permanently  
B. List only senior employees  
C. Show who owns processes and which decisions require approval  
D. Remove escalation  
**Answer: C**

4. What is a project milestone?  
A. A visitor badge  
B. A password  
C. A final invoice  
D. A defined progress checkpoint  
**Answer: D**

5. What should an evidence index provide?  
A. Traceability between evidence items and demonstrated skills or outcomes  
B. A list of unrelated file names  
C. Only final grades  
D. A replacement for the portfolio  
**Answer: A**

6. Why is quality more important than evidence volume?  
A. Short portfolios always pass  
B. Assessment should show usable, accurate and controlled work rather than repetition  
C. Quantity is never considered  
D. Documents do not need purpose  
**Answer: B**

7. Why should the administrative control system use one authoritative task register?  
A. It eliminates follow-up  
B. It makes all tasks urgent  
C. It keeps work visible and reduces fragmented tracking  
D. It removes ownership  
**Answer: C**

8. Which task status gives the clearest operational information?  
A. “Pending”  
B. “Maybe”  
C. “Busy”  
D. “Waiting for Finance approval; follow up 14:00”  
**Answer: D**

9. What should determine an escalation trigger?  
A. Risk, authority, impact or defined process exception  
B. The learner's mood  
C. Message length  
D. Alphabetical order  
**Answer: A**

10. Why should follow-up dates be earlier than final deadlines where appropriate?  
A. To make deadlines irrelevant  
B. To leave recovery time for missing or incorrect inputs  
C. To reduce accountability  
D. To close tasks early  
**Answer: B**

11. What should a good opening control review?  
A. Only completed work  
B. Only stationery  
C. Systems, diary, expected activity and prior handover  
D. Social media  
**Answer: C**

12. What should a closing control achieve?  
A. Delete all open tasks  
B. Remove owners from actions  
C. Ignore tomorrow's commitments  
D. Reconcile outstanding items, secure information and prepare handover  
**Answer: D**

13. Why should communication evidence show follow-through?  
A. Professional communication should support action and closure  
B. Wording alone proves administration  
C. Follow-up is optional  
D. Communications never create commitments  
**Answer: A**

14. What should determine whether email, telephone or another channel is used?  
A. The longest message format  
B. Purpose, urgency, complexity, sensitivity and record requirement  
C. Personal preference only  
D. The sender's seniority only  
**Answer: B**

15. Why should To and Cc recipients be deliberately selected?  
A. To copy as many people as possible  
B. To pressure colleagues  
C. To control who needs action and who genuinely needs visibility  
D. To avoid confidentiality controls  
**Answer: C**

16. A vague instruction says, “Send this to everyone.” What should the learner demonstrate?  
A. Sending to every contact  
B. Posting publicly  
C. Ignoring the instruction  
D. Clarification of the intended recipients before sending  
**Answer: D**

17. Which is the BEST way to document a difficult internal deadline conversation?  
A. Focus on facts, impact, required action and timing  
B. Record insults  
C. Attack the colleague's personality  
D. Copy unrelated employees  
**Answer: A**

18. Why should communication commitments appear in the task register?  
A. To duplicate every email unnecessarily  
B. So promised actions and updates remain visible until closure  
C. To remove message records  
D. To avoid assigning owners  
**Answer: B**

19. What makes a filing structure sustainable?  
A. Personal employee names only  
B. Random folders  
C. Stable business functions and clear classification rules  
D. One folder called “Everything”  
**Answer: C**

20. What should a naming standard achieve?  
A. Make names as long as possible  
B. Remove dates and subjects  
C. Create several “final” copies  
D. Help users identify and retrieve files consistently  
**Answer: D**

21. What should version control distinguish?  
A. Draft, approved and superseded status  
B. File colour  
C. Printer used  
D. Employee seniority  
**Answer: A**

22. Why should access levels be part of the records portfolio?  
A. Every record should be public  
B. Different records require different authorised users  
C. Access never affects confidentiality  
D. Only IT decides whether records exist  
**Answer: B**

23. Why should legal retention periods not be invented in the project?  
A. Retention is never relevant  
B. All records are kept forever  
C. Actual requirements depend on applicable law, record type and organisational policy  
D. Every record has the same universal period  
**Answer: C**

24. A supplier register and source invoices differ. What should the learner do?  
A. Change whichever figure is easier  
B. Delete source evidence  
C. Ignore the difference  
D. Investigate the discrepancy and preserve traceability  
**Answer: D**

25. What should a front-desk simulation demonstrate?  
A. Service, access control, records and follow-through operating together  
B. Greeting skills only  
C. Security only  
D. Telephone answering only  
**Answer: A**

26. An unexpected visitor says they know the director personally. What is the correct simulated action?  
A. Grant unrestricted access  
B. Verify through the authorised process before access  
C. Reveal the director's location  
D. Ask another visitor to approve them  
**Answer: B**

27. Why should a confidential delivery be separately controlled?  
A. Confidential deliveries should remain on the counter  
B. Couriers own the record permanently  
C. Custody and authorised handoff need to be traceable  
D. Delivery logs are unnecessary  
**Answer: C**

28. What should the complaint simulation include?  
A. Only the customer's complaint text  
B. Automatic refund  
C. No status  
D. Acknowledgement, facts, owner, update commitment, action and closure/escalation  
**Answer: D**

29. Why should the visitor badge count be reconciled?  
A. Missing passes may indicate an unresolved access-control issue  
B. Badges are decorative only  
C. Visitors should keep them  
D. It replaces visitor sign-out  
**Answer: A**

30. What should a front-desk handover prioritise?  
A. Every greeting from the day  
B. Unresolved items with status, owner and next action  
C. Private gossip  
D. Completed tasks only  
**Answer: B**

31. Why should an executive weekly diary contain preparation blocks?  
A. To make the calendar look busy  
B. To remove travel time  
C. High-value meetings and decisions require time before the event  
D. To avoid meetings  
**Answer: C**

32. What should a diary conflict record show?  
A. Only the conflict time  
B. Only the executive's preference  
C. No alternatives  
D. Competing commitments, impact, options, authority and final decision  
**Answer: D**

33. What makes a meeting agenda decision-ready?  
A. Clear purpose, item wording, owner and expected action  
B. Vague headings only  
C. No timing  
D. Every possible topic  
**Answer: A**

34. Why should meeting actions be transferred into a register?  
A. To replace meeting minutes entirely  
B. To track owner, deadline, dependency and closure  
C. To remove accountability  
D. To make tasks anonymous  
**Answer: B**

35. What should the travel itinerary protect?  
A. The cheapest fare only  
B. Loyalty benefits only  
C. The underlying business commitment and realistic timing  
D. Hotel preference only  
**Answer: C**

36. A flight cancellation occurs. What is the FIRST useful step?  
A. Buy any replacement without approval  
B. Cancel the client meeting immediately  
C. Ignore the change until the traveller calls  
D. Confirm facts, impact and available alternatives  
**Answer: D**

37. Why should travel changes be reflected in the executive diary?  
A. Schedule, transfers and meetings depend on the revised travel timing  
B. The diary is unrelated to travel  
C. Calendar updates are optional  
D. Only Finance needs the new details  
**Answer: A**

38. What should a travel change record contain?  
A. Only the new flight number  
B. Original arrangement, revised plan, reason, cost impact and approval  
C. Only the traveller's name  
D. No financial information  
**Answer: B**

39. What should inbox triage demonstrate at PA level?  
A. Processing only by arrival time  
B. Forwarding everything to the executive  
C. Classification by business impact, required action, deadline and authority  
D. Deleting routine messages  
**Answer: C**

40. Why should an unusual request to change payment details be treated differently?  
A. Payment changes are always valid  
B. Finance does not need records  
C. Urgent requests bypass controls  
D. It may be a fraud/security risk requiring independent verification  
**Answer: D**

41. What should determine gatekeeping decisions?  
A. Purpose, urgency, impact, standing instructions and executive priorities  
B. Stakeholder loudness  
C. Personal friendship  
D. Whether the caller knows the executive's first name  
**Answer: A**

42. What should an executive briefing contain?  
A. All emails without summary  
B. Issue, key facts, options, risks, deadline and decision required  
C. Personal opinion only  
D. No evidence  
**Answer: B**

43. What is evidence of anticipation in the PA simulation?  
A. Waiting until failure  
B. Guessing what might happen with no basis  
C. A foreseeable risk identified early with preventive action  
D. Acting outside authority  
**Answer: C**

44. Why should a PA refuse to disclose private diary details to a demanding stakeholder?  
A. PAs should never communicate with stakeholders  
B. Diary information is always false  
C. Senior stakeholders have no legitimate needs  
D. Confidentiality and need-to-know still apply under pressure  
**Answer: D**

45. What is the purpose of the final portfolio audit?  
A. Confirm completeness, consistency, authority, confidentiality and traceability  
B. Add random documents  
C. Remove all corrections  
D. Increase file count  
**Answer: A**

46. What is cross-document consistency?  
A. Every document has identical formatting  
B. Related records agree on key facts, dates, statuses and outcomes  
C. Every file is the same size  
D. All documents use the same title  
**Answer: B**

47. Why should authority decisions be audited?  
A. To remove managers from the scenario  
B. To approve all spending retrospectively  
C. To prove the learner acted within simulated authority and escalated correctly  
D. To eliminate escalation  
**Answer: C**

48. What should a confidentiality audit confirm?  
A. Every file is public  
B. Passwords are included in the portfolio  
C. Real customer data was used  
D. Fictional data, controlled access and no unnecessary disclosure  
**Answer: D**

49. How should unresolved actions appear in final handover?  
A. With owner, status, next action and deadline  
B. Hidden  
C. Marked completed  
D. Deleted  
**Answer: A**

50. What makes a continuous-improvement action useful?  
A. It only states “do better”  
B. It identifies a problem, cause, control, owner and success measure  
C. It has no owner  
D. It removes evidence  
**Answer: B**

51. What BEST demonstrates progression from Administrator to PA?  
A. More typing  
B. More meetings  
C. Stronger anticipation, prioritisation, briefing, authority judgement and follow-through  
D. More confidential information known  
**Answer: C**

52. What should a final professional portfolio allow another competent administrator or PA to do?  
A. Guess what happened  
B. Recreate all documents from scratch  
C. Contact the learner for every detail  
D. Understand priorities, locate evidence and continue outstanding work without relying on the learner's memory  
**Answer: D**
