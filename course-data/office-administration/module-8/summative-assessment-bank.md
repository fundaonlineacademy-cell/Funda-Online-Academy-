# Module 8 Summative Assessment Bank
## Practical Admin-to-PA Project

**Bank size:** 52 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 25 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Coverage:** All eight Module 8 lessons and integrated capstone application.

1. What is the main purpose of a capstone project?  
A. Demonstrate integrated application of the course skills  
B. Repeat every lesson word for word  
C. Use real confidential company information  
D. Produce the largest possible number of files  
**Answer: A**

2. Why should the capstone use a fictional organisation?  
A. To allow realistic practice without exposing live organisational data  
B. Because real offices have no workflows  
C. To avoid applying administrative skills  
D. Because fictional data requires no controls  
**Answer: A**

3. What is the purpose of a stakeholder and authority matrix?  
A. Show who owns processes and which decisions require approval  
B. Replace the organisation chart permanently  
C. List only senior employees  
D. Remove escalation  
**Answer: A**

4. What is a project milestone?  
A. A defined progress checkpoint  
B. A visitor badge  
C. A password  
D. A final invoice  
**Answer: A**

5. What should an evidence index provide?  
A. Traceability between evidence items and demonstrated skills or outcomes  
B. A list of unrelated file names  
C. Only final grades  
D. A replacement for the portfolio  
**Answer: A**

6. Why is quality more important than evidence volume?  
A. Assessment should show usable, accurate and controlled work rather than repetition  
B. Short portfolios always pass  
C. Quantity is never considered  
D. Documents do not need purpose  
**Answer: A**

7. Why should the administrative control system use one authoritative task register?  
A. It keeps work visible and reduces fragmented tracking  
B. It eliminates follow-up  
C. It makes all tasks urgent  
D. It removes ownership  
**Answer: A**

8. Which task status gives the clearest operational information?

C. “Maybe”

9. What should determine an escalation trigger?

C. Message length

10. Why should follow-up dates be earlier than final deadlines where appropriate?

C. To reduce accountability

13. Why should communication evidence show follow-through?
