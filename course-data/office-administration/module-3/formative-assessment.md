# Module 3 Formative Assessment Bank
## Documents, Filing & Records

**Bank size:** 32 MCQs  
**Delivery:** 15 random questions per attempt  
**Pass standard:** 70%  
**Maximum attempts:** 3  
**Prerequisite:** Complete all eight Module 3 lessons.

## Lesson 1 — Business Documents & Document Standards

1. What should come BEFORE detailed formatting of a business document?  
A. Adding decorative graphics  
B. Confirming its purpose, audience and required content  
C. Choosing the largest font  
D. Exporting it immediately  
**Answer: B**

2. Which statement BEST describes a controlled document?  
A. Any file saved on a computer  
B. A document printed in colour  
C. A document whose approved version and changes are managed  
D. A document with more than one page  
**Answer: C**

3. Why must templates still be checked before use?  
A. They may contain outdated names, dates, placeholders or instructions  
B. Templates cannot contain formatting  
C. Templates always remove approval requirements  
D. They cannot be saved electronically  
**Answer: A**

4. A document looks professional but contains the wrong customer account number. What is the main problem?  
A. The logo is too small  
B. The paragraph spacing is inconsistent  
C. The document is too short  
D. Content accuracy has failed  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 2 — Creating and Formatting Professional Documents

5. What is the MAIN benefit of using heading styles?  
A. They make every heading a different colour  
B. They support consistent hierarchy and efficient formatting  
C. They remove the need for proofreading  
D. They prevent all page breaks  
**Answer: B**

6. When is a numbered list MOST appropriate?  
A. For unrelated ideas with no order  
B. For decorative emphasis only  
C. When sequence or referenced steps matter  
D. When the document has no structure  
**Answer: C**

7. Why should a document be checked after conversion to PDF or before printing?  
A. Layout can change in the final output  
B. PDF automatically corrects factual errors  
C. Printing removes version requirements  
D. Final output never needs approval  
**Answer: A**

8. Which formatting practice is LEAST professional?  
A. Consistent heading levels  
B. Appropriate white space  
C. Readable tables  
D. Random use of multiple fonts and sizes  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 3 — Filing Systems & Classification

9. What is the purpose of classification in filing?  
A. To make records difficult to locate  
B. To group records predictably for storage and retrieval  
C. To remove access controls  
D. To avoid indexing  
**Answer: B**

10. Which filing method is MOST suitable where each customer has a unique account number?  
A. Numeric filing  
B. Random filing  
C. Colour-only filing  
D. Informal desk filing  
**Answer: A**

11. What is a cross-reference?  
A. An instruction to destroy a record  
B. A password for a filing room  
C. A pointer directing a user to a related or actual record location  
D. A duplicate original stored everywhere  
**Answer: C**

12. Why should removed physical files sometimes be tracked?  
A. So nobody can use them again  
B. To increase paperwork only  
C. To avoid returning them  
D. So authorised users know where the file is and it can be returned  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 4 — Electronic Records & Folder Management

13. Which folder structure is generally MORE sustainable?  
A. `Operations > Suppliers > 2026 > Contracts`  
B. `My Stuff > Random > New`  
C. `Desktop > Things`  
D. `Personal Email Archive`  
**Answer: A**

14. What is a major risk of uncontrolled duplicate files?  
A. They always use too little storage  
B. Users may work from different versions and lose confidence in the authoritative copy  
C. They prevent documents from being opened  
D. They automatically improve collaboration  
**Answer: B**

15. What should determine access permissions to a shared folder?  
A. Whoever asks first  
B. The age of the employee  
C. Legitimate role and business need under organisational rules  
D. Whether the folder name is short  
**Answer: C**

16. Why should important work not exist only on one employee's desktop?  
A. Desktop files cannot be edited  
B. Desktops cannot contain folders  
C. It makes filenames longer  
D. It creates continuity, access and backup risks  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 5 — Document Version Control & Naming Conventions

17. Version control should primarily answer which question?  
A. Which file has the most pages?  
B. Which copy is current and authorised for the intended use?  
C. Which employee created the oldest draft?  
D. Which filename is shortest?  
**Answer: B**

18. What does “superseded” mean?  
A. Replaced by a newer authorised version  
B. Deleted accidentally  
C. Never reviewed  
D. Printed twice  
**Answer: A**

19. Why is `final_FINAL_reallyfinal2.docx` a weak filename?  
A. It is too professional  
B. It contains no letters  
C. It does not reliably communicate controlled version status  
D. It proves the file is approved  
**Answer: C**

20. Does the newest edit automatically equal the approved version?  
A. Yes, always  
B. Yes, if the file is on a shared drive  
C. Yes, if the editor is senior  
D. No, approval status must be confirmed separately  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 6 — Records Retention, Retrieval & Disposal

21. What is a retention schedule?  
A. A calendar of staff leave  
B. An organisational schedule defining how long record categories are kept and how they are disposed of  
C. A list of passwords  
D. A meeting timetable  
**Answer: B**

22. What is an inactive record?  
A. A record no longer needed frequently for daily work but still retained  
B. A record that may always be destroyed immediately  
C. Any record older than one month  
D. A document with no title  
**Answer: A**

23. What is a legal hold?  
A. A type of physical shelf  
B. A reminder to print records  
C. An instruction preventing disposal of relevant records while a formal matter is active  
D. A rule requiring every record to be kept forever  
**Answer: C**

24. Why may ordinary deletion be insufficient for sensitive electronic records?  
A. Electronic records never require disposal  
B. Deletion automatically creates approval  
C. Recycle bins are retention schedules  
D. Data may remain recoverable or outside the approved disposal process  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 7 — Data Accuracy, Quality Checks & Audit Trails

25. What is verification?  
A. Confirming information against a reliable or authoritative source  
B. Changing data until totals match  
C. Deleting duplicate evidence  
D. Guessing missing values  
**Answer: A**

26. What is reconciliation?  
A. Formatting a report  
B. Comparing related records and investigating differences  
C. Printing two copies  
D. Archiving every document  
**Answer: B**

27. Why is sharing user credentials harmful to an audit trail?  
A. It makes login faster  
B. It creates more storage  
C. Actions can no longer be reliably attributed to the real user  
D. It improves traceability  
**Answer: C**

28. What should happen when a material discrepancy cannot be explained?  
A. Change the figures so they agree  
B. Ignore it  
C. Delete the source record  
D. Pause affected action where appropriate and escalate for investigation  
**Answer: D**

## Lesson 8 — Records Management Practical Workflow

29. Why must records controls work together?  
A. A weakness in one stage can undermine the full lifecycle  
B. Only one control is ever necessary  
C. Classification makes approval unnecessary  
D. Retention rules replace access controls  
**Answer: A**

30. What is an authoritative record?  
A. Any personal working copy  
B. The recognised official or controlled record for the business purpose  
C. The oldest draft  
D. The copy with the largest file size  
**Answer: B**

31. Why must the correct version be sent for approval?  
A. Approval automatically fixes wrong versions  
B. The approver never needs to see content  
C. Otherwise there may be uncertainty about what was actually authorised  
D. Version control ends before approval  
**Answer: C**

32. Which is a sign that a records workflow needs improvement?  
A. Clear filenames  
B. Controlled access  
C. Reliable retrieval  
D. Duplicate folders and outdated active versions  
**Answer: D**
