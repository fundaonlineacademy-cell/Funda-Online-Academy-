Verify a Funda Academic Document

Employers and other authorised third parties can verify an officially issued Funda certificate or Statement of Results. Enter the document number exactly as it appears on the document.

For privacy and security, this service returns only the information required to confirm an officially issued academic document. It never exposes the learner's ID/passport number, private PDF, account, assessments, payments, identity documents or other Academy records.
