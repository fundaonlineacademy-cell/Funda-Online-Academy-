Legacy Student Programme

No Student Left Behind.

Funda Online Academy continues to improve its learning platform, course content, assessments and student support. The Legacy Student Programme is our structured pathway for eligible former students who may wish to return, complete previous learning or upgrade their learning experience under the Academy's current academic system.

Explore programme benefits Browse current courses

Our commitment to former students

Our growth should benefit the students who helped us grow.

Eligibility is not automatic. Previous study must be verified, and any applicable benefit is subject to the Academy's current programme terms, course availability and relevant academic requirements.

Programme benefits

Support for eligible returning students

The programme recognises that former students may return for different reasons. Depending on verified previous study and the applicable programme conditions, an eligible student may qualify for one of the following benefits.

Legacy Course Upgrade

For an eligible course previously completed with Funda, where the learner wishes to return to the improved version of that course.

70% OFF

Restart Your Previous Course

For an eligible former student who previously paid for a course but did not complete it and wishes to restart.

50% OFF

Returning Student Benefit

For an eligible former student returning to study a different current Funda Online Academy course.

25% OFF
Important: Previous-study benefits are subject to verification and Funda Online Academy's applicable terms and conditions. A displayed percentage does not confirm eligibility until the Academy has verified the learner's previous study record and the relevant programme conditions.
How the programme works

A clear verification process

The purpose of verification is to make sure the correct benefit is applied to the correct former student and course. It also protects the integrity of the Academy's academic and financial records.

1

Identify your previous study

Have the details of the course you previously studied with Funda available when making an enquiry.

2

Request verification

The Academy checks the relevant historical student and course information that is available in its records.

3

Confirm the applicable pathway

If you qualify, the Academy confirms which Legacy Student Programme benefit applies to your situation.

4

Continue through the current process

You then follow the Academy's current registration, enrolment, payment and approval process for the relevant course.

Programme clarity

What the programme does — and does not — mean

The Legacy Student Programme is a student-support initiative. It does not remove current course requirements or bypass the Academy's normal enrolment and academic controls.

Who is the programme intended for?

It is intended for eligible former Funda Online Academy students whose previous study can be verified and whose circumstances meet the applicable programme conditions.

Does every former student automatically receive a discount?

No. Eligibility must first be verified. The benefit depends on the student's previous course history and the programme conditions that apply to that case.

Does the programme guarantee course approval?

No. Current registration, enrolment, payment, approval and course requirements still apply. The programme determines an eligible legacy benefit; it does not replace the normal academic or administrative process.

Can I use the programme for a different course?

Eligible former students may qualify for the Returning Student Benefit when returning to study a different current course, subject to verification and the applicable terms.

What if my previous course has changed?

The Academy will consider the current course structure and the verified historical record when determining the appropriate legacy pathway. Course content, assessments and requirements may have changed as the Academy has improved its academic system.

How do I ask about my eligibility?

You can contact Funda Online Academy on WhatsApp and provide enough information for the team to identify your previous study record. Do not send sensitive banking information through WhatsApp.

Previously studied with Funda?

Contact the Academy if you would like your previous study record checked for possible Legacy Student Programme eligibility.
