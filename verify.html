OFFICIAL DOCUMENT CHECK

Verify a Funda academic document

Use the Academy's public verification service to confirm a Certificate or Statement of Results. Enter the document number exactly as it appears on the document.

Privacy by design: a public verification result shows only the information needed to confirm the document record and its current status. It does not display ID or passport numbers, payment information, assessment history, private files, account details or other learner records.

A successful result confirms that the document record exists in Funda Online Academy's verification register. If a document cannot be verified, check the number carefully or contact the Academy for further confirmation.
