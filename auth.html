Funda Online Academy Policies & Terms

Payment Rules

  • Courses below R2,000 must be paid in full before the course starts.
  • Courses above R2,000 require 50% payment upfront.
  • The remaining balance must be paid according to the agreed payment arrangement and before completion.
  • A payment plan may be arranged with the academy where applicable.

Student Responsibilities

  • Students must be committed to their chosen course.
  • Students must treat academy staff and other students with respect.
  • Students must follow academy rules and instructions.
  • Assessments must be submitted on time.
  • Assessments must not be submitted through WhatsApp unless specifically authorised by the academy.

Course Rules

  • Students must carefully select their course before enrolment.
  • Course changes are not permitted after the course has started unless approved by the academy.
  • Students who voluntarily drop out are not entitled to a refund.
  • Students must complete their assessments according to academy requirements.

Information & Privacy

  • Students must provide accurate information during registration.
  • Funda Online Academy will take reasonable measures to protect student information.
  • Identification information is restricted to authorised academy personnel where required for administration, verification and certification.
  • Students must keep their login passwords confidential.
