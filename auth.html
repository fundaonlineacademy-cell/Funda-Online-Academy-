Funda Online Academy Policies

Payment Rules

  • Courses below R2,000 must be paid in full.
  • Courses above R2,000 require a minimum 50% upfront payment.
  • The remaining balance must be paid according to the agreed payment arrangement and before course completion.
  • Where applicable, a payment plan may be arranged with the academy before the course starts.

Student Responsibilities

  • Students must be committed to completing their course.
  • Students must treat academy staff and other students with respect.
  • Students must follow all Funda Online Academy rules and instructions.
  • Students must submit assessments within the required deadlines.
  • Assessments must not be submitted through WhatsApp.
  • Students are responsible for maintaining access to their student account.

Course Rules

  • No course changes are permitted after the course has started.
  • Students who voluntarily drop out are not entitled to a refund.
  • Students must complete the required learning activities and assessments.
  • Course completion is subject to meeting the academy's assessment and completion requirements.

Privacy and Information Protection

Funda Online Academy respects the privacy of students. Personal information provided during registration and enrolment will be handled responsibly and protected against unauthorised access.

Identification information, including ID or passport information, should only be accessible to authorised academy personnel for legitimate administrative, enrolment, assessment and certificate purposes.

Assessments

  • Students must complete assessments according to the requirements of their course.
  • Students must submit their own work.
  • Academic dishonesty or submitting another person's work may result in disciplinary action.
  • Assessments must be submitted through the official academy system and not through WhatsApp.
