Funda Online Academy Policies

Please read the following payment rules, student responsibilities and terms before creating your account.

Payment Rules

  • Courses below R2,000 require payment of the full course fee.
  • Courses above R2,000 require 50% payment upfront and the remaining balance on completion.
  • Where applicable, a payment plan may be arranged with the academy before the course begins.
  • Students must follow the payment arrangement agreed with the academy.

Student Responsibilities

  • Students must remain committed to their studies.
  • Students must treat academy staff and other students with respect.
  • Students must follow the academy's rules and instructions.
  • Students must submit assessments within the required timeframes.
  • Assessments must not be submitted through WhatsApp unless specifically authorised by the academy.

Course Changes and Refunds

  • Course changes are not permitted after a student has started a course, unless approved by the academy.
  • Course fees are generally non-refundable where a student voluntarily drops out after starting the course.
  • Any exceptional refund request will be considered according to the academy's applicable refund policy.

Privacy

Funda Online Academy will take reasonable steps to protect student information and restrict access to authorised personnel. Personal information may be used for student administration, communication, assessments, records and certificates.

Required Student Information

Students may be required to provide accurate information including their name, surname, ID/passport number, course, phone number, highest education level, gender, email, address and how they heard about Funda Online Academy.
