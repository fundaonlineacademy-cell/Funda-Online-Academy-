🤝 EMPLOYER & INDUSTRY PARTNERSHIPS

Connect practical learning with real workplaces.

Funda Online Academy is building responsible relationships with employers and industry organisations that want to support workplace readiness, identify suitable talent when opportunities arise, and help shape the skills learners need.

How employers can participate

  • Consider suitable Funda learners or graduates when genuine opportunities exist.
  • Offer supervised workplace exposure where your organisation has capacity.
  • Tell us which skills your industry needs so training remains relevant.
  • Verify an Academy certificate using its certificate number.
No recruitment promise. Funda facilitates preparation and connections. Employers retain their own recruitment, selection, safety and workplace requirements.

PARTNERSHIP PROCESS

Simple, controlled and professional

1

Submit interest

Your organisation sends its contact details and indicates the type of partnership it is interested in.

2

Academy review

Funda reviews the enquiry, confirms appropriate details and contacts the organisation where suitable.

3

Agree the opportunity

Recruitment or workplace exposure arrangements are recorded clearly, including relevant requirements and limitations.

4

Connect eligible learners

Only suitable learners using authorised, consent-based processes are considered. Employers retain their own selection decisions.

EMPLOYER PARTNERSHIP ENQUIRY

Tell us how you would like to work with Funda.

Complete this form for recruitment, workplace exposure, industry skills feedback or a broader employer partnership.

Learner privacy comes first.
Submitting this form does not give an employer direct access to private student records. Learner information is shared only through authorised Academy processes and appropriate consent.
Partner recognition.
Where a formal relationship is approved, the Academy may recognise the organisation as an Employer & Industry Partner. This does not imply accreditation, endorsement or guaranteed recruitment.
EMPLOYER VERIFICATION

Verify an Academy certificate

Enter the certificate number exactly as it appears on the learner's Funda Online Academy certificate.

Verification confirms whether the certificate number matches an Academy record and displays limited completion information. It does not expose the learner's private account or contact details.

Our employment principle: We do not promise learners a job, and we do not pressure employers to hire. We prepare learners, create responsible connections and allow employers to make independent decisions based on their own requirements.
