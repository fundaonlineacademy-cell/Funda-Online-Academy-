Policies & Legal Information

These policies explain the main terms that apply when you create an account, enrol, submit payment, study with Funda Online Academy or use Academy services. They should be read together with any course-specific information shown before enrolment.

Student TermsPrivacy & POPIAPaymentsRefunds & CancellationsLearning & Certification

1. Student Terms & Conditions

By creating or using a Funda Online Academy student account, you agree to provide accurate information and to use your account only for your own learning activities.

  • Your login details are personal and must be kept secure.
  • Creating an account does not automatically grant access to paid course content.
  • Course access may depend on completed registration, payment verification, eligibility checks where applicable, and Academy approval.
  • Students are expected to follow Academy house rules, academic-integrity requirements and reasonable instructions relating to assessments, support and platform security.
  • Funda Online Academy short courses do not create a guarantee of employment, promotion, professional registration or admission to another institution.

2. Privacy & Personal Information

Funda Online Academy collects and uses personal information for legitimate Academy purposes such as account administration, enrolment, identity and eligibility verification, payment review, student support, assessment administration, certification, communications and legal or operational record keeping.

Information we may process

  • Identity and contact details, including ID or passport information where required.
  • Course, enrolment, payment and academic records.
  • Uploaded documents, including proof of payment, prior certificates and support-ticket attachments.
  • Support, communication and platform-use records necessary to operate the student service.

Personal information is handled in line with applicable South African privacy requirements, including POPIA where applicable. Access is limited according to operational need and system permissions. Students should not upload documents that are unrelated to the purpose for which they are requested.

Never send your password, OTP or banking PIN through a support ticket, email or WhatsApp message.

3. Payment Policy

Course fees displayed on the live course catalogue or enrolment screen are the applicable Academy prices at the time of the application, subject to any verified discount or approved special offer.

  • Students must use the payment details displayed by the official Academy enrolment process and should verify unusual payment requests before paying.
  • Proof of payment must relate to the student and enrolment for which it is submitted.
  • Submission of proof of payment does not by itself confirm approval or course access. Payments and enrolment information may be reviewed before access is activated.
  • Legacy, incomplete-legacy or returning-student discounts are subject to the eligibility and verification rules shown during registration.
  • If a payment cannot be matched or verified, the Academy may request additional information before approving the enrolment.

4. Refunds, Cancellations & Incorrect Payments

Refund requests are assessed according to the circumstances of the transaction, the student's enrolment status, any course access already provided, applicable Academy rules and applicable law. A request is not automatically approved merely because it has been submitted.

  • If an amount was paid incorrectly or duplicated, the student should contact Academy support promptly and provide the payment reference and proof of payment.
  • Where a legacy-upgrade application received a provisional discounted payment but is later declined because the claimed prior-study eligibility cannot be verified, the amount paid for that declined application may be processed for refund after the Academy completes its review.
  • Where a refund is approved, it should be returned through an appropriate verified payment route after the required checks are completed.
  • The Academy may request identity, banking or transaction verification before processing a refund to reduce fraud and payment errors.
  • Any rights that a student has under applicable consumer law remain unaffected by this policy.

Students should use the official dashboard support route for refund or payment-review queries so that the request can be recorded and investigated.

5. Learning, Assessments & Certification

Students must complete the applicable learning, assessment and administrative requirements before a course can be treated as successfully completed. Certificates are issued only after the relevant completion requirements and Academy checks have been satisfied.

  • Assessment, competency and attempt rules may differ by course and are shown in the relevant learning environment.
  • Academic dishonesty, misuse of another person's account or falsified documents may result in review, restriction or cancellation of access.
  • Where a certificate is available after successful completion, it will be made available through the Academy's approved certificate process.

6. Policy Updates & Support

The Academy may update these policies when its systems, services or legal obligations change. Material changes should be reflected by an updated policy version or effective date. For questions, use the official support options shown on the Funda Online Academy website or student dashboard.
