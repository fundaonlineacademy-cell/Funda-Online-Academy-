Funda Online Academy Influencer & Brand Ambassador Agreement

Term: 12 months, including a 90-day introductory period. Renewal is subject to Funda review.

Direct referrals: You earn 20% on qualifying revenue from your own directly attributed student referrals. There are no downlines, recruitment commissions or team overrides.

Qualifying revenue: Commission is based on verified money actually received by Funda. Discounts are calculated on the amount received. Instalments earn commission as each verified instalment is received. Refunds, reversals, chargebacks, fraud and manipulated or prohibited self-referrals do not qualify.

Ranks and bonuses: Lifetime qualifying revenue determines achievement rank. Achievement bonuses are total unlocked values, so previously paid rank bonuses are deducted when advancing.

Monthly performance payments: These begin at Gold and are earned only when the applicable new monthly qualifying-revenue target is achieved. Rank alone never guarantees a monthly payment.

Promotion: You must promote Funda truthfully and professionally, use clear advertising disclosures where required, and must not make unauthorised promises concerning accreditation, employment, admission, refunds, pricing or guaranteed outcomes.

Student money and information: Do not collect student payments personally unless Funda expressly authorises it in writing. Student personal information must not be misused or improperly shared.

Competition: During the agreement you may promote unrelated brands, but may not promote a directly competing online academy/course provider without Funda's prior written approval.

Termination: Either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice. Serious fraud, misconduct, referral manipulation, student-data misuse or material breach may result in immediate suspension or termination. Legitimate verified earnings already earned remain subject to normal reconciliation.

Tax and legal status: You are responsible for your own tax obligations except where Funda is legally required to report, deduct or withhold. The actual legal classification of the relationship remains subject to South African law.

Governing law: Republic of South Africa.

This electronic acceptance records your agreement to the programme terms. The full programme compensation schedule and current written programme rules form part of this agreement.
