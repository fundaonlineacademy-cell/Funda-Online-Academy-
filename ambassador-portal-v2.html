Privacy & Access

Your Ambassador account can only access your own Ambassador record, your own earnings, your own payouts and privacy-limited referral summaries.

Student emails, phone numbers, ID/passport numbers, addresses, payment proofs, results, certificates and private support records are not available to Ambassador accounts.

Programme Agreement & Rules

The current programme has three earning methods: 15% Direct Commission, one-time Achievement Bonuses and qualifying Monthly Performance Payments.

Direct referrals: 15% commission on verified qualifying revenue from your own directly attributed student referrals.

Achievement bonuses: one-time rewards when a new qualifying rank is reached for the first time.

Monthly Performance Payments: begin from Gold / Level 4, subject to the applicable monthly conditions and Academy verification. They are not guaranteed salaries.

Student privacy: Ambassador status never grants access to private student accounts or protected student information.

Responsible promotion: Ambassadors must use accurate Academy information and may not make unauthorised guarantees concerning accreditation, employment, admission, refunds, pricing or outcomes.

Qualifying revenue: refunds, reversals, chargebacks, fraud, manipulation and prohibited self-referrals do not qualify.
