Student Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to important questions about studying, enrollment, quality assurance and student support.

Do I need an account to browse courses?＋

No. You may browse available courses, fees and course information without an account. Registration is required only when you are ready to begin the enrollment process.

Where are your offices?＋

Funda Online Academy operates as an online learning provider. Our learning and student-support services are delivered remotely, allowing students to register, study, submit work and receive support online without needing to attend a physical campus.

Is Funda Online Academy a registered business?＋

Yes. Funda Online Academy operates as a registered business. Our formal business and quality-assurance information is maintained as part of our institutional records and certification processes.

What is CPD and why is it important?＋

CPD means Continuing Professional Development. It supports the ongoing development of knowledge, practical skills and professional competence. Funda Online Academy incorporates CPD-aligned quality principles within its training and certification processes where applicable.

Are all courses accredited qualifications?＋

Our programmes are primarily short, skills-focused courses. CPD recognition or accreditation and other quality arrangements may apply to particular training activities, but a short course should not automatically be understood as a nationally registered qualification. Students should review the information provided for the specific course they choose.

Is NSFAS funding available?＋

No. Funda Online Academy short courses are self-funded and are not funded through NSFAS. Students are responsible for paying the applicable course fee shown before enrollment.

How do you maintain learning quality?＋

Quality is treated as part of the learning process, not only certification. The Academy applies assessment review, internal verification, learner monitoring, academic-integrity expectations and quality-assurance procedures designed to support consistent and credible learning outcomes.

Are assessments checked and monitored?＋

Yes. Assessments are subject to the Academy's assessment and quality-control processes. Academic integrity and malpractice procedures also form part of our approach to protecting the credibility of student achievement.

When do I receive learning access?＋

Learning access opens after your enrollment information and proof of payment have been reviewed and your application has been approved by the relevant Academy team.

Can I track my application while it is pending?＋

Yes. Once you have submitted an application, you can return to your Student Dashboard to view your enrolled course and follow its approval status.

Will I receive a certificate?＋

Students who successfully meet the completion requirements of their course may receive the applicable Funda Online Academy certificate. Course-specific requirements and certification information should be reviewed before enrollment.

Can I study at my own pace?＋

Most Academy learning is designed to be flexible and online. Any course-specific deadlines, assessment dates or completion periods will be shown in the relevant course information and student learning area.

Do I need previous qualifications to enrol?＋

Entry requirements depend on the course. Many short courses are designed to be accessible, but students should always check the individual course requirements before submitting an application.

What support is available while I study?＋

Students can access Academy support for enrollment, account and learning-related queries. Additional learning resources and Library access will be available through the student environment as the relevant services are activated.

How can I ask a question that is not listed here?＋
