FAQs

Questions Before You Enrol?

Do I need an account to browse courses?

No. You can browse courses, view fees and read course information before creating an account.

When do I get access to learning?

After your enrollment and proof of payment have been reviewed and approved by the relevant Academy team.

Can I track a pending application?

Yes. Returning students can sign in and view the status of their enrollment from the Student Dashboard.
