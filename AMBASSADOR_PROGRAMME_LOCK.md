# Funda Online Academy Ambassador Programme — Locked Baseline

Locked after final audit and housekeeping on 2026-10-08.

## Approved operating model
- Direct-referral Ambassador / Creator Partner programme only.
- Everyone remains an **Ambassador**. No Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Executive or Elite ranks.
- No downlines, recruitment commissions, team overrides or network-building rewards.
- Fixed direct commission: **15%** of verified qualifying student payments attributed through the Ambassador referral link/code.
- User-facing heading: **Ambassador Performance & Rewards**.
- User-facing milestone terms: **Qualifying Revenue Target / Milestone**, **Achievement Incentive**, **Monthly Performance Reward**, **Maximum Total Cash**.
- No salary/employment wording and no “up to” wording in the approved reward table.

## Protected implementation areas
- Public Ambassador Programme presentation.
- Ambassador Dashboard Performance & Rewards presentation.
- Direct-referral attribution and 15% commission logic.

- Existing historical/internal compatibility field names may remain where changing them could break records; they must not reintroduce old rank wording to users.
