Funda Online Academy Departments

These departments define who is responsible for different areas of the student journey. This makes it easier for students to know where to send their queries and allows staff access to be separated properly later.

👑

Management

The main oversight department for the entire academy.

  • Audit all departments.
  • Review escalated matters.
  • Monitor academy operations.
  • Review important decisions.

Communication Department

Communication is separate from the operational departments. Its purpose is to keep students informed about important academy-wide information, reminders, notices and online-safety matters.

📢 General Announcements

Future communication tools can send announcements to all students or selected groups.

🛡️ Phishing & Safety Alerts

Send warnings about fake emails, fake payment requests, suspicious links and other online threats.

📅 Important Reminders

Communicate registration deadlines, assessment reminders, maintenance and other important dates.

👥 Student Community

Share information that applies broadly to students without interfering with Finance, Assessment or other departments.

Future Department Security

The department tabs currently organise the dashboard. They do not create staff permissions yet. When we implement department staff accounts, the actual permissions will be enforced through Supabase authentication and Row Level Security.

Planned hierarchy:

Management → full oversight
Admin → administration
Finance → financial records
