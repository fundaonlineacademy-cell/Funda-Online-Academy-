RESPONSIBLE PROMOTION

What every partner agrees to protect.

Use accurate course facts.
Fees, duration and course claims must match current Academy information.
Disclose the partnership.
Sponsored, paid, gifted or commission-based content must be clearly disclosed to the audience where applicable.
No misleading guarantees.
Partners may not promise jobs, salaries, accreditation status, guaranteed outcomes or benefits the Academy has not approved.
Respect learner privacy.
Creator partnerships never provide access to private student accounts, payment records, ID numbers or assessment records.
